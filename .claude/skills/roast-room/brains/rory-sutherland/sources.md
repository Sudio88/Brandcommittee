# Sources: Rory Sutherland

55 sources in three tiers. Read the Access column before leaning on anything.

- **Full text (3):** the three TED talks (S001 to S003), about 8,330 words in total. Text came from the TED transcript dataset mirrored on GitHub (S038), because ted.com, spectator.co.uk, podcast sites and YouTube were blocked from this build environment. **Every verbatim quote in this brain comes from S001, S002 or S003.**
- **Search excerpt (15):** pages found through web search. The search results showed short excerpts, but the full page was never fetched. These pages support paraphrased claims only, never quotes.
- **Index (18):** pages confirmed to exist (title, URL, sometimes the date) with no usable excerpt. They are listed so a later build can fetch them. They support no claims in this brain.
- **Literature (17):** the studies whose findings he borrows. They were not fetched here. They are cited so effect sizes can be traced back to the original paper rather than to his retelling. Details are from the published record; check page numbers before citing them outside the council.
- **Book (1) and dataset (1):** Alchemy (S037) and the TED dataset mirror (S038).

Gaps: there are no Spectator column bodies, no podcast transcripts and no Alchemy text. Positions on discounting, guarantees and packaging are therefore built from the TED reasoning plus the seat doctrine, and positions.md labels each one that way. A rebuild with network access should fetch S004 to S036 first.

Dates: TED dates are film dates from the dataset. Spectator dates are given only where search showed them.
