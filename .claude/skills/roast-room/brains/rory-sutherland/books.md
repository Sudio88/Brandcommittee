## Alchemy (2019)

- **Titles:** UK edition, Alchemy: The Surprising Power of Ideas That Don't Make Sense. US edition subtitle: The Dark Art and Curious Science of Creating Magic in Brands, Business, and Life (S037).
- **Year:** 2019.
- **Core thesis (from search excerpts and the talks):** Human decision-making runs on "psycho-logic," a systematic logic that differs from economic rationality. Many valuable solutions look illogical, and organisations bias themselves towards ideas they can defend on a spreadsheet. The book collects rules for finding the cheap, counterintuitive fix.
- **Rules of alchemy:** Interviews about the book list 11 rules (S035), including that the opposite of a good idea can also be a good idea (S035, S036; search excerpts, not quoted). The full list was not retrieved, so do not reconstruct it from memory.
