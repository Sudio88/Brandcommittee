# Books: Jay Conrad Levinson

Built from book excerpts, tables of contents, publisher copy and his articles. Only short excerpts were available; no book was read in full. He wrote or co-wrote dozens of Guerrilla titles; only those used by this brain are listed.

## Guerrilla Marketing (1984; revised 1993, 1998; 4th edition 2007)

- **Year:** 1984 for the first book, subtitled Secrets for Making Big Profits from Your Small Business (S009, S017). Google Books and one essay date the first printing to 1983 (S042); this brain uses 1984. 4th edition 2007, with Jeannie and Amy Levinson (S004, S043).
- **Core thesis:** Small firms can beat big budgets by investing time, energy and imagination, judging by profit, and treating marketing as an unending process built on the subsequent sale.
- **Key frameworks:** substitution test; geometric growth; the 16 monumental secrets (chapter 3 of the 4th edition, S043); weapons by media type (minimedia, maximedia, e-media, info-media, human-media, nonmedia, S043); combinations; follow-up. See frameworks.md #1 to #9, #11, #13, #14.
- **Taught in:** S001, S003, S004, S005, S008, S033, S034, S043; summaries S046 to S049.
- **Edition note:** a reader notes the 1993 edition leans toward acquiring customers more than keeping them (S047). Cite the 4th edition for retention doctrine.

## Guerrilla Marketing Attack (1989)

- **Year:** February 1989 (S042).
- **Core thesis:** The weapons approach extended to direct marketing and customer relations for small and medium businesses.
- **Key frameworks:** chapter "One Hundred Weapons for the Attack" and "Thirtythree Marketing Myths" (S042). See frameworks.md #6.

## Guerrilla Marketing with Technology (c. 1997)

- **Core thesis:** The same doctrine with technology as a weapon; publisher copy describes the series as "substituting time, energy, and imagination for money" (S040). Source of the armament secret's emphasis. See principles.md #16.

## Guerrilla Marketing for Free (c. 2003; year from catalog memory, not retrieved)

- **Core thesis:** A catalog of no-cost and low-cost weapons; his spa article draws 20 keys from "a full 120 described in my book" (S006).

## Guerrilla Marketing for Nonprofits (c. 2010)

- **Core thesis:** Adds information to the substitutes: "time, energy, imagination, and information" (S041).

## Guerrilla Marketing and Joint Ventures (c. 2015, with Sohail Khan)

- **Core thesis:** Partnering as "the most powerful weapon in the guerrilla marketing arsenal" (S039). See frameworks.md #10.

## Guerrilla Marketing Remix and "Mindset" material

## Not his: Marketing Warfare (Al Ries and Jack Trout, 1986)

- Positional guerrilla strategy for the smallest firm: pick a defensible segment, do not act like the leader, be ready to move (S022, S023, S024). Listed only to keep it apart from Levinson. Never cite it as his.
