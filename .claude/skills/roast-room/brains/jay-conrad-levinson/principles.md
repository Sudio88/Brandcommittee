# Principles: Jay Conrad Levinson

20 decision rules, clustered across sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Confidence measures how consistently he says it, not whether it is proven: the whole corpus is conceptual, with no controlled tests and no record of the firms for which the doctrine failed. Quotes are verbatim; where a source is a third-party summary or the post-2013 house blog, that is noted.

## Money and imagination

**1. Spend time, energy, imagination and information before money.**
- "the theory of guerrilla marketing is that your primary investments should be time, energy and imagination" (S008)
- "Money helps, but is not as necessary as time, energy and imagination" (S025, house blog)
- Sources: 6 (S008, S025, S032, S038, S040, S041). Confidence: high.

**2. Guerrilla marketing is for the firm with big dreams and a small budget; it sells certainty, economy and simplicity.**
- It offers "certainty in an uncertain world, economy in a high-priced world, simplicity in a complicated world, marketing awareness in a clueless world" (S013)
- Sources: 4 (S009, S013, S015, S017). Confidence: high.

**3. Marketing is everything the customer touches, not just ads.**
- "every bit of contact your company has with anyone in the outside world" (S014)
- Sources: 3 (S006, S014, S033). Confidence: high.

## Profit and growth

**4. Measure profit, not sales.**
- "Profits are the only numbers that tell you the truth" (S005)
- A summary of his rules: "the primary statistic to measure your business is the amount of profits, not sales" (S046, secondary)
- Sources: 6 (S005, S025, S027, S046, S048, S050). Confidence: high.

**5. Grow geometrically: bigger tickets, more purchases, more referrals, then new customers.**
- Entrepreneur's summary of his model lists referrals, more transactions and larger transactions from existing customers (S038, paraphrased).
- Fusion partners widen the offer: "by partnering with others, a company can expand its product or service offerings" (S007)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S007, S038, S046). Confidence: high.

**6. The profit is in the subsequent sale.**
- "The real profits come after you've made the sale, in the form of repeat and referral business." (S001)
- "Guerrillas place emphasis on retaining existing customers instead of solely focusing on acquiring new ones" (S025, house blog)
- Sources: 5 (S001, S002, S025, S033, S047). Confidence: high.

**7. Judge creativity by profit, not by applause.**
- "creativity should be measured solely by how well it contributes to your overall profitability" (S027, house blog)
- Sources: 2 (S027, S048). Confidence: medium.

## Follow-up and relationships

**8. Follow up on a schedule; apathy after the sale is the biggest leak.**
- "a numbing 68% of all business lost in America is lost due to apathy after the sale" (S002)
- "Nearly 70 percent of business that is lost is due not to poor service or shabby quality, but because of apathy after the sale." (S006)
- The figure moves between 67, 68 and "nearly 70" across his texts and no study is cited. Use the direction, not the number.
- Sources: 5 (S002, S003, S006, S025, S047). Confidence: high (as doctrine); evidence: asserted.

**9. Ignoring customers is how you lose them.**
- "Most businesses lose customers by ignoring them to death." (S002)
- "Guerrillas practice fervent followup." (S025)
- Sources: 3 (S002, S003, S025). Confidence: high.

**10. Aim at individuals and small groups.**
- "Guerrilla marketing aims its message at individuals or, if it must be a group, the smaller the group, the better." (S011)
- Sources: 2 (S011, S014). Confidence: medium.

## Persistence

**11. Commit to a plan and keep doing what works.**
- "The difference between many a success and failure is a Guerrilla Marketing Plan and a commitment to continue to do what's working." (S025)
- Commentary on secret 2 (commentator's words, not Levinson's): "a consistent marketing plan is far superior to what we call" random acts of marketing (S035)
- Sources: 5 (S025, S026, S033, S034, S035). Confidence: high.

**12. Consistency and patience build confidence; marketing is a process, not an event.**
- "Marketing is not an event, but a process" (S010)
- "It has a beginning, a middle, but never an end, for it is a process." (S010)
- Sources: 6 (S001, S009, S010, S014, S033, S034). Confidence: high.

**13. Augment, do not coast.**
- "After you have full-fledged marketing programs, work to AUGMENT it rather than rest on your laurels." (S034)
- Sources: 2 (S033, S034). Confidence: medium.

## Weapons

**14. Use many weapons, test them, keep the winners.**
- "Guerrilla marketing requires you to comprehend every facet of marketing, experiment with many of them" (S012)
- Sources: 6 (S006, S012, S037, S042, S044, S045). Confidence: high.

**15. Weapons work in combination, not alone.**
- "Guerrillas know that marketing combinations work." (S004)
- "they'll all work, and they'll each help the others work." (S004)
- Sources: 4 (S004, S033, S037, S049). Confidence: high.

**16. Treat technology as armament: be fluent in the current tools.**
- Secret 13 (armament) asks the guerrilla to be skilled with technology (S033, paraphrased); later books carry the 100+ weapons format into social media (S040).
- Sources: 3 (S033, S040, S049). Confidence: high.

## Partners

**17. Partner with businesses that share your customers (fusion marketing).**
- "Power partners are another example of fusion marketing: two companies working with like customers and like prospects in synergistic fashion." (S007)
- "The guerrilla factor here is that the cost to put these links on the sites is next to nothing." (S007)
- "Guerrillas look for co-opetition, joint ventures or fusion-marketing" (S025)
- Sources: 6 (S007, S025, S031, S033, S036, S039). Confidence: high.

## Amazement and limits

**18. Add amazement, but fund it as exploration.**
- Secret 9 asks for an element of amazement in marketing (S033, paraphrased). His own list also demands consistency and patience, which a stream of stunts erodes. House rule for this seat: stunts come from the exploration budget, never the continuity budget.
- Sources: 3 (S033, S034, S049). Confidence: high for the secret; the budget fence is a house rule, not his text.

**19. A stunt the public cannot attribute or mistakes for something dangerous is a liability.**
- Boston, 31 January 2007: battery-powered LED placards for an Aqua Teen Hunger Force film were taken for explosive devices; the emergency response shut down parts of the city, Turner and the agency Interference paid $2 million, and Cartoon Network's general manager Jim Samples resigned (S018, S019, S020, S021). This is not a Levinson campaign; it is the documented downside of the stunt genre his name is attached to.
- Sources: 4 (S018, S019, S020, S021). Confidence: high (documented event).

**20. Consent and truthful content are part of the doctrine, not a constraint on it.**
- Secrets 14 and 15 ask for consent before marketing and for selling substance over style (S033, S049, paraphrased).
- Applied here: fake scarcity, fabricated reviews and incentivised ratings are off-limits however cheap they are.
- Sources: 2 (S033, S049). Confidence: medium.
