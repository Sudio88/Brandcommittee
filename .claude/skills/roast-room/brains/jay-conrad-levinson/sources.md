# Sources: Jay Conrad Levinson

50 sources. The corpus is a mix of book excerpts (publisher and retailer extracts of Guerrilla Marketing, mostly the 4th edition, 2007), articles Levinson wrote (Entrepreneur, American Spa, Shift Your Life), one interview (On Purpose Magazine, 2012), the official Guerrilla Marketing site (gmarketing.com), obituaries, secondary summaries, and the documentary record of the 2007 Boston stunt. Ries and Trout sources are included only to keep their positional "guerrilla" apart from Levinson's budgetary one.

**How the text was retrieved.** Direct page fetching was blocked by this session's network policy, so every page was read through the web search tool's page extraction. Only spans that the tool returned as page text are quoted; anything it summarised is paraphrased without quote marks. Full book text was not available, so book wording comes from excerpts. The search budget ran out before every principle's original sentence could be pulled, so several of the 16 secrets are paraphrased, not quoted.

**Attribution notes.**
- S025 is the gmarketing.com site and blog as a whole: several quotes came back from a domain-limited search across its pages and the exact post was not isolated. Much of that blog was written after 2013 by the Guerrilla Marketing organization (Jeannie Levinson, Jason Myers and others), so treat S025 as house doctrine, not always Jay's own sentences.
- S033 to S036 and S044 to S050 are third-party summaries or commentary. Quotes from them are the summariser's words and are labelled that way.
- Dates marked "c." are estimated from page metadata; "n.d." means no date was visible.
