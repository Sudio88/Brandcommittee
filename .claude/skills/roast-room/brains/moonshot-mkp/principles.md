# Principles: Head of Marketplace (mkp)

23 decision rules. Confidence: **high** = Moonshot measured or decided in 2+ vault notes; **medium** = one vault note or strong external; **low** = practitioner or inference. Vault beats external; newest dated note beats older.

## Fees and take

**1. Model on measured take, never on published fee stacks.** Shopee 33% and TikTok 26.3% are measured from Jan-Jul statements; published 25.7% / 21.9% understate by ~7 and ~4 points (V001, V008). Any model still on 25.7% is ~7 points optimistic on every Shopee SKU (V004 inference). High.

**2. Say which take you used.** 33% / 26.3% is measured; 35% / 28% is the planning convention (V029, V040). The measured figure predates the 4 Aug 2026 Shopee round (+~2.14pp, S006), so 35% is the safer Shopee planning number until Aug-Sep statements are re-measured. Medium.

**3. A fee checked once and reused has already moved.** Thai platforms changed fees four times in 2026; re-pull Seller Centre before modelling and tag anything older than 60 days "re-verify in Seller Centre" (V001, V007). High.

**4. Net-to-bank is not a fee rate.** Settlement of 64-69% already nets seller promos and ads (V001). High.

**5. Fees are charged on the post-discount price, but fixed per-order costs are not.** ฿1.07/order, pick/pack and GWP do not shrink with price, so low-AOV promos lose margin faster than the headline (S004, S014; V035 Strip 7 AOV "~200 นิด ๆ"). Medium.

**6. Subsidised campaigns raise TikTok's fee base.** Since 21 Mar 2026 platform-funded buyer subsidies count in the transaction-fee base (S020, re-verify in Seller Centre). Medium.

## Programmes, tiers and packages

**7. Programme fees are purchases of traffic.** Prime/Xtra, ร้านโค้ดคุ้ม, Lazada Premium and campaign packages must show incremental contribution, not GMV (V045, V035). High.

**8. Keep Prime/Xtra on Linee through Q4; test exit on Non-mall first.** An early-year exit saw "เซลล์ฮวบ" and the shop re-joined around February; exit est. −20-30% sales vs a 15% fee saving (V045, V008). High.

**9. Every package is a test with a loss cap.** 12.12 Platinum (~฿300k) declined on a possible ฿100-200k loss; FMCG & Beauty Platinum joined at an expected ฿500k over 3 days (V035, V010). High.

**10. Mall must earn its premium.** ~3.21pp higher top commission on Shopee and 12 extra days of fund hold; it pays only if conversion uplift beats Δfee ÷ margin (S006, V004 §D2). Low (no Mall conversion study found).

## Eligibility and shop health

**11. Stay eligible for the next campaign first.** "Mega-day ops failure doesn't cost this campaign, it disqualifies us from the next one" (V002). High.

**12. The registration deadline is the last date, not the target.** Submit 2-4 weeks out; flash nominations for the top 6 value hours (V002, V032). High.

**13. The courier, not the stockout, is the named failure.** *"แพ็กเสร็จแล้ว แต่ไม่มีรถมารับของ"*: packing ends an hour before last pickup (V002). Medium.

**14. Stock is deducted at approval.** Scope campaign SKUs out of OMS sync before nomination; an attempted sync can delete Shopee variants (V002). High.

**15. System stock is not stock.** Pick only per system or label (*"ห้ามหยิบเพิ่มนอกระบบตามโปรปากเปล่า"*); count physically before nominating (V031, V008). High.

**16. Once approved, only add stock or lower price.** Nominated vouchers cannot be edited; ISP changes need ~14 days and no promos during the change (V004 §B1, V002, V045). High.

## Listings, data and ads

**17. Fix the listing before buying traffic.** Visitors with ฿0 sales: fix or delist; visitors up and conversion down: offer or page failure (V014). High.

**18. One GMV basis per readout, confirmed preferred.** Three 10.10 totals (฿635.8k / ฿581k / ฿531k) cannot all be "the number" (V016). High.

**19. Tag before the event, not after.** 0 of 9 SKUs had the Shopee Ads tag on 10.10, so the post-mortem took 30 exports (V015). Medium.

**20. Marginal ROAS above BEROAS, not average ROAS above 4.** Moonshot's ad cut lost ~฿20k+ profit at a 2.3 marginal return vs ~1.7-1.9 BEROAS (V014). High.

**21. Leave GMV Max alone once set.** Hold each ROI setting ≥ 3 days, last edit D-3; "TikTok pays us to set ROI at or below recommended and then leave it alone" (V002, V005). Medium.

## Accounts, licence and law

**22. No platform move that breaks the licence or the label.** Monchhichi is Thailand only, marketplaces may sit outside Schedule C; exact peroxide % printed; no treat/cure claims; no superlatives on TikTok (V019, V021, V024, V005). High.

**23. Do not scale accounts on an unknown rule.** Multi-account rules are a GAP on all three platforms; read the TikTok AHR status and appeal path before adding satellites (S058, S060; research GAP). Medium.
