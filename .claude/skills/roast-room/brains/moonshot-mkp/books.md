# Books: Head of Marketplace (mkp)

Reading list for this pillar. Grades: **PRIMARY-PLATFORM** = official platform documentation (seen via search summary in this build; read the live page before quoting); **SECONDARY** = surfaced and summarised in this build's research; **CANON** = standard reference named from the seat's own knowledge, not fetched in this build, so cite chapter and page before quoting it. Vault notes come first (V001, V002, V005, V045); these come after.

## Platform documentation (read before every campaign)
- **Shopee Seller Education Hub TH**, Shopee: penalty point policy (นโยบายคะแนนความประพฤติ, article 7052), penalty-criteria change (article 22251), Penalty Point Course PDF. The source of truth for NFR, LSR, points and tiers. PRIMARY-PLATFORM (S050, S051, S002).
- **TikTok Shop Seller University TH**, TikTok: Transaction Fee, Commerce Growth Fee, Mall and Non-Mall commission tables, Account Health and Violation Points, Seller Flash Sale, Mall Program T&C. PRIMARY-PLATFORM (S020, S022, S025, S026, S058, S059, S086, S044).
- **SEA TikTok Shop Ads Guidebook**, TikTok (seller-th). GMV Max and affiliate creative authorisation. PRIMARY-PLATFORM (S088).
- **TikTok Ads Help: GMV Max / Product Shopping Ads FAQ**, TikTok. GMV Max as the only Sales format from July 2025. PRIMARY-PLATFORM (S087).
- **Shopee Business Insights and Shopee Ads placed vs confirmed** (SG/MY pages). Metric definitions; check the TH tooltip. PRIMARY-PLATFORM, non-TH (S081, S082).
- **Lazada Seller Center University TH.** Not reached in this build: GAP. Fee figures came only from trackers (S031, S034, S036).

## Fee and take-rate tracking
- **Cube Asia Shopee take-rate tracker**, Cube Asia. Independent take-rate trend; compare with Moonshot's measured 33%. SECONDARY (S018, not read in full).
- **Khummai fee pages** (Shopee, TikTok, Lazada, fee-updates). Fastest Thai tracker of rate changes; use to know what to re-verify, never as the rate. PRACTITIONER (S004, S009, S028, S031, S034).
- **Momentum Works, Ecommerce in Southeast Asia (annual)**. Thai GMV and platform shares (US$35.5B 2025 as reported). SECONDARY via press (S090).

## Regulation for listings
- **ASEAN Cosmetic Directive, Annex III** (release 5 Jun 2023). H2O2 0.1% in oral products sold to consumers; >0.1-6% via dental practitioners only. PRIMARY (S075; S074 summary).
- **Thai Cosmetic Act B.E. 2558** and the Thai FDA notification rules. Notification before production or import, 3-year validity; claims rules for listings and live. CANON (not fetched); notification facts via S073.

## Platform economics and operations canon
- **Platform Revolution**, Parker, Van Alstyne & Choudary, W. W. Norton, 2016. Why platforms raise take rates once sellers are locked in; frames Shopee at 83% concentration. CANON.
- **The Business of Platforms**, Cusumano, Gawer & Yoffie, Harper Business, 2019. Platform power over complementors; context for the Thai Trade Competition fine (S057). CANON.
- **Working Backwards**, Bryar & Carr, St. Martin's Press, 2021. Input metrics and the weekly business review; the model for a shop-health and listing input dashboard. CANON.
- **The Goal**, Eliyahu Goldratt, North River Press, 1984. Find the constraint (courier, stock count, registration queue) before adding volume. CANON.
- **Inventory and Production Management in Supply Chains**, Silver, Pyke & Thomas, CRC Press, 4th ed. 2016. Safety stock under variable demand and lead time; the rigorous version of the vault's one-line formula (F10). CANON.

## Measurement
- **eightx: ROAS vs contribution margin**. BEROAS = 1 / CM, and why CM must be before ads. PRACTITIONER (S084).
