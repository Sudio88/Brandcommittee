# Stories: Head of Marketplace (mkp)

Institutional memory: dated episodes from the vault that bear on marketplace operations. Figures as the vault states them; conflicts noted. Newest context first within each group.

## Fees and programmes

1. **The take that kept rising (Jan-Jul 2026, note dated 2026-09-19).** Shopee's measured take climbed every month of 2026, from ~21% to 33%, while the published beauty stack said 25.7%. TikTok measured 26.3% vs 21.9% published, on 15,766 Linee orders plus the MSL export. Then Shopee raised commission again on 4 Aug. Lesson: model on statements, not rate cards, and re-measure after every round. (V001; S006)

2. **Prime exit, early 2026, re-joined around February (told on 2026-08-27).** The team tried leaving Prime; sales "เซลล์ฮวบ" and the shop re-joined. The August review priced Prime at ~฿400k in July (~11% of net sales) against an est. −20-30% sales on exit: save ~฿1M, lose ~฿2M. Decision: keep Prime for Linee through Q4, test on the Non-mall shop, decide Q1/April. Lesson: a shop-level switch cannot be A/B tested per SKU; test it on a second shop. (V045, V008)

3. **ISP rise after 9.9 and the two-week wait (2026-08-27 → 2026-10-05).** ISP was raised by SKU (+10% under ฿500 AOV, +5% above, ~3% under ฿200), needing a letter ~14 days ahead and no promos during the change. By October a card/ISP change had delayed campaign joining with a repeated "two-week wait". Lesson: price changes and campaign registration share one calendar. (V045, V029)

## Campaigns and packages

4. **12.12 package declined at ~฿300k (2026-09-28).** Shopee offered 12.12 Platinum at ~฿300k; the team saw a possible ฿100-200k loss and said no, while joining FMCG & Beauty on Platinum (expected ฿500k over 3 days; 5 slots ~+฿50k). Diamond's ฿720k needed a ฿150k baseline × 6 against a historic ~4×; ฿700k needed ~42,000 visitors, nearly double the record. Lesson: convert any package to orders to break even (F4); this is the reference loss cap. (V035, V010)

5. **9.9: zero flash slots (Sept 2026, note dated 2026-09-19).** No flash slots, "we were not in the queue", while the mechanic itself converted 147 impressions at 136 orders per 1,000. Lesson: the registration deadline is the last date, not the target. (V002)

6. **Linee 10.10 vs 9.9 vs 7.7 (readouts 2026-10-16).** Portfolio 10.10 ฿581k vs ฿664k on 9.9 on 20% less spend; Linee −25% vs 9.9 and −50% vs 7.7. New buyers 1,817 → 994 → 787, returning flat ~170-180. Strips: buy 7 get 7 did ฿450k on 7.7; mostly 7 get 1 did ฿179k on 10.10. 14+7 and 7+7 were live-only, so the strong offer barely reached shelf traffic. Lesson: the level problem is new-buyer acquisition and offer reach, not campaign eligibility. (V017, V016)

7. **Three numbers for one day (2026-10-16).** 10.10 was reported as ฿635.8k (MKP recap, probably placed), ฿581k (decks) and ฿531k (Shopee review, confirmed). 0 of 9 SKUs carried the Shopee Ads tag, so the post-mortem took 30 exports, and the ~31.8% platform rebate (฿177k in a day) sat in no channel P&L. Lesson: pick the basis and tag SKUs before the event. (V016, V015)

8. **10.10 cancellations doubled (2026-10-16).** Linee cancels went 7.6% → 15.6%; 26 of 117 came in the 00:00 hour. The 02:00-11:00 block lost ฿86.9k with no owner. Lesson: cancellations and dead hours are shop-health and staffing items, not marketing items. (V015)

9. **September target 16M → 12M → 11M (2026-09-17 / 09-25).** Cut four times; landed ~9.5M (09-17) or "89% of target" (09-25), unreconciled, with Shopee at 83% of sales. New rules: lock Q4 targets, ±20% accuracy, alert after 2 days below run-rate. Lesson: marketplace plans built on a moving target cannot be judged. (V041, V037, V009)

## Accounts and platforms

10. **TikTok main account lost (Sept-Oct 2026).** The main account became unavailable; marketing accounts were verified one phone number at a time in the company name (1 by 10-01); by 10-08 one opened, one assigned to Tammy, one waiting on a SIM, a 14-day no-live window on the new account, and possible rebate loss under investigation. Dual-SIM phones (~฿1,000) chosen over an agency number service. Lesson: the primary channel in May's notes became the recovery project in October; check enforcement history before scaling satellites. (V037, V032, V027, V021)

11. **Dual live ruled out the same day it was planned (2026-09-17).** The planning meeting set a Monchhichi dual live via a marketing account; a later meeting the same day found same-host dual-channel live counts as a "re-run": ban and −2,000 points against a 200-point start, Tier 6 (~฿10M/month) only. Lesson: read the platform rule before the action item. (V041, V043)

12. **300 Spiffy videos, almost no sales (2026-10-06).** ~300 TikTok videos from the KOL programme, 3-4 above 100 likes, no affiliate sales, ~฿700 ad spend. Then Spiffy TikTok follower gain fell from 1,243 (Sept) to 6 (Oct 1-13). Lesson for this seat: on a new or reset TikTok shop, content volume without paid push and sales velocity does not move the algorithm. (V028, V017)

13. **Shop-level GMV Max not adopted (2026-09-30).** Shop-level GMV Max would stop all product campaigns and, with Monchhichi inside the Linee shop, would sell the whole catalogue including GWP. Lesson: shop architecture decides which ad products are safe. (V033)

## Shop, stock and listings

14. **Lazada big-pack collapse (2026-09-25).** Big-pack sales fell from ~120,000 to ~4,000 a week with no change in ads or traffic; cause unknown. Separately, stopping Lazada in-app produced no sales, so a ~฿80-87/day minimum was restarted (10-06). Lesson: an unexplained collapse with flat inputs points at listing, price or eligibility; check those before media. (V037, V028)

15. **The 7,000-unit stock gap (2026-10-05).** One toothpaste showed ~10,000 in the system against ~3,600 physical (40 cartons × 72); Monchhichi White Mint ~17,500 vs ~16,000 reported, with ~1,900-2,000 shipped to Tops (outright sale). Last manual count end-August; Ken audits; BOGO/bundle deduction suspected. Lesson: nominate from physical stock only. (V031, V008)

16. **Loom: 786 visitors, ฿0 (2026-10-16).** Two weeks running, 786 Shopee visitors and no sales. Verdict: "Fix it or delist it." Linee KIT the same week: visitors +60%, buyers 150 → 58. Lesson: traffic to a broken listing is waste. (V014)

17. **Shopee Video, a free lift (2026-09-17).** Loading existing videos into Shopee Video converted where they had made zero; by 10-01 Moonshot uploads were restricted without a visible shop ban, to check via Bank and Ploy. Lesson: use every free platform surface, and treat unexplained restrictions as a shop-health signal. (V042, V043, V032)

## Cross-border

18. **Singapore dark ramp (2026-09-17).** Skipping the promo on 8-9 Sept collapsed 9.9 D-day to zero; moving it to "9.9 night → 10.10" recovered sales. Sept MTD SGD 1,320 on ~SGD 2,000 spend; Oct 9k / Dec 20k forecasts rejected; spend to be set from BEROAS or the budget moves to D2C. Lesson: do not go dark on double-digit ramps, and do not forecast a market with no fee stack. (V042)
