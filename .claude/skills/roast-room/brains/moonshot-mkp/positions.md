# Positions: Head of Marketplace (mkp)

The seat's stance on the decisions it gets asked again and again. Each: **default**, what **flips** it, and the **evidence**. Dates are vault note dates. Platform rules carry "re-verify in Seller Centre" where older than 60 days.

## Programmes and tiers

**1. Keep or exit Shopee Prime/Xtra (ร้านโค้ดคุ้ม).**
- Default: **keep for Linee through Q4** (V045, 2026-08-27; V008, 2026-10-16).
- Flips when: the Non-mall test shop, first built to "ร้านค้าแนะนำ" for a readable baseline, shows a sales loss below Fee saved ÷ margin (F5), or the Aug 2026 round has removed the PRIME commission discount (S009, re-verify in Seller Centre). Real decision Q1/April.
- Evidence: exit est. −20-30% sales; worked example save ~฿1M, lose ~฿2M → −฿140k before redeploy; early-year exit "เซลล์ฮวบ" (V045). Not on Loom (too small).

**2. Mall vs non-Mall.**
- Default: **no tier change on the hero shops in Q4.** For Monchhichi licensed goods, prefer the tier that signals authenticity, but only after written licensor approval of the marketplace channel (V019).
- Flips when: a measured conversion or campaign-slot uplift beats ~5.6% more orders (3.21pp ÷ 57% margin) and 12 extra fund-hold days are affordable (F6).
- Evidence: Shopee Mall 19.26% vs non-Mall 16.05% top (S006); Mall hold 15 days vs 3 (V004 §D2); no Mall conversion study (GAP).

**3. Join a campaign package.**
- Default: **join only base traffic campaigns and packages whose N (orders to break even) sits below a measured uplift; decline the rest** (V035, 2026-09-28).
- Flips when: Ploy shows incremental sales and incremental cost of sale excluding ads, with a loss cap and read date.
- Evidence: FMCG & Beauty Platinum joined (expected ฿500k / 3 days; 5 slots ~+฿50k); 12.12 Platinum (~฿300k) declined, possible ฿100-200k loss; N ≈ 2,199 orders on Linee CPO ฿136 (F4).

**4. TikTok Beauty Fest-type packages.**
- Default: **no** while the main account is down and rebate eligibility on new accounts is unknown (V027).
- Flips when: the shop has a content library and a GMV Max baseline; package claims (100% brand GMV uplift) are TikTok's own (V018).

## Price and promo (eligibility side)

**5. Deepen a discount for a campaign.**
- Default: **no deeper than the ceiling, and only if the new price will not undercut the next campaign's floor** (V001, V002). Promotion seat owns depth; this seat owns eligibility.
- Flips when: break-even uplift (F2) is below a measured uplift; for Linee strips, restore buy 7 get 7 before a deeper % (7.7 ฿450k vs 10.10 ฿179k, V017).

**6. Raise ISP.**
- Default: **yes, by SKU, as decided after 9.9**: +10% AOV < 500, +5% AOV > 500, ~3% under 200; letter ~14 days ahead; no promos during the change; voucher tier ~499 (V045).
- Flips when: the change window overlaps registration for 11.11/12.12 (card/ISP change already delayed campaign joining, V029).
- Evidence: Strip 7 +10% allows an 11.4% volume loss before it costs money (F2).

## Ads and traffic

**7. Shift budget between in-app, CPAS and D2C.**
- Default: **move only on marginal ROAS vs BEROAS and NMC**, per brand (V014, V037).
- Flips when: a channel's marginal return falls below ~1.9 (Linee Shopee) or attribution is suspect (Monchhichi CPAS claims ~70% of GMV, ROAS ~18.6: "Don't scale CPAS on that number", V014).
- Evidence: stopping Meta/awareness did not improve Shopee ROAS (~3.4 → ~3.0, V034); Linee CPAS 29.8k → 6.0k tracked the traffic loss (V017).

**8. Scale GMV Max.**
- Default: **product-level only; scale proven auto campaigns** (Linee Auto ROAS 7.04 on ฿7.1k never scaled, V015). Shop-level GMV Max not adopted (V033).
- Flips when: ROI holds within 80-120% of target with utilisation < 80% (V005).

**9. Lazada spend.**
- Default: **keep minimum in-app on (~฿80-87/day)**; stopping it produced no sales (V028).
- Flips when: the 9-11 Oct C-pass test shows CAC and new-vs-repeat per SKU worth more (V034). Cut CPX ads when sales do not pick up.

## Live and accounts

**10. Add live hours.**
- Default: **only with a GMV/h and contribution/h baseline** (GAP, V012). Concentrate on Linee + Monchhichi (V043, V041).
- Flips when: session exports show contribution/h positive after MC, ads and promo (F14); for agencies, GMV/h above ~฿304 on a ~฿50k / 300h fixed fee (V043).
- Evidence: Linee LIVE ฿317k → ฿221k with ads −9% ("content or slot quality, not budget", V014); Spiffy LIVE ฿9.1k → ฿19.4k with ฿0 ads.

**11. Open more TikTok marketing / satellite accounts.**

- Flips when: the main-account loss proves enforcement-driven (linked-account risk) or rebate eligibility is lost on sub-accounts (V027).
- Evidence: 1 verified by 10-01; one assigned to Tammy, one waiting on a SIM by 10-08 (V032, V027).

**12. Dual live / re-runs.**
- Default: **no** below Tier 6 (~฿10M/month); penalty ban and −2,000 points (V043).
