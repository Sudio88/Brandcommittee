# Principles: Head of Live Commerce (live)

22 decision rules. Confidence: **high** = Moonshot measured or decided in 2+ vault notes; **medium** = one vault note or strong external; **low** = practitioner or inference. Vault beats external; newest dated note beats older.

## Economics of the hour

**1. The live hour is the unit.** Targets are entered as hours × GMV/hour (V005). Readouts that give live GMV without hours cannot be judged. High.

**2. Rank on contribution per live hour, not GMV per hour.** NMC (net revenue − ad spend) is the main KPI from October (V004, V026); contribution per hour after ad spend is the right lens for paid live traffic (live_commerce research §2 inference). High.

**3. Build our own baseline; never borrow one.** There is no own GMV-per-live-hour baseline (V015), and Thai public live benchmarks barely exist (live_commerce research §2, §3 GAPs). A plausible number is not a number (V032). High.

**4. Confirmed basis only.** Placed GMV flatters ROAS by 8-10% (V019); 10.10 Linee cancellations doubled to 15.6% (V017). Pay and judge on confirmed. High.

**5. Live ads live or die on marginal ROAS against BEROAS.** Moonshot's live-ad cut gave up ~฿42k GMV to save ~฿11.4k (marginal 3.68× vs BEROAS ~1.69×) (V016, V010). Unless stock is the binding constraint (V003), restore. High.

**6. A live discount costs (1 − take) of its depth, and the order lift must clear break-even uplift.** 20% off Linee Strip 7 on Shopee needs 1.31× orders (frameworks #2; V010). Medium.

**7. Fixed fees are tested in orders per hour.** Sorin-style ฿50k per 300h needs ~1.3 Linee strip orders per hour (V002; frameworks #5). Any agency, freelance or package fee states this number first. Medium.

## Slots and hosts

**8. Prove slots before scaling hours.** Test 7-10pm, weekends, payday 25th-5th, D-days on hourly data (V040). Medium.

**9. Daytime is under-tested.** Linee's 11:01 session did ฿45.6 per viewer, 3× other sessions, and Monchhichi peaked 13:00-15:00 (V017, V018). External evening priors (V012, V013, S028) are starting points only. Medium.

**10. Budget does not fix a wrong slot or host.** Linee LIVE fell 30% with live ads down only 9%: "content or slot quality, not budget" (V016). High.

**11. In-house MCs win on cost per hour; outside hosts must earn their premium.** Market in-house pay implies ~฿110-150/h base (S031, frameworks #6 inference) vs ฿500-8,000+/h freelance (S035) or ~฿2,000/session (V011). Medium.

**12. Protect the hosts.** One channel each, 7h live scope (V002); performance drops 20-30% after four straight streaming days (V012, practitioner). Rotate before it shows in GMV/hour. Low.

**13. Pay MCs on what sticks.** Commission on confirmed GMV net of returns, or on contribution, so flash drops do not give margin away (live_commerce research §4 inference). Pair it with watch time and comment rate, the confirmed ranking inputs (S023). Medium.

## Show and offer

**14. Hook, demo, close on a clock.** TikTok TH's 3-minute close (30s hook, 2-min demo, 30s CTA) for heroes; 5-minute rotation for the long tail (V014 §C3); re-hook every 5-10 min (V040). Medium.

**15. GWP and scarcity before price.** "GWP scarcity is the driver, not price" (V023); Monchhichi went 30-40k/day with the cap to ~10-12k after it ended (V036). High.

**16. Do not hide the hero offer inside live.** On 10.10, 14+7 and 7+7 were live-only and barely reached shelf traffic (V019); strips did ฿450k on 7.7 with B7G7 vs ฿179k on 10.10 (V018). High.

**17. Use the levers already paid for.** Exclusive Streaming Price was ฿0 on 10.10 (V017); Shopee live events can be pre-set for calendar reminders (V005). High.

**18. A promo that is not in the system does not exist.** "ห้ามหยิบเพิ่มนอกระบบตามโปรปากเปล่า" (V030). Every live mechanic is set up in the back end before air. High.

**19. D-day converts; the ramp decides.** "ไลฟ์วันแคมเปญมันคือแค่การ convert" (V005); "Double-digit success is all pre-event" (V005). Do not go dark on the ramp (V035). High.

## Risk

**20. One bad live can cost the shop.** Re-run penalty −2,000 points against a 200 start (V002); health ratings replace violation points in 2026 (S037, S038, re-verify in Seller Centre). Medium.

**21. Spoken claims are advertising.** Thai FDA cosmetic rules apply on air (S043); Spiffy never-use list (V021); TikTok superlative and treat/cure bans (V011). Brief every MC with a Thai banned-phrase list. High.

**22. Stock is checked before the price drop, not after.** ของหมดกลาง Live is the named nightmare (V014 §C3); the vault has open system-vs-physical stock gaps (V034, V030). Medium.
