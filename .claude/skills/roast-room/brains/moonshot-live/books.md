## Live commerce strategy and evidence
- **"It's showtime! How live commerce is transforming the shopping experience".** McKinsey Digital, 2021. Why live converts; dated and China-led. SECONDARY (S027).
- **Momentum Works: TikTok Shop in the U.S. 2025 / H1 2025; SEA e-commerce 2025 report.** Momentum Works. Channel mix (LIVE vs video vs Shop tab), SEA scale. SECONDARY (S003, S007, S008).
- **Wang (2025), Douyin livestream fieldwork.** *Big Data & Society*. How beginner streams open with low-price items, coupons and samples. PRIMARY academic (S021).
- **Cube Asia: skincare strategy across Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop in Thailand; live-shopping penetration in SEA.** Cube. Off-store share of TikTok skincare sales. PRIMARY data via blog (S052); penetration piece not fetched (S058, GAP).
- **The 人货场 (people, goods, venue) live-commerce model.** Chinese practitioner doctrine on traffic, hero and profit SKUs and the 5-minute loop; no book read, known here only through the Bingo Digital summary (S020). PRACTITIONER.

## Economics and measurement
- **Marketing Metrics: The Manager's Guide to Measuring Marketing Performance.** Farris, Bendle, Pfeifer & Reibstein, Pearson. Net marketing contribution, break-even, contribution per unit. CANON.
- **Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments.** Kohavi, Tang & Xu, Cambridge UP, 2020. Holdouts for live ads and slot tests. CANON.
- **Recast: pull-forward promotions.** Recast. Why D-day live lifts can be borrowed. PRACTITIONER (S053).
- **Polar Analytics: contribution margin CM1/CM2/CM3.** Layer definitions for CPO and CLH. PRACTITIONER (S054).
