# Positions: Head of Live Commerce (live)

The seat's stance on the decisions it gets asked most. Each: **default**, **flips when**, **evidence**. Dates are vault note dates; newer vault wins.

## Hours and slots

**1. Add live hours.**
- Default: **no new hours until the hour is measured.** Move existing hours to better slots first; capacity is already 42 LH/day (V002).
- Flips when: a slot cell shows CLH > 0 after live ads on at least 4 comparable sessions and beats the median slot (frameworks #7).
- Evidence: no own GMV/live-hour baseline (V015, 2026-10-16); Linee LIVE −30% on ads −9% shows hours were not the constraint (V016, 2026-10-16).

**2. Extend D-day lives (Fern +1-2h after peak).**
- Default: **yes on D-day, with a readout.** Extensions go where the peak tail is, not into the dead block.
- Flips when: the extension's confirmed GMV/hour falls below break-even G for that brand (frameworks #3), or the MC is past four straight live days (V012).
- Evidence: Fern's lives extended 1-2h for 10.10 (V005, 2026-10-01); no readout of the extension exists (GAP). 02:00-11:00 lost ฿86.9k with no owner (V017).

**3. Run overnight or 24h live.**
- Default: **no as always-on; yes as a short D-day flash test.**
- Flips when: a midnight-to-morning flash test beats break-even G. Spiffy 9.9 was set up exactly this way, short and targeted, before any deeper discount (V007, 2026-08-28).
- Evidence: "some brands run 24h live" is an agency remark, not a benchmark (V002); 26 of 117 10.10 Linee cancels came in the 00:00 hour (V017).

**4. Which platform gets the hours now.**
- Default: **Shopee Live first.** Shopee is 83% of September sales (V025) and the main TikTok account is unavailable (V034).
- Flips when: the new TikTok account clears its 14-day no-live window (V006) and LIVE GMV Max has a 7-day learning run with a holdout (V011, V012).
- Evidence: external SEA live share (33% of TikTok Shop GMV, S005) is unverified and loses to our split; Thai video/live ~25% of e-commerce (V014 §D6, Priceza PRIMARY).

## Price and promo

**5. Deepen a live discount or add a live flash drop.**
- Default: **only inside the ceiling, with a stated break-even uplift.** GWP or bundle reveal before % off.
- Flips when: the measured order lift on comparable sessions beats CPO_full ÷ CPO_live (20% off Linee Strip 7 needs 1.31×, frameworks #2), and the drop does not write a floor below the next double-day price (V012).
- Evidence: ceilings (V010, 2026-09-19); "GWP scarcity is the driver, not price" (V023); live promo set signed off once and reused (V004, 2026-09-25).

**6. Turn on Exclusive Streaming Price for 11.11.**
- Default: **yes.** It was ฿0 on 10.10, an unused lever (V017).
- Flips when: the ESP price breaks the ceiling or a TikTok/Shopee price-baseline rule (re-verify in Seller Centre).
- Evidence: 11.11 plan: ESP on, 3-4 prime sessions per brand, Moonshot live ads restored, second push 18:00-23:00 (V017, V018, 2026-10-16).

**7. Keep the strongest offer live-only.**
- Default: **no for the hero offer; yes for a small urgency drop.**
- Flips when: live reach on that brand matches shelf traffic.
- Evidence: 14+7 and 7+7 were live-only on 10.10 and barely reached shelf (V019); strips ฿450k on 7.7 (B7G7) vs ฿179k on 10.10 (V018).

**8. Join a campaign package that bundles live slots or support.**
- Default: **no unless priced as a test** with orders to break even, loss cap, read date and owner.
- Flips when: required orders per live hour ≤ best measured, and the loss fits the reference cap.
- Evidence: 12.12 Platinum ~฿300k declined, possible ฿100-200k loss (V009, V023); at Linee full-price CPO ฿170 that is ~1,760 incremental orders (frameworks #5).

## Hosts and pay

**9. Raise MC commission.**
- Default: **restructure before raising.** Pay on confirmed GMV net of returns or on contribution, plus watch-time and comment-rate KPIs (live_commerce research §4; S023).
- Flips when: a slot proves CLH headroom and retention risk is real (MCs asked to take content duties, V002).
- Evidence: Thai MC commission norms are a GAP; MC-run channel 50/50 split is a proposal, not a decision (V002).

**10. Hire the November live agency.**
- Default: **only with a priced proposal that states orders per hour.** No proposal, no owner yet (V006, 2026-10-08).
- Flips when: fixed fee per hour ≤ the Sorin quote (~฿167/h + 1-2%, V002) or a pure-commission deal ≤ 16% where our in-house CLH is negative.
- Evidence: crossover maths: Sorin fixed beats 16% commission above G ≈ ฿1,152/h (frameworks #6). Role-definition reference ฿1,900-2,500/LH (V040) has no dated source; if an agency quotes it, break-even G on Linee Shopee at 20% off is ~฿3,500-4,600/h.

**11. Use affiliate or KOL live hosts.**
- Default: **yes on commission; no on fixed slot fees without an orders test.**
- Flips when: a fixed slot (micro-KOL TikTok live ~฿16-40k, V014 §C4 ONE) clears orders to break even on history.
- Evidence: Monchhichi TikTok commission 3% vs Shopee 15% (V033), below the ~15% visibility floor for creators (vault GMV Max note, V011); big-KOL rule: allowed if it aims for break-even (V037). Flag to KOL seat.

**12. Simulcast one MC on two channels, or run MC marketing channels.**
- Default: **never simultaneous below Tier 6.** Separate MCs per channel; MC-run satellite clip channels are fine (no live overlap).
- Flips when: shop sales reach Tier 6 (~฿10M/month) and TikTok approves for that month only (V002).
- Evidence: re-run penalty: ban and −2,000 points vs 200 start (V002, 2026-09-17); the 09-17 dual-live action was superseded the same day (V003 vs V002).

## Budget and brands

**13. Restore Moonshot live ads.**
- Default: **yes**, at the September level (~฿12.2k/week) with a marginal-ROAS readout.
- Flips when: Moonshot stock cover drops below the promo window (import stuck, ~2 weeks cover, V003); then hold ROAS and protect stock.
- Evidence: Moonshot LIVE ฿103k → ฿61k after the cut; marginal ROAS 3.68× vs BEROAS ~1.69× (V016, frameworks #4); 11.26 ROAS on 8.8 (V017).

**14. Shift budget into TikTok LIVE GMV Max.**
- Default: **not until the new account can go live**; then budget 2-3× historical LIVE spend, ROI at or below recommended, no edits for ~7 days (V011, V012).
- Flips when: the main account is recovered or the new account passes 14 days (V006).
- Evidence: a TikTok live ROAS breakdown was requested and is still open (V008, 2026-09-28); never quote GMV Max ROI as ROAS (V011); shop-level GMV Max not adopted because it sells GWP (V027).

**15. Put MC hours on Spiffy, Loom or Moonshot.**
- Default: **Linee + Monchhichi first** (Care's concentration test, V003). Spiffy: keep the ฿0-ads sessions running, do not add hours. Loom: **no hours until the listing is fixed** (786 visitors, ฿0 sales, V016; live conversion fell, V004).
- Flips when: Spiffy's live growth (฿9.1k → ฿19.4k on ฿0 ads, V016) holds for 4 weeks with positive CLH. **CONFLICT:** 09-17 said Spiffy live did not convert (V002); 10-16 shows growth (V016). Newer wins on direction; absolute size is still small.
- Evidence: Spiffy Shopee ceiling 48% (V010); Loom is quiet premium, no hype grammar on air (V038).

**16. Mall vs non-Mall for live.**
- Default: **not a live decision; live runs on whichever shop holds the hero.** Flag to MKP.
- Flips when: never on live grounds alone.
- Evidence: Prime kept for Linee through Q4, non-Mall exit test first (V034, V028); Mall settlement holds ~15 days vs 3, about two weeks of working capital (V014 §D2, re-verify in Seller Centre).

**17. Run lucky draws or prize games on air.**
- Default: **flavour-guessing for engagement, no prize draws** until TikTok TH and Shopee TH policy is checked.
- Flips when: written policy confirms the mechanic is allowed (MKP to verify).
- Evidence: flavour game used on 10.10 (V005); US TikTok prohibits gambling and game-like activities in LIVE (S041); TH GAP.
