# Stories: Head of Live Commerce (live)

Institutional memory: dated episodes from the vault that bear on live commerce. Figures as the vault states them; conflicts noted; lessons are this seat's reading.

## Live itself

1. **Moonshot cut its live ads and its live (week 7-14 Oct, read 2026-10-16).** Live ads went ฿12.2k → ฿0.8k; Moonshot LIVE fell ฿103k → ฿61k. September live ROAS had been 8.45, and 8.8 returned 11.26; on 10.10 live ads got ฿600 and zero attribution. Lesson: live traffic was paid-supported; marginal ROAS ~3.68× was far above BEROAS ~1.69×, so the cut cost ~฿13k contribution a week unless stock forced it. (V016, V017)

2. **Linee live fell without the budget moving (same week).** Linee LIVE ฿317k → ฿221k (−30%) with live ads −9%; Linee live 9.9 ฿208k → 10.10 ฿128k. The note's verdict: "content or slot quality, not budget." Lesson: diagnose slot, MC and content before buying traffic. (V016, V017)

3. **The 11:01 session (10.10 review).** One Linee session did ฿77k at ฿45.6 per viewer, 3× the other sessions, with peak concurrency of only 17. Lesson: daytime is under-tested, and a small room of buyers beats a big room of browsers. (V017)

4. **Spiffy: "did not convert", then the cheapest growth line (09-17 → 10-16).** On 09-17 Spiffy live did not convert and Care set a test to concentrate live on Linee + Monchhichi. By 7-14 Oct Spiffy LIVE rose ฿9.1k → ฿19.4k with ฿0 live ads: "Cheapest growth line in the portfolio." Lesson: newer data wins on direction, but ฿19k a week does not earn more MC hours yet. (V002, V003, V016)

5. **Monchhichi's live doubled on the fandom (10.10 readout).** Monchhichi Shopee LIVE ฿69.9k → ฿138.7k, peaking 13:00-15:00; 61% returning buyers, ~68% of 10.10 GMV on pre-order. Lesson: Monchhichi is an event and drop format; the afternoon slot is real for it. (V018)

6. **ESP at zero baht (10.10).** Exclusive Streaming Price was ฿0 on 10.10, an unused lever; the 11.11 plan turns it on with 3-4 prime sessions per brand and a second push 18:00-23:00. Lesson: check the paid-for levers before asking for more money. (V017, V018)

7. **The strong offer nobody on the shelf saw (10.10).** 14+7 and 7+7 were live-only, so the strong offer barely reached shelf traffic; strips did ฿450k on 7.7 with buy 7 get 7 vs ฿179k on 10.10. Lesson: live-only is for urgency drops, not for the hero offer. (V019, V018)

8. **Live was the only growing channel, then it wasn't (Sept).** 09-17: Live +9% MoM, the only growth. 09-25 close: KOL and Live performance fell; Live also absorbed promo weight on mid-month and payday, starving non-live days. Lesson: an MTD read is not a month; and live wins can be borrowed from the shelf. (V003, V004)

9. **The dual live that died the same day (2026-09-17).** The planning meeting set a Monchhichi dual live through a TikTok marketing account; the Content Ramp-up meeting the same day ruled it out: same-host dual-channel is a "re-run", ban and −2,000 points against a 200-point start, Tier 6 (~฿10M/month) only. Lesson: check the platform rule before the plan leaves the room. (V003, V002)

10. **The TikTok account gap (Sept to Oct).** Main TikTok account unavailable; marketing accounts verified one phone number at a time; a new account waits 14 days before going live. Lesson: Shopee Live carries the load until TikTok can go live again; plan traffic around the gap. (V034, V006)

11. **Fern's 10.10 extension and the targets that weren't filled (10-01 → 10-16).** 10-01 decided Fern's lives run 1-2h past peak and live targets go in as hours and GMV/hour; the 10-16 readouts found no per-brand, per-platform targets, so every "vs target" field was blank, and 02:00-11:00 lost ฿86.9k with no owner. Lesson: a decision without a logged hour and a target cannot be read. (V005, V017)

## Bearing on live from other pillars

12. **12.12 package declined at ฿300k (2026-09-28).** Possible ฿100-200k loss; the team joined FMCG & Beauty on Platinum instead. Lesson: any fixed fee, including a live agency, states orders to break even (~1,760 Linee orders at full-price CPO) and a loss cap. (V009, V023)

13. **September target 16M → 12M → 11M (09-17 / 09-25).** Cut four times; landed ~9.5M or "89% of target", unreconciled. Rule since: targets ±20%, alert after 2 days below run-rate, lock Q4. Lesson: live hours × GMV/hour must sit inside a locked target, entered before the campaign. (V003, V004, V025)

14. **Linee 10.10 vs 9.9 vs 7.7.** Linee −25% vs 9.9 and −50% vs 7.7; new buyers 1,817 → 994 → 787, returning flat ~170-180. Lesson: the level problem is new-buyer acquisition; a live session that only re-sells returning buyers does not fix it. (V018)

15. **300 Spiffy videos, almost no sales (2026-10-06).** ~300 TikTok videos, 3-4 above 100 likes, no affiliate sales, ~฿700 of ads: "videos that go viral generally had some paid ad input." Lesson: volume without traffic does not sell; the same holds for live hours without traffic. (V026)

16. **Lazada big-pack collapse (2026-09-25).** Big-pack sales fell ~120,000 → 4,000 a week with no change in ads or traffic; cause unknown. Lesson: when a line collapses with inputs flat, look at listing, price and stock before adding hours or ads. (V004)

17. **Monchhichi cap GWP (09-24 → 10-05).** 30-40k/day with the cap at peak, ~10-12k after it ended; buy 3 get 1 did not lift baskets. Lesson: scarcity GWP is the live reveal that works; price mechanics did not. (V036, V023)

18. **"Promo by mouth" banned at the warehouse (2026-10-05).** "ห้ามหยิบเพิ่มนอกระบบตามโปรปากเปล่า": the warehouse picks only what the system says. Lesson: an MC's on-air freebie that is not in the back end is a stock and service failure. (V030)
