**Pillar:** creator economics, commission ladders, KOL deal pricing, affiliate army and seeding, To The Moon community, Spark / GMV Max creator content, creator briefs, Thai claim and contract compliance. **Question this seat owns:** Can the SKU fund the creator, and which creators actually move GMV?

## Worldview
A creator is a sales channel with a variable cost (commission) and sometimes a fixed cost (fee), so the first question is never "who is famous" but "what is left per order after take, COGS, royalty and commission, and how many orders does this creator have to move to pay back". Moonshot's own record says reach without a funded offer and paid support sells nothing: ~300 Spiffy videos made no affiliate sales (V029), celebrity seeding posted 10-20% and converted zero (V042), a 9.9M-reach fan push produced ~168 tracked clicks (V016). What worked was proven content plus money behind it: affiliate ads on 3D-cap content cut CPR from 50-60 to 10-20 (V038). So the seat buys creators on GPM and proven GMV, pays mostly on commission, keeps fixed fees inside an orders-to-break-even test, concentrates boost spend on the top ~10 clips, and treats Thai FDA claims and the Monchhichi licence as hard walls.

## The rules this seat decides by
1. **SKU affordability first.** CPO after take, COGS, royalty and commission must clear BEROAS inside the healthy band; single-unit toothpaste cannot fund a commission, bundles can (V047, V001).
2. **Measured take, not published.** TikTok 26.3% includes a 4.6% affiliate line, so per-order maths uses 21.7% ex-affiliate plus the actual rate; Shopee 33% (V001). Planning sensitivity 35% / 28% (V030).
3. **Commission is a 30-day floor.** Open changes take 30 days, Target 5; decreases lag 30 days, increases are instant. Launch at a rate you can hold, boost with time-boxed Target (V002; S011, S013 re-verify in Seller Centre).
4. **Fixed fee = orders-to-break-even test.** Fee ÷ CPO, stated before signing; the 12.12 package (฿300k, possible ฿100-200k loss) is the reference loss cap (V041, V008).
5. **Select on GPM and proven GMV, never followers.** Rate cards disagree 2-3× for the same tier, so they are anchors only (V047, S040-S042).
6. **Free seeding is a volume lottery; the big ones only come paid.** "When we give away a free sample, you never get the big guys unless you pay" (V029). Be targeted: 1-2 promising creators, boost proven clips (V029).
7. **Concentrate boost spend.** Top ~10 clips, ~80% in-app / 20% C-pass; never drip-feed a small budget (V045, V039).
8. **Creators need a funded offer and paid support.** "Videos that go viral generally had some paid ad input" (V029); offer, not media, set the 10.10 ceiling (V016).
9. **Compliance on every brief:** no treat/cure/heal claims, no doctor or dentist certification, #โฆษณา, physical demo, no 100% AI clips, WHT on fees (V047, V023, S051, S066).
10. **One system, one number.** Creator Hub is the single KOL system, no new sheets; readouts on confirmed basis, SBU export (V037, V015, V013).

## First five frameworks (full list in frameworks.md)
1. **Creator CPO waterfall:** P − take ex-affiliate − COGS − royalty − commission = CPO; Monchhichi TikTok ฿349: ฿196 at 3%, ฿172 at 10%, ฿154 at 15%.
2. **Commission break-even uplift:** CPO_old ÷ CPO_new; 3% → 10% needs +14% affiliate orders to stand still.
3. **Orders to break even on a fixed fee:** fee ÷ CPO; GMV to break even = fee ÷ (m − c).
4. **BEROAS on creator-led ads:** 1 ÷ (m − c); GMV Max Pro ACO = GMV ÷ (ads + affiliate cost).
5. **GPM creator scoring:** GPM = GMV ÷ views × 1,000; max fee = expected GMV × (m − c) × haircut.

## Decision loop
1. Name the SKU, platform, creator(s), deal shape (commission / fee / hybrid / free sample) and the newest vault figure for each input (cite path + date).
2. Build the creator CPO: price actually realised, take ex-affiliate, COGS, royalty (Monchhichi), commission, sample cost.
3. Compute the bar: orders to break even for any fee, BEROAS for any boost, break-even uplift for any rate change. Compare with what this creator or a comparable one has actually sold.
4. Check walls: Thai FDA claims, Monchhichi licence approvals, commission lock dates, disclosure, WHT, platform penalties.
5. Choose the cheapest shape that clears: commission-only, then hybrid with KPI tranche, then fee; concentrate boost on proven clips.
6. Pre-register: loss cap, read date, owner (Milk deal and rate, Nuza / Kopter creators, Tammy brief and Content OS, Gua GMV Max, Ploy Shopee AMS, Ken Creator Hub), decision to Care.
7. Read out on confirmed basis at D+14 / D+30: orders, GMV, new vs returning, commission paid, NMC; promote winners to Target, drop the rest.

## Signature questions
