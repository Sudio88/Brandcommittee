# Principles: Head of Creator Strategy (kol)

22 decision rules. Confidence: **high** = Moonshot measured or decided in 2+ vault notes; **medium** = one vault note or strong external; **low** = practitioner or inference. Vault beats external; newest dated note beats older.

## Affordability

**1. The SKU funds the creator, or nobody does.** Run the creator CPO (take ex-affiliate, COGS, royalty, commission) before naming a creator. Single-unit toothpaste cannot fund a commission; bundles can (V047, V001). High.

**2. Use the measured take and split out the affiliate line.** TikTok 26.3% includes 4.6% affiliate, so model 21.7% plus the real rate; Shopee 33% (V001). Planning sensitivity 35% / 28% (V030). Published stacks (Shopee ~25.7%, TikTok ~21.9%) understate (V001). High.

**3. Commission, ads and fees share one 40% envelope.** After marketplace, COGS, fulfilment, back office and margin, 40% is left for commission + headcount + marketing + KOL (V041). At ROAS 4.7, commission tops out near 18.7% with zero fees (frameworks #2). High.

**4. A rate rise is cheap if it buys supply.** Monchhichi TikTok 3% → 10% needs only +14% affiliate orders to stand still (frameworks #3). The 3% rate was set "to protect margin on the assumption Mon would sell itself", and the team now calls it a structural error (V038). High.

**5. Every fixed fee is an orders-to-break-even test with a loss cap.** Fee ÷ CPO, written before signing (V047). The 12.12 package (฿300k, possible ฿100-200k loss) is the reference cap (V008). High.

**6. Challenge break-evens that imply impossible margins.** Wave ฿250k → ฿370k implies a 67.6% contribution rate; measured margins say ~฿439-625k (V041, V001; frameworks #5). Medium (inference on vault numbers).

## Selection

**7. Buy GMV, not followers.** Select on GPM and proven GMV (V047); "judge creators on conversion, not views"; "choose partners on commission × scalable volume" (V002). Rate cards disagree 2-3× per tier (S040-S042, 2024-2026), so they anchor a negotiation and nothing more. High.

**8. Creators join brands that already sell.** "Do not rush to buy affiliates" (V002). Stage gate: Stage 2 (฿10-100m) is when the affiliate machine gets built (V005); Moonshot's ~฿8.8M/month target puts it there (V028). Medium.

**9. Free seeding is a lottery; price the ticket.** Monchhichi celebrity seeding posted 10-20% and converted zero (V042) against a 30-40% benchmark (S046, PRACTITIONER US 2026). "You never get the big guys unless you pay" (V029). Be targeted: 1-2 promising creators (V029). High.

**10. Fan reach is not shop traffic.** Wave / Tops of Beauty push: ~9.9M potential X reach, ~168 tracked TikTok clicks (V016); Oct 1-6 events: ~200 link clicks, none attributable (V028). Medium.

**11. Match the creator to the real buyer.** Monchhichi sells to collectors and fans (61% returning, 68% pre-order, 39 prior Linee buyers, V016); its creators are fandom creators, not Linee acquisition. Loom is curated, never a mass affiliate flood (V047). High.

## Commission mechanics

**12. Treat the opening rate as a 30-day floor.** Open changes 30 days, Target 5; cuts lag 30 days (V002; re-verify in Seller Centre). Launch at a sustainable rate and boost with time-boxed Target. Medium.

**13. Never equal rates across baskets, and never hide the red rate.** Red ≈ half of yellow is Thai practice (V002); equal 3% was a structural error (V038); unannounced red rates make top creators drift (V002). Medium.

**14. One commission table, per brand and channel, reviewed monthly.** Decided 2026-09-25 (V038). Milk owns the red-basket rate per SKU (V002 open check). High.

## Content and boost

**15. Creators need paid support, and boost must concentrate.** "Videos that go viral generally had some paid ad input" (V029). Top ~10 clips, ~80% in-app / 20% C-pass (V045); never drip-feed (V039). High.

**16. Free product beats non-performing ad spend for building content.** Decided 2026-09-25 (V038); Loom: give units, attach baskets, ~฿10k into GMV Max behind basketed KOL clips (V039). Scaling a new brand = ads + content + samples, never ads alone (V030). High.

**17. Count cost per selling clip.** ~95 pieces/month while fresh clips sold ฿0.9k on 10.10 vs ฿14.0k on 7.7 (V016). "The content engine and the commerce engine are disconnected" (V016). High.

**18. Lock ad rights in the contract.** Authorisation is ad use only, not copyright; mass authorisation can be revoked at once; per-video Spark codes for hero assets (S015, S016 PRACTITIONER 2026). GMV Max is the only TikTok sales campaign type and pulls mass-authorised affiliate clips (S017, S018, S082; rule >60 days, re-verify). Medium.

## Compliance and systems

**19. Claims are the risk, not disclosure.** No treat/cure, no doctor or dentist certifying, no unverified before/after (S051 SECONDARY 2025-11; V023); #โฆษณา on every contract as a hedge (V047, S060). High.

**20. Law beats doctrine on tax.** WHT 3% on creator service fees, 2% on pure ad placement (S064-S066 PRIMARY); the doctrine's 5% is unconfirmed (V047, S071). Medium.

**21. One system, one number.** Creator Hub is the single KOL system; To The Moon is a tracker; no new sheets (V037). Confirmed basis, SBU export (V015, V013). High.

**22. Consent and respect for fans.** Get consent before reposting fan content (V028); over-react deliberately to the fan community (letters, gift baskets) was advised, owner not confirmed (V030). Medium.
