# Stories: Head of Creator Strategy (kol)

Institutional memory: dated episodes from the vault that bear on creators, commission and content. Figures as the vault states them; conflicts noted; arithmetic marked as this seat's.

## Creators who did not sell

1. **300 Spiffy videos, almost no sales (designed 2026-08-28, read 2026-10-06).** Plan: 50 KOLs × ฿2,500 for 6 clips (~฿125k, ~300 clips, fee includes the assets), PR boxes to 50 curated KOLs, colour boost 200 boxes / 1,000 singles. Result: ~300 TikTok videos, only 3-4 above 100 likes, no affiliate sales, ~฿700 ad spend. "Videos that go viral generally had some paid ad input." Open question: wrong KOL selection or wrong P&L assumption. Lesson: a creator wave without paid support and a selling offer is a content purchase, not a sales channel; at a 15% rate it needed ~554 orders to break even (frameworks #5). (V045, V029)

2. **Spiffy followers 1,243 → 6 (2026-10-16).** September follower growth was KOL-driven; in Oct 1-13 it collapsed, so KOL support appears to have stopped after the 30-creator 8 Oct drop. Spiffy TikTok ROAS 0.46, cut; meanwhile Spiffy LIVE rose ฿9.1k → ฿19.4k on ฿0 live ads. Lesson: creator momentum stops the day the programme stops; live was the cheaper growth line. (V016, V013, V033)

3. **Monchhichi celebrity seeding: 10-20% posted, zero conversion (2026-09-17).** First batch. The meeting concluded content/UGC supply is no longer the constraint; traffic likely is. Lesson: seeding needs post-rate control and a traffic plan; the benchmark is 30-40% (S046). (V042, V007)

4. **9.9M reach, 168 clicks (2026-10-16).** The Wave / Tops of Beauty fan push had ~9.9M potential X reach and ~168 tracked TikTok clicks. Oct 1-6 events: IG ~7% engagement, ~100k organic live views, ~200 link clicks, ~฿12,000 sales, none attributable. On X, the brand account cannot post Wave content or attach a buy link. Lesson: fan reach is not shop traffic; no UTM, no event money. (V016, V028)

5. **Linee TikTok affiliate ads with no lift (2026-09-28).** ~฿500/day behind Linee affiliate content showed no visible lift in affiliate sales. Lesson: a drip on unproven clips teaches nothing; concentrate or stop. (V035, V039)

6. **Loom lifestyle content did not convert (2026-09-25 to 10-16).** Loom ads ~฿600-700/day at ROAS ~1; 786 Shopee visitors and ฿0 sales two weeks running; content 9 → 6 → 2. Lesson: products that need explaining need demo creators and live, and a listing that converts; "fix it or delist it". (V038, V013, V016, V045)

## Creators and content that worked

7. **Affiliate ads on the 3D cap (2026-09-25).** Affiliate ads using 3D cap and body-care content cut CPR from 50-60 to 10-20 and lifted Monchhichi on Lazada from ฿400 to ฿6,000/day. Monchhichi was top seller for 2 days on payday with the cap. Lesson: proven content plus a scarce GWP plus spend is the formula that moved Mon. (V038, V035)

8. **Fresh clips ฿0.9k vs ฿14.0k (2026-10-16).** ~95 pieces/month produced, yet fresh TikTok clips sold ฿0.9k on 10.10 vs ฿14.0k on 7.7; Linee GMV Max ROI rose 2.93 → 3.93 while impressions fell 297k → 56k on hero clips 4-7 months old: "Starved, not broken." Lesson: count selling clips, refresh hero creative before the double-day. (V016)

9. **Shopee Video, a free lift (2026-09-17).** Loading existing videos into Shopee Video converted sales where they previously made zero. Lesson: every creator clip with rights should also run on Shopee Video with an AMS link. (V044, V043)

## Commission and money

10. **The 3% structural error (2026-09-21 to 09-25).** Monchhichi TikTok commission was 3% on both red and yellow baskets, set to protect margin on the assumption Mon would sell itself; Shopee affiliate 15%; normal list ~10%. Proposal: trim Shopee slightly, raise TikTok to ~10%; 09-21 "negotiating 7-10% with Ploy, to confirm with Care"; final rate not recorded. The May GTM plan had 15-25%. Monchhichi TikTok affiliate ROAS ~1.0. Lesson: a rate below creator visibility buys no supply; a planned rate that never ships is the costliest one. (V038, V041, V019, V007)

11. **The ฿2M envelope and the 40% rule (2026-09-21).** Affiliate creator budget ฿2M; Milk proposes her own envelope (example ฿300k to deliver ฿2M sales); Wave's role ideally moves into Milk's budget. Cost model: 57-60% fixed stack, 40% for commission + headcount + marketing + KOL. Big KOL Wave ฿250k → ฿370k break-even ("anything above is upside"). The team feared confirming ฿50-60k on KOLs who self-select; decision owner unclear. Lesson: two-axis commitment (revenue and cost) per owner; recompute any break-even that implies a 68% margin. (V041, V007)

12. **Celebrity pairs and the ฿900k competitor (2026-10-01).** A celebrity pair went from ~฿200k+ each to ~฿500k for the pair; a mid-level pair at ~฿400k was suggested. A competitor reportedly paid ฿900k and got back ฿1.2M: "ไม่คุ้ม". 10.10 plan instead: 20 KOL posts + ≥15 Spark/Gen codes released gradually, 2 KOLs per brand co-posting, 30 Spiffy KOLs posting on 8 Oct, judge paid creators on data. Lesson: at 57% margin the competitor lost ~฿216k (this seat's arithmetic). (V033)

13. **12.12 package declined at ฿300k (2026-09-28).** Shopee 12.12 Platinum ~฿300k, possible ฿100-200k loss: declined; FMCG & Beauty Platinum joined (expected ฿500k over 3 days). Lesson: the reference loss cap for any fixed creator or package fee. (V036, V008)

14. **CN agency rejected (2026-09-22 / 10-05).** Agency quotes of ฿300k for 200 influencers rejected; Chinese-in-Thailand creators ~5k followers at ฿7,500; freelancers per deliverable, ฿15-20k max; WeChat only. Lesson: pay per deliverable, never per headcount promise. (V040, V032)

## Systems, targets and buyers

15. **KOL workflow rebuilt three times (2026-09-28).** No technical PM; decision: Creator Hub is the single KOL system, To The Moon only a campaign tracker, no new sheets; content flows KOL → Content OS → PIM; first objective of the October system is that the team stops using Excel. Lesson: without one system there is no creator-level GMV, so "which creators move GMV" stays a GAP. (V037)

16. **To The Moon's top creators are staff (2026-09-25).** 60-100 KOLs; 2 of the top 3 are internal staff; no loyalty or ambassador tier; affiliate share unknown; KPI 10%. Lesson: measure before building tiers. (V038)

17. **September 16M → 12M → 11M (2026-09-17).** Target cut repeatedly; landing ~9.5M; KOL −4% MoM, Live the only grower (+9%); asked for the root cause, marketing said "no idea". New rules: ±20% target accuracy, alert after 2 days below run-rate, lock Q4 targets. Lesson: the creator channel enters its own target (KOL videos, Spark codes, GMV) and actuals in the SBU sheet. (V042, V038, V010)

18. **Linee 10.10 vs 9.9 vs 7.7 (2026-10-16).** Linee −25% vs 9.9, −50% vs 7.7; new buyers 1,817 → 994 → 787, returning flat ~170-180; strips buy 7 get 7 ฿450k (7.7) vs mostly 7 get 1 ฿179k (10.10). Lesson: creators are the new-buyer engine, but they cannot sell a weak offer; brief creators on the strongest live offer. (V016)

19. **Monchhichi is a fandom, not a Linee funnel (2026-10-16).** 61% returning buyers, ~68% of 10.10 GMV on pre-order, only 39 Monchhichi buyers had bought Linee, mixed orders 120 → 15. Lesson: Mon creators are collector and nostalgia creators; brief for scarcity and flavour stories (KOL watch metric: 3 of top 10 read a full Thai sentence, V020). (V016, V020)

20. **The fan who objected (2026-10-08).** A fan objected to a story repost; it was removed with an apology. Rule: consent before reposting. (V028)

21. **Lazada big-pack collapse (2026-09-25).** Moonshot big-pack sales fell from ~120,000 to ~4,000/week with no change in ads or traffic; cause unknown. Lesson (inference): keep an event log, and check reseller listings, affiliate links and listing changes first when a line collapses with flat traffic. (V038)
