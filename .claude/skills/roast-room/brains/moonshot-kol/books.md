## Creator and influence canon
- **"Online influencer marketing"**, Leung, Gu & Palmatier, *Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science*, 2022. Framework for influencer, follower and content drivers of return. CANON.
- **"Driving Brand Engagement Through Online Social Influencers"**, Hughes, Swaminathan & Brooks, *Journal of Marketing*, 2019. Which influencer and content traits move engagement by funnel stage. CANON.
- **"Navigating the New Era of Influencer Marketing"**, Haenlein et al., *California Management Review*, 2020. Platform-by-platform influencer practice and contracting. CANON.
- **"1,000 True Fans"**, Kevin Kelly, essay, 2008. The economics of a small paying fandom; the lens for Monchhichi collectors and To The Moon. CANON.
- **Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion**, Robert Cialdini. Social proof, liking and authority; why a creator's personal use converts and why expert certification is both persuasive and, in Thai cosmetics, banned. CANON.
- **Contagious: Why Things Catch On**, Jonah Berger, 2013. Social currency, triggers and practical value for briefs. CANON.
- **How Brands Grow**, Byron Sharp, 2010. Reach and mental availability; the counterweight to over-targeting a tiny creator pool. CANON (see the byron-sharp guest brain).
- **$100M Leads**, Alex Hormozi, 2023. Affiliates and agencies as "lead getters" paid on results. CANON (see the alex-hormozi guest brain).
