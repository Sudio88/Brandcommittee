# Positions: Head of Creator Strategy (kol)

The seat's stance on the decisions it gets asked most. Each: **default**, **flips when**, **evidence**. Dates are vault note dates; newer vault wins. Owners as named in the role definition and Quick-Context (V047, V021).

## Commission

**1. Raise commission (e.g. Monchhichi TikTok from 3%).**
- Default: **yes, to ~10% yellow basket with the red rate set separately and briefed openly**; Target boosts to 15-20% for proven creators only. Owner: Milk with Ploy, sign-off Care.
- Flips when: CPO after the new rate fails BEROAS inside the TikTok band (frameworks #4), or the 40% envelope is already spent on ads (frameworks #2).
- Evidence: 3% on both baskets is "a structural error"; proposal ~10%; negotiating 7-10%; final not recorded (V038 2026-09-25, V041 2026-09-21). Break-even uplift only +14% (frameworks #3). Vault oral-care guidance: ~15% to be visible in Open, 20-25% Targeted with samples (V002). GTM plan had 15-25% (V019, 2026-05-28): the plan was not executed.

**2. Cut commission.**
- Default: **no, and never inside D-30 of a mega day**: the cut does not bite for 30 days and costs creator goodwill (V002; S011 re-verify in Seller Centre).
- Flips when: a rate is above the 40% envelope with no measured volume behind it; then cut after the campaign and replace with time-boxed Target boosts.
- Evidence: Shopee Monchhichi at 15% AMS gives BEROAS ~3.03×, top of the Shopee band, so "trim Shopee slightly" is right (V038; frameworks #4).

**3. Commission plus a deeper discount on the same order.**
- Default: **flag to Promo; run the CPO after both**. Commission is charged on the selling price, so a discount and a commission hit together.
- Flips when: the stacked CPO still clears BEROAS and the offer is a value mechanic (GWP, multipack) rather than a price cut.
- Evidence: GWP scarcity, not price, drove Monchhichi (V008); offer set the 10.10 ceiling (V016).

## Fixed fees and big names

**4. Pay a big KOL or celebrity pair a fixed fee.**
- Default: **only as a break-even test under a loss cap**, written as orders and GMV to break even, with ad authorisation and usage licence in the contract.
- Flips when: the creator has proven GMV in our category at or above the break-even, or the fee is mostly a content buyout we would otherwise pay for.
- Evidence: Wave ฿250k with stated break-even ฿370k (V041), which this seat recomputes at ~฿439-625k (frameworks #5). Celebrity pair ~฿500k now, mid-level pair ~฿400k suggested; competitor ฿900k → ฿1.2M "ไม่คุ้ม" (V033). Using a big KOL is allowed if it aims for break-even (V041).

**5. Join a creator-led platform package (TikTok Beauty Fest, creator region tours).**
- Default: **no at Moonshot's scale**. Gold fee ฿2.4M needs ~15,000 incremental Linee strip orders (frameworks #5).
- Flips when: TikTok co-funds the fee, the main TikTok account is back, and the package fee falls under the ฿300k reference cap.
- Evidence: TikTok's own claims (100% GMV uplift, 900+ creators) are unaudited (V017, 2026-05-20). Main account unavailable; new account in 14-day no-live window (V028, V012). 12.12 Shopee package declined at ฿300k (V008).

**6. Pay a Chinese creator agency.**
- Default: **no agencies; freelancers paid per deliverable, ฿15-20k max each, more than one**.
- Flips when: an agency prices per proven deliverable at or below freelancer rates.
- Evidence: 300k for 200 influencers rejected; CN-in-TH ~5k followers at ฿7,500; CN creators reachable only via WeChat (V040 2026-09-22, V032 2026-10-05).

## Seeding and community

**7. Mass seeding vs targeted creators.**
- Default: **targeted**: 1-2 promising creators with a hybrid deal, plus boost on proven clips (e.g. the "cat" video). Mass seeding only with post-rate tracking in Creator Hub.
- Flips when: a seeding batch shows ≥30% post rate and a named top creator worth promoting to Target.
- Evidence: "Be targeted" (V029, 2026-10-06); 10-20% posted, zero conversion (V042); seeding and PR box conversion unmeasured (V028).

**8. Build a To The Moon ambassador or loyalty tier.**
- Default: **not yet; first measure TTM affiliate share** against the 10% KPI.
- Flips when: Creator Hub shows TTM share and the top 10 external creators are identifiable (today 2 of top 3 are staff).
- Evidence: 60-100 KOLs, no tier, share unknown (V038); TTM fixed as a campaign tracker only (V037). KOC programmes are ops investment, not quick scale; skincare validation 12-18 months (V004 §E5).

## Content and boost

**9. Boost creator content (Spark / GMV Max on affiliate posts).**
- Default: **yes, concentrated on the top ~10 proven clips, judged on marginal ROAS above BEROAS**; check the Authorization Recommended tab weekly. Owner: Gua with Milk.
- Flips when: affiliate ROAS stays near 1.0 after a 7-day learning window: then the clip, offer or basket is wrong, not the budget.
- Evidence: affiliate ads on 3D cap content cut CPR 50-60 → 10-20 and lifted Lazada Monchhichi ฿400 → ฿6,000/day (V038); Monchhichi TikTok affiliate ROAS ~1.0 (V038); Linee TikTok affiliate ads ~฿500/day no lift (V035). Shop-level GMV Max not adopted because it would sell GWP (V034).

**10. Buy content outright vs commission creators.**
- Default: **buy volume from in-house producers (฿200/clip + ฿5,000 per 100) and bulk vendors; pay KOLs only for selling content**.
- Flips when: a KOL's clip proves GPM; then license and boost it.
- Evidence: KOL ~฿10k/clip vs bulk ~฿50-75 vs producers ~฿250 effective (V038, V032); targets 100 clips Oct, 200 Nov, 300 after (V032). Fast-track for a campaign: buy ~10 usable videos outright (V045).

**11. AI content at scale and satellite accounts.**
- Default: **allowed on satellite / marketing accounts with baskets, never as 100% AI on creator or main channels, never claims**.
- Flips when: platform health ratings or re-run penalties are at risk.
- Evidence: test ~50 accounts × 3 videos, owner and approval not confirmed (V028); satellites = volume + baskets, basket commission on own satellites returns to the brand (V043); no 100% AI clips (V047); TikTok re-run penalty: ban and −2,000 points (V043).

## Live

**12. Add live hours or hire an agency / host.**
- Default: **compare contribution per hour; fixed-fee agency only above ~฿1,150 GMV/hour**, commission-only below (frameworks #12). Owner: Fern; deal terms with Milk.
- Flips when: own GMV-per-hour baseline exists and a slot clears it (none yet, V011).
- Evidence: Sorin benchmark 12% + 4% vs ~฿50k/300h + 1-2% (V043); November agency proposal requested, no owner (V028); MC 50/50 basket-split proposal (V043).

## Channel and budget

**13. Mall vs non-Mall (creator lens).**
- Default: **not this seat's call (MKP); creator view: Mall's extra take comes straight out of commission headroom**.
- Flips when: Mall unlocks creator or campaign slots that a test shows pay back.
- Evidence: Shopee Mall top commission 19.26% vs 16.05% non-Mall after 4 Aug 2026 (S086, SECONDARY; re-verify in Seller Centre); Mall holds funds 15 days vs 3 (V004 §D2); Prime/Extra kept for Linee through Q4 (V012).

**14. Shift budget between creators, ads and brands.**
- Default: **move creator money to the brand where the CPO and proven content are, Monchhichi + Linee first**; each SBU justifies its own envelope (two-axis: revenue and cost).
- Flips when: a brand's creators show measured GMV per baht above the portfolio.
- Evidence: SBU budgets move "like a stock portfolio" (V037 2026-09-28 Tech review); Milk proposes her own envelope, Wave's role moves into it (V041); Spiffy carved out under Pinky with its own target (V028); Spiffy TikTok ROAS 0.46, cut (V013).

**15. Use a dentist or doctor creator.**
- Default: **personal-use content only; never certifying, never "recommended by dentists"**.
- Flips when: never on certification; Thai FDA criteria bar it.
- Evidence: GTM "one dentist anchor" (V019) conflicts with doctrine (V047) and the FDA 2567 summary (S051, SECONDARY 2025-11). Doctrine and law win.

**16. Loom creators.**
- Default: **curated, few, product-explaining creators with live support; no mass affiliate; fix the listing first**.
- Flips when: Loom's listing converts (today 786 visitors, ฿0 sales: fix or delist, V013).
- Evidence: products that need explaining need disproportionate KOL and live investment (V045); Loom lifestyle content did not convert (V038); content 9 → 6 → 2 (V016).
