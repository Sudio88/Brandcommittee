# Books: Head of Pricing & Promotion (promo)

Reading list for this pillar. Grades: **SECONDARY** = surfaced and summarised in this build's research (search summary, not read in full); **CANON** = standard reference named from the seat's own knowledge, not fetched in this build, so cite chapter and page before quoting it; **PRIMARY-PLATFORM** = official platform documentation. Vault notes are the first read; these come after.

## Promotion effectiveness and incrementality
- **"How Promotions Work"**, Blattberg, Briesch & Fox, *Marketing Science* 14(3), 1995. Empirical generalisations: promo effects are immediate and strong, which is why managers overuse them. SECONDARY (S039).
- **Promotion Dynamics**, Neslin & van Heerde, now publishers, 2008. Pre/post dips, long-term effects, reference prices. SECONDARY (S040).
- **"Decomposing the Sales Promotion Bump with Store Data"**, van Heerde, Leeflang & Wittink, *Marketing Science*, 2004. Splits lift into switching, category expansion and time-shifting; the basis of this seat's incrementality test. SECONDARY (S041).
- **Bijmolt et al., 2005 meta-analysis** (cited via *Oxford Handbook of Pricing Management*). Average promo elasticity −3.63; the prior for break-even checks. SECONDARY (S038).
- **Hendel & Nevo**, *Quantitative Marketing and Economics*, 2003 (post-promotion dip). Stockpiling and post-promo dips. SECONDARY (S043).
- **Mela, Jedidi & Bowman**, long-term promotion exposure and "lying in wait" for deals. Why frequent discounting trains customers to wait. SECONDARY (S046).
- **Sales Promotion: Concepts, Methods, and Strategies**, Blattberg & Neslin, Prentice Hall, 1990. The textbook on promo economics and baselines. CANON.
- **Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments**, Kohavi, Tang & Xu, Cambridge UP, 2020. How to design holdouts and avoid false lifts; applies to voucher holdouts. CANON.

## Pricing strategy and architecture
- **The Good-Better-Best Approach to Pricing**, Rafi Mohammed, *HBR*, 2018. Tiering instead of discounting the core; Loom, Kids and Monchhichi ladders. SECONDARY (S048).
- **The Strategy and Tactics of Pricing**, Nagle & Müller (with Gruyaert in later editions), Routledge. Value-based pricing, price-volume trade-offs, break-even sales change; the original source of the break-even uplift method. CANON.
- **Confessions of the Pricing Man**, Hermann Simon, Springer, 2015. Price as the strongest profit lever; why small price moves beat volume pushes. CANON.
- **Monetizing Innovation**, Ramanujam & Tacke (Simon-Kucher), Wiley, 2016. Design the product around the price; useful for new SKUs such as Monchhichi Strip and Nom Yen. CANON.
- **BrandZ premium pricing analysis**, Kantar / *Marketing Week*, 2020. Heavy discounting damages equity, most for premium brands. SECONDARY (S049).

## Measurement and unit economics
- **Common Thread Collective / Taylor Holiday**, contribution-margin daily pacing and CM layers. PRACTITIONER (S061, S060).
- **eightx, "ROAS vs contribution margin"**. BEROAS = 1 / CM before marketing. PRACTITIONER (S059).
- **Working Backwards**, Bryar & Carr, St. Martin's, 2021. Input vs output metrics for the weekly promo readout. CANON (named in sales research).
