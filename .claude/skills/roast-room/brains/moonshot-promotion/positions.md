# Positions: Head of Pricing & Promotion (promo)

The seat's stance on the decisions it gets asked most. Each: **default**, **flips when**, **evidence**. Dates are vault note dates; newer vault wins.

## Campaigns and packages

**1. Join a paid campaign package (Shopee Platinum/Diamond, TikTok Beauty Fest).**
- Default: **no, unless priced as a test** with fee, loss cap, read date and owner. Orders to break even = fee ÷ incremental CPO (frameworks #4).
- Flips when: required uplift ≤ measured history (9.9 D-day ~4×) and expected loss ≤ cap; or the package unlocks a new-buyer pool we can measure.
- Evidence: joined Shopee FMCG & Beauty on Platinum (expected ฿500k over 3 days); declined 12.12 Platinum ~฿300k, possible ฿100-200k loss; Ploy to present incremental sales and incremental cost of sale excluding ads (V037, 2026-09-28). Diamond ฿720k needed 6× vs historic 4× (V037). TikTok Gold ~฿4.8M+ is TikTok's own claim (V018, 2026-05-20).

**2. Join a double-day (9.9 / 10.10 / 11.11 / 12.12) at all.**
- Default: **yes, at registration-only cost, with ramp promos on**. Do not go dark on the ramp (V045).
- Flips when: entry requires a depth past the ceiling (V001), or penalty points put the shop at risk of a 28-day ban (V002).
- Evidence: the 30% participate/skip floor "never binds for us" (V001), so it is not a reason to join. 11.11 is the bigger month: November is the major month (V029).

**3. Flash-sale slots.**
- Default: **yes, nominate early, top 6 value hours** (V034). Best slot ~฿10-12k (V037).
- Flips when: the flash price writes a floor below the planned double-day price (V002).
- Evidence: 9.9 got zero slots because not queued; 147 impressions converted at 136 orders per 1,000 (V002). Shopee Shocking Sale nomination: 24-hour window, 5 working days out (V004 §A1, re-verify in Seller Centre).

## Depth and mechanics

**4. Deepen the discount.**
- Default: **no; hold BAU core promo, flex 3-5-7%** (V039, 2026-09-25).
- Flips when: break-even uplift (frameworks #3) is below a measured uplift for that mechanic, depth stays under the ceiling, and the deeper price will not undercut the next campaign's floor. For Linee, the evidence favours restoring buy 7 get 7 on strips over a deeper %: 7.7 ฿450k vs 10.10 ฿179k (V017, 2026-10-16).
- Evidence: Lowering ROAS caps to chase volume cut ROAS without lifting sales (V032).

**5. GWP vs price cut.**
- Default: **GWP**. Everyday cap ~฿50 (V049); Monchhichi cap ~฿20 cost (V040).
- Flips when: the gift is not licensor-approved (Monchhichi), stock of the gift is thinner than the promo window, or the gift is the SKU customers already buy.
- Evidence: Monchhichi 30-40k/day with cap promo vs ~10-12k after (V040, V032); worked CPO: 349 + cap ฿144.40 vs 299 no gift ฿137.40 (frameworks #1). Stickers are the interim GWP until caps land ~17 Oct (V031).

**6. Bundles, multipacks, add-ons.**
- Default: **add-on first, multipack at single-pack value, never on stock-limited MSL** (V004 §A2, V015).
- Flips when: MSL import lands with committed stock.
- Evidence: MSL single ฿234 vs 4-pack ฿185 (V015). Add-on product cannot also sit in a bundle; show savings across pack 1 / 7 / 14 (V032). Monchhichi "buy 3 get 1" did not lift AOB, sales fell; next test "buy 2, cap at 50% off" (V032, 2026-10-05).

**7. Raise ISP (shelf price).**
- Default: **yes after double-days, by SKU**: +10% AOV < ฿500, +5% AOV > ฿500, ~3% under ฿200; voucher tier ~฿499 (V048, 2026-08-27).
- Flips when: a campaign window starts within ~14 days (letter needed, no promos during change) (V048).
- Evidence: campaign-joining was delayed by a card/ISP change, "two-week wait" (V031).

**8. Spiffy 50% off.**
- Default: **not on Shopee** (needs 3.23× vs a 3.1× band top); **TikTok or own-site yes** (2.65×) (V001).
- Flips when: Shopee take re-measured lower, or Spiffy COGS (18%) verified lower against a landed BOM.
- Evidence: Spiffy roughly break-even, "don't scale until the margin line is in" (V014). Spiffy TikTok ROAS 0.46: cut that ad line (V014).

**9. Loom promotions.**
- Default: **no headline % off on Loom**; quiet premium (V025). Fix the listing or delist first: 786 Shopee visitors, ฿0 sales in both weeks (V014, 2026-10-16).
- Flips when: listing fixed and a value set (hero + mini) is tested with a holdout.

## Commission, live, budget

**10. Raise creator commission.**
- Default: **raise only with an equal-or-lower discount on the same order**, and set dates to the lock rules (Open 30 days, Target 5) (V005).
- Flips when: commission replaces a deeper consumer discount (commission × volume beats % off) or the visibility floor (~15% oral care Open) blocks reach (V005).
- Evidence: Monchhichi TikTok 3% vs Shopee 15%; negotiating 7-10%, final not recorded (V011, V043). At 10% + 7% royalty Mon TikTok ceiling falls to ~63% (frameworks #10).

**11. Add live hours / live-only offers.**
- Default: **add hours only where contribution per hour is positive; use LIVE flash price for D-day depth**, not a shelf cut (V002).
- Flips when: own GMV/live-hour baseline exists (GAP today, V012) and shows the slot pays.
- Evidence: Exclusive Streaming Price ฿0 on 10.10, unused (V015). 14+7 and 7+7 were live-only on 10.10, so the strong offer barely reached shelf traffic (V016): put the hero offer on shelf too. 11.11 plan: Exclusive Streaming Price on, 3-4 prime sessions per brand (V015).

**12. Shift budget from discount to ads (or back).**
- Default: **move baht to whichever has the higher marginal contribution**; compare promo cost per incremental order to ads CAC.
- Flips when: CAC is known (unknown today, asked weekly, V029).
- Evidence: Oct 1-6 spend -43% and contribution -26% (V009): cutting spend cut sales by more than it saved. Moonshot cut cost ~฿22k profit (V014).

## Platform and structure

**13. Mall vs non-Mall.**
- Default: **stay as is through Q4**; Mall costs ~3pp more commission on Shopee (S010, re-verify in Seller Centre) and ~2 weeks of working capital (15-day hold) (V004 §D2).
- Flips when: Mall-only campaign slots or vouchers are shown to add > 3pp margin via conversion; Monchhichi may need Mall for authenticity and licensor channel approval (marketplace research inference; V019 channel gap).
- Evidence: no Mall conversion uplift data exists (GAP).

**14. Exit Shopee Prime/Xtra.**
- Default: **keep for Linee through Q4**; test exit on Non-mall in Q1, decide April (V048, V010).
- Flips when: redeployed fee runs at ~30% cost of sale and the Non-mall test holds sales.
- Evidence: Prime ฿400k in July ≈ 11% of net sales; early-2026 exit saw "เซลล์ฮวบ", re-joined ~Feb (V048). Aug 2026 round may have narrowed the PRIME commission discount (S011, re-verify).

- Flips when: the pre-event price is above the planned campaign price for the platform's look-back window.
- Evidence: real spikes 15th and 25th; mid-month and payday hold ~฿200k (V037).

**16. Launch-day giveaways (Nom Yen first 10/20 orders free or 50% off).**
- Default: **yes as a capped hook** (fixed count, fixed cost, pre-registered), not as a price.
- Evidence: ideas raised, not finalised (V030, 2026-10-06).

**17. Monchhichi Strip (MOQ 25,000 boxes, RRP ฿349, +~10% cost + 7% royalty).**
