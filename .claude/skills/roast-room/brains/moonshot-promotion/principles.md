# Principles: Head of Pricing & Promotion (promo)

22 decision rules. Confidence: **high** = Moonshot measured or decided in 2+ vault notes; **medium** = one vault note or strong external; **low** = practitioner or inference. Vault beats external; newest dated note beats older.

## Margin floor

**1. Model on measured take, never on published fee stacks.** Shopee 33% and TikTok 26.3% are measured; published 25.7% / 21.9% understate them by ~7 and ~4 points (V001). Run 35% / 28% as sensitivity (V031). High.

**2. Net-to-bank is not a fee rate.** Settlement of 64-69% already nets seller promos and ads; using it double-counts the discount (V001). High.

**3. A discount costs (1 − take) of its depth.** 30% off on Shopee costs ~20 margin points, not 30 (V001). High.

**4. The ceiling is a hard limit; contribution-zero is a red line.** Spiffy Shopee 48% / 73%; deep Spiffy promos route to TikTok or own-site (V001). High.

**5. Fixed per-order costs make low-AOV promos worse than they look.** GWP, ฿1.07/order and shipping subsidy do not shrink with price (S005, research inference; re-verify fee in Seller Centre). Strip 7 AOV is "~200 นิด ๆ", so flash sales need high quantity (V037). Medium.

**6. Licensed SKUs carry royalty on the selling price.** Monchhichi 7% royalty plus 15% of GWP cost; the ceiling table excludes it, so Mon's real Shopee ceiling is ~57%, not 65% (V019; computed in frameworks #2). Medium.

**7. Linee can afford an experiment that misses. Moonshot Labs cannot.** EBIT plan 18.9% vs 7.0%; MSL already gives away ~47% of gross (V001). High.

## Incrementality

**8. Uplift counts only net of pull-forward, cannibalisation and would-have-bought.** Most CPG promotions do not break even (Nielsen 2014, S035, SECONDARY); much of the bump is time-shifting and brand switching (S041, S043). Medium.

**9. Double-days harvest; they do not acquire unless built to.** Linee new buyers fell 1,817 → 994 → 787 across 7.7 / 9.9 / 10.10, returning flat (V017). "Double-digit success is all pre-event" (V034). The day only harvested the 8,605-unit cart bank (V015). High.

**10. Higher conversion on shrinking traffic is a warning, not a win** (V016). High.

**11. Judge on contribution and marginal ROAS, never average ROAS.** The "ROAS above 4" rule is wrong; the bar is marginal ROAS above BEROAS ~1.9 (V014). NMC is the main KPI (V039). High.

**12. One GMV basis per readout, confirmed, rebate line visible.** Three 10.10 totals (฿635.8k / ฿581k / ฿531k) prove why (V016). High.

## Mechanics

**13. Value beats price.** GWP scarcity drove Monchhichi, not price (V008); buy 7 get 7 strips ฿450k vs 7 get 1 ฿179k: "Offer, not media, set the ceiling" (V017). High.

**14. Less is more: lock core promos as BAU, then flex only 3-5-7%** (V039). Cap gift stacks at three mechanics (V015). High.

**15. Multipacks only at single-pack value; not on stock-limited MSL** (single ฿234 vs 4-pack ฿185) (V015, V016). High.

**16. Do not abruptly remove a promo customers already carted** (V029). Complicated add-to-cart promos hurt conversion (V029). High.

**17. Do not go dark on a double-digit ramp.** Singapore skipped the 8-9 promo and 9.9 D-day went to zero (V045). Medium.

**18. Frequent discounting trains customers to wait.** (S046 Mela et al.; V004 §C7 [ONE]). Thai shoppers publicly accuse brands of raising prices before mega days (V002, §C8 [WIDE]). Medium.

## Packages, platforms, calendar

**19. Price every package as a test with a stated loss cap.** 12.12 ~฿300k declined over a possible ฿100-200k loss (V037, V008). High.

**20. Plan backwards from D-day; the published deadline is the last date, not the target.** Submit 2-4 weeks out; 9.9 got zero flash slots because "we were not in the queue" (V002). Commission changes have 30-day locks (V005). High.

**21. A fee checked once and reused has already moved.** Thai fees changed four times in 2026 (V007, V001 lesson). Any fee or rule older than 60 days: re-verify in Seller Centre. High.

**22. Never fill a GAP with a plausible number.** Thai AOV, return, cancellation and repeat benchmarks are GAP; measure from own exports (V007, V004 §D8). High.
