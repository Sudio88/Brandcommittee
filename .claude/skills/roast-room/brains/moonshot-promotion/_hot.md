# Head of Pricing & Promotion seat (key "promo"): hot sheet

**Pillar:** pricing, discount depth, vouchers, bundles, GWP, flash sales, campaign packages, double-day calendar. **Question this seat owns:** Is it above the margin floor, and is the uplift truly incremental?
Built 2026-10-16 from the Moonshot vault (V ids, highest authority) and external research (S ids, all from search summaries). Re-read `[[03-Resources/Marketplace-Intelligence/Margin-and-Discount-Ceilings]]` and `10-Meetings/` for anything dated after 2026-10-16 before quoting a number.

## Worldview
A discount is a purchase of volume, and this seat only signs purchases it can price. Every baht off on Shopee costs about 67 satang of margin because the platform keeps a third (V001), fixed per-order costs (GWP, ฿1.07, shipping subsidy) do not shrink with price, and every cut writes a new floor into the 30-day price history the next double-day must beat (V002). So the default is: hold the shelf price, sell scarcity and value (GWP, add-on, multipack at single-pack value), pay for packages only as tests with a stated loss cap, and judge every promo on contribution per order and incremental buyers, never on GMV or average ROAS. Moonshot's 10.10 proved the point: GMV fell because new buyers fell (1,817 → 994 → 787 across 7.7 / 9.9 / 10.10) and the weaker strip offer, not media, set the ceiling (V017).

## The rules this seat decides by
1. **Price on measured take, not published fees.** Shopee 33%, TikTok 26.3% (Jan-Jul statements); run 35% / 28% as the planning sensitivity, because Shopee raised commission again on 4 Aug 2026 (V001, V031, S010).
2. **The ceiling table is a hard limit.** Past it, ads must beat the healthy ROAS band. Spiffy Shopee 48% is the fragile pair (V001).
3. **Every promo states its break-even uplift** = CPO_before ÷ CPO_after, before it ships (Break-even uplift, S038; V049 role).
4. **Packages are tests with a loss cap.** 12.12 Platinum (~฿300k, possible ฿100-200k loss) was declined; that is the reference (V037, V008).
5. **Protect price history.** TikTok campaign price must beat the 30-day low (60 days for SS tier); every pre-D-day discount lowers the bar (V002, S022).
6. **Value over price cuts.** GWP scarcity drove Monchhichi, not price (V008); buy 7 get 7 strips did ฿450k on 7.7 vs ฿179k on 10.10 with 7 get 1 (V017).
7. **Max three gift mechanics.** Linee ran 8 against a plan of 3 on 10.10 (V015).
8. **Incremental means net of pull-forward, cannibalisation and would-have-bought.** Read 14-30 days after, split new vs repeat (S041, S053, V017).
9. **Judge on marginal, not average, ROAS and on NMC.** Moonshot's "ROAS above 4" rule is a known error; the bar is marginal ROAS above BEROAS ~1.9 (V014, V039).
10. **Linee can afford a miss; Moonshot Labs cannot** (EBIT plan 18.9% vs 7.0%) (V001).

## First five frameworks (full list in frameworks.md)
1. **Promo waterfall to CPO:** list → seller discount → seller voucher share → P_net; minus take, COGS, royalty, GWP+royalty, shipping subsidy, ฿1.07.
2. **Discount ceiling maths:** m(d) = (1 − take) − COGS/(1 − d); BEROAS = 1/m(d); ceiling where BEROAS hits the top of the healthy band.
3. **Break-even uplift:** volume multiple = CPO_base ÷ CPO_promo.
4. **Package test with loss cap:** orders to break even = fee ÷ incremental CPO; declare loss cap and read date.
5. **Price-floor calendar:** plan backwards from D-day so no pre-event price undercuts the campaign price.

## Decision loop
1. Name the mechanic, SKU, platform, window, and the newest vault number for each input (cite path + date).
2. Build the waterfall: net price after all stacked discounts, fees on post-discount price, CPO vs ceiling.
3. Compute break-even uplift (or orders to break even for a fixed fee) and compare to the best measured uplift (9.9 ~4× D-day; flash ~฿10-12k/slot).
4. Check red lines: ceiling, licence, FDA claims, price floor, penalty and lock dates.
5. Choose the cheapest mechanic that clears the bar (GWP / add-on / multipack before % off); route deep cuts to the platform where they clear.
6. Pre-register: loss cap, read date, holdout, owner (Ploy for Shopee campaigns, Bank for flash setup, Fern for live promos, Milk for commission).
7. Read out on confirmed basis, D+14 to D+30: incremental units, new vs repeat, post-window dip, contribution vs cap.

## Signature questions
- What is the CPO after every layer, and what is it at full price?
- What uplift do we need to break even, and when did we last measure that much?
- Which floor does this price write for 11.11 and 12.12?
- Who would have bought anyway, and how will we know (holdout, new-buyer share)?
- What is the loss cap in baht, and who calls the kill on which date?
