# Stories: Head of Pricing & Promotion (promo)

Institutional memory: dated episodes from the vault that bear on pricing and promotion, newest decision context first within each group. Figures as the vault states them; conflicts noted.

## Packages and targets

1. **12.12 package declined at ฿300k (2026-09-28).** Shopee offered a 12.12 Platinum package ~฿300k; the team saw a possible ฿100-200k loss and said no, while joining the FMCG & Beauty campaign on Platinum (expected ฿500k over 3 days). Diamond's ฿720k target needed a ฿150k baseline × 6 against a historic ~4×, and ฿700k needed ~42,000 visitors, nearly double the record. Lesson: a package is a test with a loss cap; this is the reference cap. (V037, V008)

2. **September target 16M → 12M → 11M (2026-09-17 / 09-25).** Cut four times; landed ~9.5M (09-17) or "89% of target" (09-25), unreconciled. Peter: lock targets, don't revise; new rule ±20% accuracy and alert after 2 days below run-rate. Lesson: a promo plan built on a moving target cannot be judged; fix the baseline first. (V044, V039, V009)

3. **October at 65% of pace (2026-10-08).** ฿1M in Oct 1-6 against ฿8.8M; spend -43%, contribution -26%. Lesson: pulling spend and promo support at once cut sales by more than it saved. (V029, V009)

## Double-days

4. **Linee 10.10 vs 9.9 vs 7.7 (2026-10-16 readouts).** Portfolio 10.10 ฿581k vs ฿664k on 9.9 (-12%) on 20% less ad spend; Linee -25% vs 9.9 and -50% vs 7.7. New buyers 1,817 → 994 → 787; returning flat ~170-180. Strips: 7.7 buy 7 get 7 ฿450k vs 10.10 mostly 7 get 1 ฿179k. Lesson: "Offer, not media, set the ceiling"; the level problem is new-buyer acquisition. (V017)

5. **The cart bank harvest (10.10).** Linee banked 8,605 cart units over 4-9 Oct; cart-to-buyer jumped from 23-33% to 49.2% on D-day while add-to-cart stayed flat. 02:00-11:00 lost ฿86.9k with no owner. Cancellations 7.6% → 15.6%, 26 of 117 in the 00:00 hour. Lesson: D-day converts intent banked before it; own the pre-event ramp and the dead hours. (V015)

6. **Three totals for one day (10.10).** ฿635.8k MKP recap (placed), ฿581k decks, ฿531k Shopee confirmed. Lesson: pick one basis, confirmed, rebate visible; placed flatters ROAS 8-10%. (V016)

7. **Eight gift mechanics against a plan of three (10.10).** The Linee gift stack sprawled; the note says cap at three. Lesson: every extra mechanic adds cost and confusion; the complicated add-to-cart promo also hurt conversion (10-08). (V015, V029)

8. **The unused live lever (10.10).** Exclusive Streaming Price ฿0; 14+7 and 7+7 were live-only so the strong offer barely reached shelf traffic. Lesson: put the hero offer where the traffic is; use LIVE flash price for depth. (V015, V016)

9. **9.9 ROAS up, contribution down (2026-09-19 note).** GMV Max ROAS 4.01× and improving while incremental margin fell 48% → 43% and first-order contribution per new buyer fell ฿194 → ฿137. Lesson: ROAS is not contribution. (V002, V005)

10. **9.9 with zero flash slots (2026-09-19 note).** Not in the queue; the mechanic itself converted 136 orders per 1,000 impressions. Lesson: the published deadline is the last date, not the target. (V002)

11. **Singapore goes dark (2026-09-17).** Skipping the promo on 8-9 Sep collapsed 9.9 D-day sales to zero; moving it to "9.9 night → 10.10" recovered them. Lesson: do not go dark on a double-digit ramp. (V045)

## GWP and mechanics

12. **Monchhichi cap (Sep 2026).** Daily 30-40k at the cap-promo peak, ~10-12k after it ended around 21 Sep; top seller 2 days on payday with the cap. Cap costs ~฿20 and sells at ฿279, more profitable than toothpaste: sell it, remove "ห้ามซื้อ". Lesson: "GWP scarcity is the driver, not price." (V040, V036, V008)

13. **Buy 3 get 1 flops (2026-10-05).** No AOB lift, sales fell. Next test: buy 2, cap at 50% off; stickers as GWP until caps land ~17 Oct. Lesson: volume mechanics do not move a fan product; scarcity does. (V032, V031)

14. **Strip 14 after the promo (2026-10-05).** Sales dropped sharply when its promo ended; discontinuation still unconfirmed. Lesson: a SKU that only sells on promo is a price, not a product. (V032, V021)

15. **Lazada big-pack collapse (2026-09-25).** Big-pack sales fell from ~120,000 to 4,000 per week with no change in ads or traffic; cause unknown. Lesson: check price, listing and promo eligibility before blaming media; open problem. (V039)

16. **MSL single beats 4-pack (10.10 review).** Contribution ฿234 single vs ฿185 4-pack; platform rebate (31.8%) and ads (18.6%) cost ~5× the product. Lesson: on a stock-limited brand, margin first, not AOV. (V015)

## Content and creators touching promo economics

17. **300 Spiffy videos, almost no sales (2026-10-06).** ~300 TikTok videos, 3-4 above 100 likes, no affiliate sales, ~฿700 ad spend. Lesson: content volume without paid support or a commercial hook does not sell; open question whether the P&L assumption was wrong. (V030)

18. **Shopee Prime exit and return (early 2026, told 2026-08-27).** An exit early in the year saw "เซลล์ฮวบ" and the shop re-joined around February. Lesson: programme fees buy traffic; model the sales loss before saving the fee. (V048)

19. **Moonshot ads cut (Oct 2026).** Saved ฿64.7k, lost ฿150k GMV, marginal ROAS 2.3 vs BEROAS ~1.7-1.9: cost ~฿20k+ profit. Lesson: average-ROAS rules mislead. (V014)
