# Books: Seth Godin

Built from how he describes and applies his books in public (talks, the 2024 interview, and blog posts). No book text was fetched or reproduced, so no quote below is from a book. Publication years are from the standard bibliographic record; the corpus confirms the order and some dates (for example, This Is Marketing launched in November 2018, S037). Core concepts are summarised in his own later wording.

## Permission Marketing (1999)

- **Core thesis:** Interrupting strangers is getting less effective; earn the privilege of sending anticipated, personal and relevant messages to people who want them.
- **Key frameworks:** Permission (frameworks.md #9); the TV-industrial complex as the thing being replaced (S001).
- **Taught in:** "Permission is anticipated, personal and relevant." (S048); also S043, S004, S001.
- **Story:** Jack Trout wrote that it was based on a flawed idea; it became a bestseller and, by his account, helped create the email marketing market (S020).

## Purple Cow (2003)

- **Core thesis:** In a world of too many choices, people ignore the ordinary. Build remarkability into the product, for the people who care, and let them spread it. Remarkable is a product decision, not an advertising brief.
- **Key frameworks:** The Purple Cow test, otaku, very good is average (frameworks.md #1 to #3).
- **Taught in:** S001 (the 2003 TED talk introduces the cow and closes with "That's a purple cow" (S001)), S003 (remarkable means worth making a remark about; not gimmicky viral video), S010, S051.
- **Figures he cites:** first 10,000 copies shipped in a milk carton (S003). Written so he could dedicate a book to Lionel Poilâne (S045).

## The Dip (2007)

- **Core thesis:** Most worthwhile pursuits have a Dip, a long stretch where effort rises and rewards lag, that separates those who reach mastery from those who give up. Other paths are cul-de-sacs (dead ends) that never improve. Persist through the Dip; quit the cul-de-sac; decide before you start. The book is not an argument for quitting fast.
- **Key frameworks:** Dip vs. dead end (frameworks.md #15).
- **Taught in:** S013 (a dip that must be survived vs. a dead end with no hope), S057 (decide how and when to quit before you begin), S052, S058.
- **Note:** The term "cul-de-sac" is the book's; the fetched blog posts use "dead end". No book text was fetched.

## Tribes (2008)

- **Core thesis:** A tribe is a group connected to one another, to a leader and to an idea. The internet lets people on the fringes find each other; anyone can lead by challenging the status quo, building culture and connecting people.
- **Key frameworks:** Tribes: lead and connect, three questions (frameworks.md #13); people like us (#7).
- **Taught in:** S002 (TED 2009 "The tribes we lead"), S004, S039. In 2019 he wrote "I didn’t say this clearly enough in my book." (S039): the tribe is not yours to own.

## Linchpin (2010)

- **Core thesis:** Be indispensable by doing generous, human work that cannot be reduced to a manual. Only lightly present in this corpus (he mentions a launch video from 2010, S011); not a primary source for this seat.

## What To Do When It's Your Turn (2014)

- **Core thesis:** A call to ship work that matters. Relevant here as a product case: sold in multi-packs to spread an idea, with more than 70% of orders from repeat buyers (S011).

## This Is Marketing (2018)

- **Core thesis:** Marketing is the generous act of helping someone solve a problem: choose the smallest viable audience, understand their worldview and status, make better things for them, and earn word of mouth through *people like us do things like this*.
- **Key frameworks:** Smallest viable audience (#4), who's it for and what's it for (#5), people like us (#7), status roles (#8), crossing from early adopters (#12), race to the top (#14).
- **Taught in:** S040, S043 (ends "This is marketing."), S044, S050, S037, S034. He says the book is based on his Marketing Seminar workshop (S044).
- **Figures he cites:** #1 Wall Street Journal business list at launch, a third of sales in audio (S037); more than 250,000 copies in under a year (S044).

## This Is Strategy (2024)

- **Core thesis:** Strategy is choosing which game to play inside systems most people take for granted, with tension at the centre of every launch.
- **Key frameworks:** Four strategic choices: customers, competition, validation, distribution (#6); tension; seeing the system.
- **Taught in:** S003 (the full 2024 interview).
- **Figures he cites:** none; he says he used Claude as an editor to find gaps (S003).

## Not covered by this corpus

Unleashing the Ideavirus (2000), All Marketers Are Liars (2005) and The Practice (2020) are part of his body of work but no source fetched here covers them in depth. Do not attribute specific claims or quotes from them in this seat.
