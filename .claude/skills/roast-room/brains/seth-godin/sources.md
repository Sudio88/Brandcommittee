# Sources: Seth Godin

60 sources: 2 TED talk transcripts (2003, 2009), 1 long-form podcast interview transcript (Lenny's Podcast, December 2024) and 57 posts from Seth's Blog (seths.blog, 2015 to 2021). Every quote in this brain was checked by script as an exact substring of the fetched text of the source it cites.

## How the text was obtained (read before adding quotes)

- seths.blog, ted.com and most other sites were not reachable from the build environment, so primary text came from public mirrors on GitHub and GitLab, then matched to the original publications:
  - **TED talks (S001, S002):** the TED transcripts dataset (ted_main.csv / transcripts.csv, Kaggle "TED Talks" by Rounak Banik, mirrored in the build workspace), cross-checked against the TED-RNN corpus at https://gitlab.com/barionleg/TED-RNN (input.txt). URLs are the official TED talk pages.
  - **Podcast (S003):** transcript from https://github.com/ChatPRD/lennys-podcast-transcripts (episodes/seth-godin/transcript.md). URL is the episode's YouTube page.
  - **Blog (S004 to S060):** post text from a 2022 scrape of seths.blog at https://github.com/davidwaldherr/seths.blog-Web-Scraper (blog.txt), which captured the first archive page (10 posts) of every month from January 2015 to December 2021. The month is derived from the scrape order and was spot-checked (This Is Marketing launch post lands in 2018-11; "Hypergrowth" in 2015-03 and "Almost no one" in 2019-03 match live URLs). The URL column gives the exact post URL where it was confirmed, otherwise the seths.blog month archive that lists the post.
- **Scrape artifacts:** the scraper removed double quotation marks and some line breaks, so a few sentences run together without a space after the period. Quotes were taken from passages without internal quotation marks and are reproduced exactly as fetched, including the occasional missing space. Re-check against the live post before publishing any quote outside this brain.
- **Not used:** book text (none fetched), Akimbo transcripts (not reachable), and secondary "Godin quotes" collections. Two widely circulated lines (marketing as stories rather than stuff; content marketing as the only marketing left) have no primary source here and are excluded.
- **Evidence type:** all sources are opinion, argument and anecdote by the author. None are controlled studies.
