# Principles: Seth Godin

21 decision rules, clustered across sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Confidence measures how consistently he says it, not whether it is proven. His support is anecdote, case examples and argument; none of these rules come from controlled studies.

## Product

**1. The product is the marketing. Fix the thing before buying attention for it.**
- "that is when I learned marketing is the product." (S003)
- "Great products work far better than great ads do." (S016)
- "Hoping to grow your business by word of mouth by willing your customers to talk about you isn’t nearly as productive as making something worth talking about." (S051)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S003, S016, S051). Confidence: high.

**2. Remarkable means worth a remark, and it is designed in, not bolted on. It is not a gimmick or a stunt.**
- "The word remarkable means worth making a remark about." (S003)
- "So I'm not talking about coming up with some viral video that's ridiculous in its gimmickry." (S003)
- "Remarkable often starts with the problem you set out to solve and the way you choose to solve it." (S010)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S003, S010, S028). Confidence: high.

**3. Very good is invisible. Being safe and average is the risky choice.**
- "The riskiest thing you can do now is be safe." (S001)
- "Because all mediocre means is average." (S003)
- Sources: 2 (S001, S003). Confidence: medium.

**4. Build the reason to tell into the product itself.**
- "The question is, will this work better for my users if they tell other people about it?" (S003)
- Sources: 2 (S001, S003). Confidence: medium.

**5. Make what they want, not what you want.**
- "You can't paint a picture of where you want to go. You have to paint a picture of where they want to go" (S003)
- Sources: 2 (S003, S060). Confidence: medium.

**6. Quality is meeting a spec you chose for them. Raise the spec; then ship.**
- "Quality is not luxury. Quality is not perfection. Quality means meeting spec, and if you meet spec, you're done." (S003)
- Sources: 1 (S003). Confidence: low.

## Audience

**7. It is for someone, not everyone. The mass market does not exist for you.**
- "Your work is for someone, not everyone." (S009)
- "We may dream of the mass market, but the mass market doesn't dream of us." (S022)
- "You’re going to reach virtually no one.That’s okay.The question is: which no one?" (S040)
- Sources: 4 (S002, S009, S022, S040). Confidence: high.

**8. Specific beats general.**
- "When you’re specific, you only find two kinds of people: people who are delighted, and the rest." (S038)
- Sources: 2 (S038, S042). Confidence: medium.

**9. Choosing your customers chooses your future. Say who it is not for.**
- "You got to pick, today, before you even turn the page, who are you for and who are you not for?" (S003)
- Sources: 2 (S003, S006). Confidence: medium.

**10. Market to the people who care and are listening, then let them tell the others.**
- "you sell to the people who are listening, and just maybe, those people tell their friends." (S001)
- "But if you delight the early adopters, they spread the word." (S050)
- Sources: 3 (S001, S028, S050). Confidence: high.

## Permission and trust

**11. Earn attention with permission; do not steal it.**
- "Permission is anticipated, personal and relevant." (S048)
- "The race for cheap, unearned attention is a race that can’t be won." (S043)
- Sources: 3 (S004, S043, S048). Confidence: high.

**12. Aim to be missed, not merely noticed.**
- "You could be the one we'd miss if you were gone." (S033)
- "That tiny slice that does want to hear from you, that needs to hear from you, that would miss you if you don’t speak up–for that audience, you’re not noise." (S049)
- Sources: 4 (S002, S003, S033, S049). Confidence: high.

**13. A brand is a promise; make better ones and keep them.**
- "Your brand is a promise and your work is delivering on it." (S036)
- "making absurd promises might work at the VC level, but it doesn't work when you're talking to consumers because you can only break that promise one time." (S003)
- Sources: 4 (S003, S024, S036, S012). Confidence: high.

**14. Word of mouth is the most efficient growth engine, and it breaks in the moment you stop caring.**
- "Anyone who has done the math will tell you that word of mouth is the most efficient way to gain trust, spread the word and grow." (S026)
- Sources: 3 (S003, S026, S051). Confidence: high.

## Price and value

**15. Do not compete on cheap. Low price is a refuge, not a strategy.**
- "Cheap is the last refuge for the marketer who can't figure out how to be better." (S017)
- "Low price is the last refuge for marketers who don’t have the patience or guts to demonstrate value for those that need it." (S035)
- Sources: 5 (S015, S017, S019, S035, S053). Confidence: high.

**16. Race to the top: a dollar more, and worth it.**
- "A dollar more, and worth it." (S015)
- "The race to the top is not only more satisfying, it’s also more likely to work." (S053)
- Sources: 3 (S015, S043, S053). Confidence: high.

## Launch and growth

**17. A launch is a moment; culture changes through persistence.**
- "it turns out that culture is changed by persistence most of all." (S032)
- Sources: 3 (S032, S050, S058). Confidence: high.

**18. Buzz is not sales. Stunts borrow attention without building trust.**
- "Buzz does not sell hamburgers." (S003)
- "An effective ad is far more valuable than a much-noticed one." (S028)
- Sources: 2 (S003, S028). Confidence: medium.

**19. Algorithms and platforms are weather, not strategy.**
- "Traffic from an algorithm isn’t the point, it’s a random bonus." (S055)
- "Amazon is good at selling everything, but they’re terrible at selling a thing." (S041)
- Sources: 4 (S041, S047, S055, S059). Confidence: high.

## Persistence

**20. Persist through the Dip, quit the dead end, and set the rule in advance.**
- "Deciding how and when to quit before you begin is far easier and more effective than making ad hoc decisions under pressure." (S057)
- "But we also sell ourselves short (and harm the people we’d be able to serve) if we’re unable to quit a project that’s gone sideways." (S052)
- Sources: 4 (S013, S052, S057, S058). Confidence: high.

**21. Expect skeptics; nobody can guarantee the outcome, so ship your best work.**
- "If you wait until the market is telling you exactly what it wants, you’re almost certainly too late." (S058)
- "Everyone has an opinion, but no one has a guarantee." (S020)
- Sources: 3 (S009, S020, S058). Confidence: high.
