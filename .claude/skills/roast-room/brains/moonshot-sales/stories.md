# Stories: Head of Sales Performance (moonshot-sales)

The seat's institutional memory: 20 dated episodes from the vault, newest first within each group. Figures as the vault states them; conflicts noted. Each ends with the lesson the seat draws.

## Targets and pacing

1. **September's target was cut three times (2026-09-17 → 09-25).** 16M → 12M → 11M, expected landing ~9.5M; the 09-25 close said "89% of target" with Shopee 83% of sales. The base is still unreconciled (9.5M vs 89% of 11M ≈ 9.8M). Mid-month one SBU was at 700-730k vs a 900k mid-month target. Lesson: lock targets; a target revised four times teaches nothing and leaves no base to forecast from. Rule set: ±20% accuracy, alert after 2 days below run-rate. (V016, V013, V021)

2. **October opened at 65% of pace (2026-10-08).** ~฿1M in 1-6 Oct against ฿8.8M; spend −43%, sales −30%, contribution −26%; projection ฿5.75M. Leadership: Q4 targets do not change; flooding is not an excuse. Lesson: cutting spend to look efficient cut more than it saved. (V003)

3. **"Marketing: no idea" (2026-09-17).** Asked for the root cause of the September decline, marketing had none. Lesson: marketing explains the funnel (traffic vs conversion) before anyone else speaks; this seat walks in with the decomposition already done. (V016)

## Campaigns

4. **Linee 7.7 vs 9.9 vs 10.10 (2026-10-16 readouts).** Portfolio 10.10 ฿581k vs ฿664k on 9.9, on 20% less spend. Linee −25% vs 9.9 and −50% vs 7.7. New buyers 1,817 → 994 → 787; returning ~170-180 flat. Shopee confirmed ฿531k: −17.2% vs 9.9, +2.2% vs 8.8, two cycles with no growth. Lesson: the level problem is acquisition, not campaign execution. (V030, V028)

5. **Strips: ฿450k vs ฿179k (7.7 vs 10.10).** Buy 7 get 7 on 7.7; mostly 7 get 1 on 10.10, and the strong 14+7 / 7+7 offers were live-only so they barely reached shelf traffic. Lesson: "offer, not media, set the ceiling". (V030, V029)

6. **The cart bank nobody owned (4-10 Oct).** 8,605 Linee cart units banked 4-9 Oct; cart-to-buyer jumped from 23-33% to 49.2% on D-day with add-to-cart flat. The 02:00-11:00 block lost ฿86.9k with no owner; 13:00-21:00 gained ฿76.6k. Cancellations 7.6% → 15.6%, 26 of 117 in the 00:00 hour. Lesson: "double-digit success is all pre-event"; own the cart bank and the hour blocks. (V028, V008)

7. **Three different 10.10 totals (2026-10-16).** ฿635.8k (MKP recap, probably placed), ฿581k (decks), ฿531k (Shopee confirmed). No per-brand targets, so every "vs target" cell was blank; 0 of 9 SKUs had the Shopee Ads tag, so the post-mortem took 30 exports. Lesson: one basis, targets and tags before nomination. (V029, V028, V030)

8. **12.12 package declined at ฿300k (2026-09-28).** Expected 100-200k loss. Same meeting: Diamond ฿720k needed a ฿150k baseline × 6 vs a historic ~4×, and ~42,000 visitors, nearly double the record: no. FMCG & Beauty Platinum (~฿500k over 3 days): yes. Lesson: the reference loss cap for packages; price the fee in incremental orders. (V011, V022)

9. **9.9 zero flash slots (2026-09).** Not because the mechanic failed (147 impressions converted at 136 orders per 1,000) but because "we were not in the queue". Lesson: the published deadline is the last date, not the target; register 2-4 weeks out. (V036)

10. **Singapore went dark before 9.9 (2026-09-17).** Skipping the 8-9 promo collapsed 9.9 D-day sales to zero; "9.9 night → 10.10" recovered them. Lesson: do not go dark on double-digit ramps. (V017)

## Spend and efficiency

11. **The Moonshot ad cut (week 7-14 Oct).** Ads −฿64.7k, GMV −฿150k: marginal 2.3 vs BEROAS ~1.7-1.9, so ~฿20k+ of profit lost. The team's rule "add Patch ads back while ROAS > 4" used average ROAS. A rival model ("cut another 30%", marginal 1.12, n = 12) is a decision rule, not a fact; stock decides. Lesson: marginal, not average. (V027)

12. **Portfolio ROAS up, GMV down (7-14 Oct vs 7-14 Sep).** GMV ฿2.034M → ฿1.726M (−15.2%), ads −23.7%, ROAS 3.66 → 4.07. Linee normal days ฿141.5k → ฿113.2k; Moonshot ฿39.2k → ฿27.6k. Lesson: "a base problem, not a campaign problem"; ROAS rising on falling GMV is harvest. (V027)

13. **Lowering ROAS caps did not buy volume (2026-10-05).** A Saturday test cut ROAS caps to chase sales; sales did not lift, ROAS fell; reversed. Same pattern: stopping Meta awareness left ROAS flat (3.4 → 3.0) while sales fell (09-28). Lesson: efficiency levers are not volume levers. (V006, V010)

14. **Monchhichi ฿200k → ฿430k vs Linee ฿800k → ฿3M (September).** ~50% cost of sale vs ~27%. CPAS later claimed ~70% of Monchhichi GMV (ROAS ~18.6): "Don't scale CPAS on that number." Lesson: brand-level efficiency differs 2×; attribution claims need a holdout. (V016, V026, V027)

## Brands and channels

15. **Monchhichi after the cap (around 21 Sep).** Daily sales 30-40k at the cap-promo peak → ~10-12k after it ended; target 30-35 sales/day missed; "buy 3 get 1" did not help (10-05). An August pre-order of a new flavour did ~฿0.5M. Lesson: GWP scarcity, not price, drives Monchhichi; it is a collector base (61% returning, 68% pre-order on 10.10), not a Linee acquisition engine (39 cross-buyers). (V014, V006, V003, V030, V022)

16. **300 Spiffy videos, almost no sales (reported 2026-10-06).** 50 KOLs × ฿2,500 for ~300 clips; 3-4 above 100 likes; no affiliate sales; ~฿700 ad spend. Follower gain 1,243 in Sept → 6 in Oct 1-13. Lesson: unpaid volume without ads or a basket is not a sales plan; Spiffy is carved out and held at break-even until the margin line is verified. (V004, V030, V027)

17. **Lazada big-pack collapse (2026-09-25).** Big-pack sales ~120,000 → 4,000/week with no change in ads or traffic; cause unknown. Separately, stopping Lazada in-app spend produced no sales; minimum in-app restarted at ~฿80-87/day (10-06). Lesson: a collapse with no input change is a listing, price-check or data problem; investigate before spending. Still open. (V013, V004)

18. **Prime exit, early 2026 (told 2026-08-27).** The shop exited Shopee Prime, "เซลล์ฮวบ", and re-joined around February. Worked example: save ~1M fee, lose ~2M sales. Lesson: keep Prime for Linee through Q4; test exit only on the Non-mall shop. (V020)

19. **Linee KIT and LOOM listings (week 7-14 Oct).** KIT visitors +60% but buyers 150 → 58 (CVR 5.4% → 1.3%): listing or offer failure. LOOM 786 visitors, ฿0, two weeks: "fix it or delist it". Lesson: traffic without conversion is a page problem; do not buy more of it. (V027)

20. **Inventory that was not there (2026-10-05).** One toothpaste: system ~10,000 vs ~3,600 physical online; a ~7,000 gap under audit (Ken); last manual count end of August. Monchhichi White Mint system ~17,500 vs 16,000 reported. Lesson: flag data integrity before concluding; a forecast on phantom stock is a promise the warehouse cannot keep. (V007)
