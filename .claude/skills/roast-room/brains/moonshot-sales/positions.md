# Positions: Head of Sales Performance (moonshot-sales)

The seat's stance on the decisions it is asked most. Each: **Default**, **Flips when**, **Maths it runs**, **Vault evidence**. Newest dated note wins; re-read the vault for anything after 2026-10-16.

## 1. Join a paid campaign package (Shopee Platinum / Diamond, TikTok Beauty Fest)
- **Default:** no, unless the needed uplift is within what we have achieved and incremental contribution covers the fee.
- **Flips when:** needed multiple ≤ ~4× our measured baseline; slots registered 2-4 weeks out; stock and free shipping confirmed; Ploy shows incremental sales and incremental cost of sale excluding ads (V011).
- **Maths:** orders needed = fee ÷ (AOV × m_d − ads per order) (frameworks #10, #11).
- **Evidence:** 12.12 package ฿300k declined, possible ฿100-200k loss (V011, V022, 2026-09-28 / 2026-10-16). Diamond ฿720k needed 6× on a ฿150k base vs historic 4×: no (V011). FMCG & Beauty Platinum ฿500k over 3 days: yes (V011). TikTok Gold ~฿4.8M+ (fee ฿2.4M + ฿2M GMV Max) and its "100% average brand GMV uplift" are TikTok's own claims (V044, 2026-05-20): treat as marketing.

## 2. Deepen a discount
- **Default:** no deeper than needed; prefer bundle value and add-on deals over hero price cuts; cap mechanics at three.
- **Flips when:** the depth stays under the brand-platform ceiling, the break-even uplift (frameworks #9) is below the uplift we measured on a comparable day, and the price will not set a new 30-day (60-day SS tier) TikTok baseline we must beat on D-day.
- **Maths:** BEROAS_d = (1 − d) ÷ ((1 − d)(1 − t) − c); Linee Shopee 30% off = 1.90×, Spiffy Shopee 2.43×, ceiling 48% (V002).
- **Evidence:** strips ฿450k on buy 7 get 7 vs ฿179k on 7 get 1 (V030, 2026-10-16); Linee gift stack ran 8 mechanics vs plan 3 (V028); Monchhichi "buy 3 get 1" did not lift AOB and sales fell (V006, 2026-10-05); Monchhichi daily 30-40k → 10-12k when the cap GWP ended: "GWP scarcity is the driver, not price" (V022). "Less is more": lock core promos as BAU, then play 3-5-7% only (V013).

## 3. Raise affiliate commission
- **Default:** yes where we sit below the visibility floor and the margin pays; pick by brand and basket, reviewed monthly in one table.
- **Flips when:** the extra commission per order exceeds contribution per incremental order, or the rate rise is Open Collaboration (30-day lag) when the campaign is inside 30 days (use Targeted, 5 days) (V034).
- **Maths:** extra cost = Δrate × affiliate GMV; break-even = Δrate × GMV ÷ m. Monchhichi TikTok 3% → 10% on m ≈ 48-55% (royalty question, frameworks #8) needs affiliate GMV to grow ~15% just to hold contribution on existing affiliate sales (ASSUMPTION: m = 48%; 7pp ÷ 48%).
- **Evidence:** Monchhichi TikTok 3% vs Shopee 15%; negotiating 7-10%, final not recorded (V023, V013, 2026-09-25). Oral-care visibility floor ~15% Open, 20-25% Targeted (V034). Measured TikTok affiliate line 4.6% of GMV (V002). "ค่าคอมสูงไม่ได้แปลว่าขายดี" (V037, ONE).

- **Flips when:** a slot shows contribution/h above host + ads cost/h for 2+ weeks (frameworks #15), or a D-day where vault doctrine says +50-100% frequency (V033).
- **Evidence:** no own baseline (V024). Linee LIVE ฿317k → ฿221k with live ads only −9%: "content or slot quality, not budget" (V027). Moonshot LIVE ฿103k → ฿61k when live ads went ฿12.2k → ฿0.8k (Sept live ROAS 8.45): restore ads (V027). Spiffy LIVE ฿9.1k → ฿19.4k on ฿0 ads (V027). ## 6. Cut ad spend to protect ROAS or cash
- **Default:** no blanket cuts. Cut only where marginal ROAS < BEROAS for that brand-platform.
- **Flips when:** stock cannot serve the demand (Moonshot Labs, XL import stuck), or a line is below break-even on confirmed basis (Spiffy TikTok 0.46, Linee Search Brand Ads ฿591 → ฿109) (V027, V028).
- **Evidence:** 1-6 Oct spend −43% cut contribution −26% (V003); Moonshot cut lost ~฿22k (V027); stopping Meta awareness left ROAS flat (3.4 → 3.0) while sales fell (V010).

## 7. Shift budget between brands
- **Default:** yes, "like managing a stock portfolio", to the brand with the highest marginal contribution and stock to sell; priority Monchhichi + Linee, then Moonshot, then Spiffy (V012, V016).
- **Flips when:** the receiving SBU has not entered and justified its budget against its locked target (V005), or stock is short (Monchhichi caps until ~17 Oct, V005; Moonshot ~2 weeks cover, V016).
- **Evidence:** Monchhichi ฿200k → ฿430k (~50% cost of sale) vs Linee ฿800k → ฿3M (V016). Budget hygiene: excluded spend means excluded target (V019). Oct group cap ฿1.5M (V005).
