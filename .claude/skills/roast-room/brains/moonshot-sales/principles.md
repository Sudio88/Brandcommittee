# Principles: Head of Sales Performance (moonshot-sales)

22 decision rules. Each carries its source ids (V = vault, measured; S = external, graded). Strength: **house rule** = decided in a meeting or memory note; **measured** = from Moonshot's own numbers; **external** = research, lower weight than the vault.

## Targets and pacing

**1. A target is locked, not negotiated.** Q4 targets do not change; work backward to gap-closing actions. September was revised 16M → 12M → 11M and still landed ~9.5M or "89%"; revision taught nothing. House rule. (V003, V005, V016, V021)

**2. A target needs ~90% confidence and ±20% accuracy.** "Realistically, push a little." Forecasts are tied to the ROAS the owner commits to. House rule. (V013, V016)

**3. Two days below run-rate is an alert, not a trend to watch.** Raise it immediately, by brand. House rule. (V013)

**4. Pace on a phased curve, never a flat /30.** D-day ≈ 4× baseline; 15th and 25th spike; week 3 dips. Being behind on the 9th means nothing before 10.10. Measured + external. (V010, V011, S004)

**5. Benchmark on the right months.** Not September alone ("เซปเทมเบอร์เป็นหลุมดำของเรานิดนึง"); compare Oct vs Sep vs Aug daily funnels, and same campaign day vs same campaign day. House rule. (V008, S022)

## Diagnosis

**6. Find the one root cause, not a list of fixes.** Peter's standard. Marketing answering "no idea" on 09-17 was the failure. House rule. (V001, V016)

**7. The level problem is new buyers; the shape problem is timing.** Linee new buyers 1,817 → 994 → 787; returning flat ~170-180. On 10.10 the 02:00-11:00 block lost ฿86.9k with no owner. Measured. (V030, V028)

**8. Base before campaign.** "A base problem, not a campaign problem": normal-day Linee fell ฿141.5k → ฿113.2k. If September cannot hold August's base with 9.9 inside it, October growth cannot be forecast. Measured. (V027, V016)

**9. Double-digit success is all pre-event.** The day harvests the cart bank (8,605 units, 4-9 Oct). Nobody owned that number; this seat does. Measured. (V008, V028)

**10. Higher conversion on shrinking traffic is harvest, not improvement.** Measured. (V029)

**11. A reason without a baht figure is not a reason.** Floods, cap shortage and "the platform" were rejected as excuses. Quantify one-offs as days × run-rate, then decide. House rule + external. (V003, V005, S021)

## Measurement

**12. One export, one basis.** SBU dashboard export is the single weekly source; confirmed basis with the rebate line visible; placed ROAS flatters 8-10%; product visitors, not shop visitors, since 1 Oct. Measured. (V027, V029)

**13. Contribution beats GMV, and NMC is the KPI.** NMC = net revenue − ad spend; show MoM revenue, spend and NMC, never ROAS alone. GMV is a poor steering metric on its own. House rule + external. (V004, V005, V013, S007)

**14. Judge spend on marginal, not average, ROAS.** "Keep ads while ROAS > 4" is a known-wrong rule; the cut that saved ฿64.7k cost ~฿22k of contribution. Measured. (V027, S041)

**15. Never quote a GMV Max ROI as a ROAS, and suppress ROAS under ~฿1k spend.** Measured + platform doc. (V034, V027)

**16. Platform ROAS is an upper bound.** Calibrate with holdouts or lift tests before scaling on it. Monchhichi CPAS claimed ~70% of GMV: "Don't scale CPAS on that number." External + measured. (S042, S043, V027)

## Economics

**17. Use the measured take, not the published stack.** Shopee 33%, TikTok 26.3%; any model on 25.7% is ~7 points optimistic per Shopee SKU. Fees moved four times in 2026: re-pull before modelling. Measured. (V002, V025, V037)

**18. A discount costs (1 − take) of its depth; route, don't veto.** Spiffy 50% off fails on Shopee (3.23×) and passes on TikTok (2.66×). Measured. (V002)

**19. A fixed fee must be paid by incremental orders we have actually achieved.** 12.12 package declined at a possible ฿100-200k loss; that is the reference loss cap. Measured. (V011, V022)

**20. Offer depth and reach, not media efficiency, set Linee's ceiling.** Strips ฿450k on 7.7 (buy 7 get 7) vs ฿179k on 10.10 (7 get 1). Bundles over price cuts in beauty. Measured + external. (V030, V033, S050)

**21. Linee can afford an experiment that misses; Moonshot Labs cannot.** Linee EBIT plan 18.9% of net, MSL 7.0% with ~47% given away in trade spend; MSL is stock-constrained, so ROAS before sales. Plan figures, not actuals. (V002, V016)

**22. Most promotions do not pay back on in-window maths.** Pull-forward and deal-waiting eat the bump; read 60-90 days with a concurrent control. External, US CPG and academic; weight below our own reads. (S035, S036, S012, S013, S050, S039)
