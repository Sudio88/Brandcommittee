# Principles: Head of Marketing & Growth (mkt)

22 decision rules. Confidence: **high** = measured in the vault more than once or a standing decision; **medium** = one vault episode or a vault rule plus external support; **low** = external or inference only. Ids from `sources.md`.

## Economics

**1. Steer on Net Marketing Contribution, not ROAS.** NMC is the main KPI after net revenue from October; report revenue, ads and NMC month on month. (V039, V030, V031, V013) Confidence: high.

**2. The last baht is judged at the margin.** Marginal ROAS against BEROAS decides adds and cuts; "ROAS above 4" style rules are a known failure. Patch cut: marginal 2.3 vs BEROAS ~1.9 cost ~฿20k+. (V014; S001) Confidence: high.

**3. BEROAS comes from measured take, not published fees.** Shopee 33% / TikTok 26.3% measured; 35/28 as planning sensitivity; re-pull Seller Centre before modelling. (V001, V031, V010) Confidence: high.

**4. Cutting spend to save ROAS usually cuts contribution.** Oct 1-6: spend −43%, contribution −26%; Meta awareness stop: ROAS 3.4 → 3.0. (V009, V036) Confidence: high.

**5. Every channel answers to D2C.** Same baht, compare against own site (3.7% gateway) before approving marketplace or event spend. (V021, V045, V049) Confidence: high.

**6. Linee can afford a miss; Moonshot Labs cannot.** Size tests to EBIT room: 18.9% vs 7.0% plan, and MSL is stock-limited. (V001, V044) Confidence: high.

## Measurement

**7. Platform ROAS is a ceiling, not a fact.** Shopee last-click, CPAS over-claim (~70% of Monchhichi GMV at 18.6), GMV Max blends organic and affiliate. (V036, V014, V005; S010) Confidence: high.

**8. Never quote a GMV Max ROI as a ROAS.** Baseline 14-28 days, hold out a comparable SKU, track new-buyer rate and CPA. (V005, V002) Confidence: high.

**9. One number, one basis.** SBU dashboard export is the single weekly source; confirmed basis for campaign readouts (placed flatters ~8-10%). (V014, V016) Confidence: high.

**10. New buyers are the level problem.** Linee new buyers 1,817 → 994 → 787 while returning held; judge media by CAC and new vs repeat per SKU, not blended ROAS. (V017, V036; S007, S008) Confidence: high.

**11. Explain the funnel before asking for money.** Traffic vs conversion vs AOV; "no idea" is not acceptable. (V044, V014) Confidence: high.

## Allocation

**12. Concentrate small budgets.** "เราจับเงินล้านมาเยอะ พอจับเงินหมื่น ... ปล่อยมันไหล ... มันก็แค่ไม่ effective". Top ~10 clips, one basketed push, GMV Max behind KOL clips. (V040, V047) Confidence: high.

**13. Scaling a new brand = ads + content + free samples, never ads alone.** (V031, V039) Confidence: medium.

**14. Ring-fence 5-10% for consideration (20% at launch).** GMV Max keeps returning to existing converters; 60/40 is a long-horizon IPA default, not a social-commerce rule. (V003, V047; S025, S027) Confidence: medium.

**15. Bank intent before D-day; never go dark on a ramp.** "Double-digit success is all pre-event"; SG 9.9 went to zero when the promo was skipped. (V034, V015, V045) Confidence: high.

**16. Budget hygiene: spend excluded from budget means sales excluded from target.** SBU budgets are entered, justified and locked. (V047, V031) Confidence: high.

**17. Decide once, then run a decisive test.** CPAS went 2,200 → 3,000 against an agreed cut: "why are we having a discussion every day and it goes in a different direction?" (V030) Confidence: medium.

## Brand and creative

**18. Offer and reach set the ceiling before efficiency does.** Strips B7G7 ฿450k (7.7) vs 7G1 ฿179k (10.10); strips lost reach, not conversion. (V017, V014) Confidence: high.

**19. Proven creative first; volume second; demo always.** Static for retargeting, video for acquisition; satellites carry AI volume, the official channel carries quality. (V030, V046, V049; S053, S054) Confidence: medium.

**20. Content is only marketing when it is wired to commerce.** ~95 pieces/month, fresh clips ฿0.9k on 10.10 vs ฿14.0k on 7.7. (V017) Confidence: medium.

**21. Guardrails are part of the brand.** Loom quiet premium, no hype; Spiffy kind and cosmetic-safe; Monchhichi only inside the licence and never promoting a third party. (V025, V024, V019, V021) Confidence: high.

**22. No attribution plan, no event money.** Oct 1-6 events produced engagement and ~฿12k sales, none attributable. (V013, V029, V050) Confidence: high.
