# Stories: Head of Marketing & Growth (mkt)

Institutional memory from the vault, newest first within each group. Each episode: what happened, the lesson, the citation. Figures as the vault states them.

## Spend and efficiency

1. **10.10 on less money (10 Oct 2026).** Portfolio ฿581k vs ฿664k on 9.9 (−12%) on 20% less ad spend; Linee −25% vs 9.9 and −50% vs 7.7. New buyers 1,817 → 994 → 787 across 7.7 / 9.9 / 10.10; returning flat ~170-180. Lesson: the level problem is acquisition; efficiency looked fine while the base shrank. (V017, 2026-10-16)

2. **Three 10.10 totals (16 Oct 2026).** ฿635.8k (MKP recap, placed), ฿581k (decks), ฿531k (Shopee confirmed). Lesson: pick one basis before arguing; placed basis flatters ROAS ~8-10%. (V016, V015, 2026-10-16)

3. **The Patch ad cut (7-14 Oct 2026).** Moonshot ads −฿64.7k, GMV −฿150k, marginal ~2.3 vs BEROAS ~1.7-1.9: ~฿20k+ profit lost. The team rule "add back while ROAS stays above 4" was the wrong test. Lesson: marginal, not average. (V014, 2026-10-16)

4. **Spend down 43%, contribution down 26% (1-6 Oct 2026).** October opened at ~65% of pace; run-rate ฿5.75M vs ฿8.8M target. Lesson: cutting spend cut sales by more than it saved. (V009, V029, 2026-10-08)

5. **The CPAS argument (6 Oct 2026).** CPAS was agreed at −10%/day on 25 Sep, yet spend rose 2,200 → 3,000; Monchhichi had separately shifted 1,000/day into C-pass on 30 Sep. Leadership: "why are we having a discussion every day and it goes in a different direction?" Lesson: one decision per brand, written down, then a decisive test. (V030, V039, V035)

6. **Lowering the ROAS cap (Saturday before 5 Oct 2026).** Caps lowered to chase volume; ROAS fell, sales did not rise; reversed. Lesson: the cap was not the constraint. (V032, 2026-10-05)

7. **Meta awareness switched off on Shopee (late Sep 2026).** ROAS ~3.4 before vs ~3.0 after: spend fell, sales fell, efficiency flat. Lesson: awareness was doing work the last-click report could not see. (V036, 2026-09-28)

8. **Monchhichi vs Linee (Sept 2026).** Monchhichi ฿200k → ฿430k (~50% cost of sale) vs Linee ฿800k → ฿3M. Question raised: would the same ฿200k do better on D2C with no 35% commission? On measured margin Monchhichi was roughly break-even (framework 3). Lesson: put every SBU next to D2C. (V044, 2026-09-17)

9. **Monchhichi CPAS "18.6 ROAS" (7-14 Oct 2026).** CPAS claimed ~70% of Monchhichi GMV. Lesson: "Don't scale CPAS on that number." (V014, 2026-10-16)

10. **Linee GMV Max Auto 7.04 never scaled; Search Brand Ads ฿591 → ฿109 (10.10).** The best line got ฿7.1k; the worst kept running. Lesson: read every line weekly and move money to the winner. (V015, 2026-10-16)

## Targets and planning

11. **September target 16M → 12M → 11M (Sept 2026).** Cut repeatedly; landing ~9.5M (09-17) vs "89% of target" (09-25), still unreconciled. Marketing answered "no idea" on root cause. Outcomes: targets accurate to ±20%, alert after 2 days below run-rate, lock Q4 targets. Lesson: lock, then work backwards to the gap. (V044, V039, V009)

12. **"1.5M × ROAS 5 = 8.5M" (5 Oct 2026).** The October media line does not compute (7.5M). Lesson: check the arithmetic before it becomes a target. (V031, V013)

## Campaigns and packages

13. **12.12 Platinum declined at ~฿300k (28 Sep 2026).** Possible ฿100-200k loss; FMCG & Beauty Platinum joined instead (expected ~฿500k over 3 days). Diamond's ฿720k would need 6× uplift on a ฿150k baseline vs ~4× historic and ~42,000 visitors, nearly double the record. Lesson: packages are tests with a loss cap. (V037, V008)

14. **Strips: buy 7 get 7 vs 7 get 1 (7.7 vs 10.10).** ฿450k vs ฿179k; the strong 14+7 and 7+7 offers were live-only on 10.10. Lesson: offer and reach, not media efficiency, set the ceiling. (V017, V016)

15. **Cart bank harvested (4-10 Oct 2026).** 8,605 Linee cart units banked; cart-to-buyer 49.2% on D-day; nobody owned the number. Lesson: D-day only harvests what was banked. (V015)

16. **9.9 GMV Max "success" (Sep 2026).** ROAS 4.01× and rising while incremental margin fell 48% → 43% and contribution per new buyer ฿194 → ฿137. Lesson: never quote GMV Max ROI as ROAS. (V002, V005)

17. **Singapore went dark (Sep 2026).** Skipping the promo on 8-9 Sep took 9.9 D-day to zero; "9.9 night → 10.10" recovered it. Lesson: never go dark on a ramp. (V045, 2026-09-17)

## Content, creators and brand

18. **300 Spiffy videos, almost no sales (Sept-Oct 2026).** ~300 TikTok videos from 50 KOLs at ~฿500/clip; 3-4 above 100 likes; no affiliate sales; ~฿700 ad spend. "Videos that go viral generally had some paid ad input." Followers 1,243 in Sept → 6 in 1-13 Oct when KOL support stopped. Lesson: volume without paid support and a basket is not marketing. (V030, V017, V047)

19. **Monchhichi celebrity seeding (Sept 2026).** ~10-20% posted, zero conversion. Lesson: content supply is not the constraint; traffic and post-rate control are. (V044, V011)

20. **Fresh clips, no sales (10.10).** ~95 pieces/month, yet fresh TikTok clips sold ฿0.9k vs ฿14.0k on 7.7; hero GMV Max clips 4-7 months old. Lesson: the content engine and the commerce engine are disconnected. (V017)

21. **Cross-sell that did not happen (7.7 → 10.10).** Linee × Monchhichi mixed orders 120 → 15; Moonshot × Spiffy 32 → 3; only 39 Monchhichi buyers had bought Linee. Lesson: adding a brand to a shop is not building baskets; Monchhichi sells to fans and collectors. (V017)

22. **Monchhichi cap GWP (Sept 2026).** 30-40k/day with the 3D cap, ~10-12k/day after it ended (~21 Sep); top seller on payday with the cap. Lesson: GWP scarcity, not price, drives Monchhichi; budget media around drops. (V040, V032, V036, V008)

23. **Loom: 786 visitors, ฿0 (Sep-Oct 2026).** Ads at ฿600-700/day ran ROAS ~1; in-app ROAS looked high only because shoppers bought other Linee products; lifestyle content did not convert. Lesson: fix the listing or delist before buying traffic. (V014, V040, V039)

## Events, offline, channels

24. **Events with no receipts (1-6 Oct 2026).** IG ~7% engagement, trending hashtag, ~฿12k sales, ~100k organic live views, ~200 link clicks, none attributable to purchases. Wave / Tops of Beauty: ~9.9M potential X reach, ~168 tracked TikTok clicks. The 25 Sep "Offline + Event Activation" note is empty. Lesson: UTM/code before money. (V029, V017, V050)

25. **Lazada big-pack collapse (Sept 2026).** Big-pack sales fell from ~120,000 to ~4,000/week with no change in ads or traffic; cause unknown. Separately, stopping Lazada in-app produced no sales and it was restarted at ~฿80-87/day. Lesson: when sales move without spend moving, look at listing, price and competitors before media. (V039, V030)

26. **Prime exit "เซลล์ฮวบ" (early 2026).** An early-year exit from Shopee Prime crashed sales; re-joined around February. Lesson: programme fees can be the cheapest traffic; test exits on the Non-mall shop. (V048, 2026-08-27)

27. **TikTok main account lost (Sep-Oct 2026).** Main account unavailable; marketing accounts opened one SIM at a time; new account must wait 14 days before going live; TikTok spend fell 1,300 → 400/day with near-zero sales. Lesson: platform risk is a marketing risk; keep satellites and follow re-run rules. (V029, V035, V039)
