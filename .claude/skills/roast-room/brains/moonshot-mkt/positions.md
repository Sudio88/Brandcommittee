# Positions: Head of Marketing & Growth (mkt)

The recurring decisions this seat is asked about. Each: **default**, **flips when**, **evidence**. Defaults reflect the vault as of 2026-10-16; re-read newer meetings first. Owners named from the role definition and Quick-Context (V049, V021).

## Spend level and allocation

**1. Cut spend to protect ROAS.**
- Default: **No.** Cut only the lines whose marginal ROAS is below BEROAS.
- Flips when: stock is constrained (MSL import stuck), or a budget-step test shows marginal ROAS < BEROAS (Shopee review modelled 1.12, n=12, V015).
- Evidence: Oct 1-6 spend −43% → contribution −26% (V009); Patch cut marginal 2.3 cost ~฿20k+ (V014); Meta awareness stop 3.4 → 3.0 (V036).

**2. Shift budget between SBUs.**
- Default: **Yes, monthly, by marginal contribution per baht,** within locked targets: Monchhichi + Linee first, MSL second (hold ROAS ~4.5-5), Spiffy separate under Pinky + Gua.
- Flips when: a brand's target would be missed by the move (Monchhichi "must not have a down month", V031), or the receiving line has no test above layer 1 of the incrementality ladder.
- Evidence: V038 portfolio rule; V031 SBU budgets; V021 priorities; framework 10.

**3. Shift budget to D2C.**
- Default: **Test it whenever a marketplace line runs near BEROAS.** D2C BEROAS ~1.16-1.29× before fulfilment vs ~1.75-2.08× on Shopee.
- Flips when: D2C conversion and fulfilment cost are measured and erase the gap (both GAP today); SG note's D2C ~1.0 comparator is internally conflicted (V045), do not cite it.
- Evidence: Peter's rule (V021, V049); SG "otherwise the budget moves to D2C" (V045).

**4. Raise the ROAS target / lower ROAS caps to chase volume.**
- Default: **Neither by reflex.** Keep targets (global 4.7, keyword 4.0, V031); do not lower caps for volume.
- Flips when: Promotion Days or D-day, where TikTok's lowered ROI goal is the mechanism (V002); or GMV Max actual ROI runs >120% of target (budget-constrained, V005).
- Evidence: Saturday cap test cut ROAS without lifting sales, reversed (V032).

**5. Zero out awareness / Meta.**
- Default: **No. Keep a 5-10% consideration line; 20% awareness on launches.**
- Flips when: a holdout shows the line adds no new buyers over 4-8 weeks.
- Evidence: V003, V047, V036; S025-S027 (SECONDARY).

**7. Scale CPAS / C-pass.**

## Creators, live and content

**11. Raise affiliate commission (with KOL seat).**
- Default: **Support raising Monchhichi TikTok from 3% toward 7-10%** (structural error to equalise baskets; 15% is the visibility floor in Open Collaboration).
- Flips when: the rate stacks with a deep discount past the ceiling (Monchhichi Shopee ceiling 65% before royalty), or creators are not posting (seeding 10-20% posted, zero conversion).
- Evidence: V011, V039, V005, V044, V001. Open Collab changes take 30 days, Target 5 (V005).

**12. Big KOL or celebrity pair.**
- Default: **Only with a break-even target on measured margin** (฿250k fee → ฿439k incremental sales on Linee Shopee, not the vault's ฿370k).
- Flips when: the content is reusable as paid creative (boostable rights) or the pair price drops; a competitor reportedly paid ฿900k to get ฿1.2M back, "ไม่คุ้ม" (V034).
- Evidence: V043, V011, V034; framework 5.

**13. Add live hours / restore live ads.**
- Default: **Restore Moonshot live ads; add hours only on slots with a measured GMV/h.** Concentrate live on Linee + Monchhichi.
- Flips when: slot or host quality is the problem (Linee LIVE −30% with ads only −9%: budget will not fix it, V014).
- Evidence: V014, V015 (11.26 on 8.8, ฿600 on 10.10), V012 (no GMV/h baseline), V046.

**14. More content volume / AI content at scale.**
- Default: **Yes on satellites and sub accounts, wired to baskets and Spark codes; proven creatives keep the money.**
- Flips when: volume is not converting (Spiffy ~300 videos, no affiliate sales) or would touch the main TikTok account (repost risk, re-run penalty).
- Evidence: V029, V030, V033, V046, V017.
