# Moonshot's council must fix acquisition, not efficiency

Moonshot Studio's six council seats are advising a business with a shrinking new-buyer base, not a campaign or ad-efficiency problem. **Linee's new buyers fell from 1,817 to 994 to 787 across 7.7, 9.9 and 10.10, while returning buyers held flat at about 170–180.** Normal-day sales also fell (Linee ฿141.5k → ฿113.2k per day; Moonshot Labs ฿39.2k → ฿27.6k). The vault's own verdict is "a base problem, not a campaign problem". To advise at expert level, each seat needs three layers of knowledge. The first is the vault's measured economics: Shopee takes 33% and TikTok 26.3% on Moonshot's statements, and Spiffy on Shopee has a 48% discount ceiling. The second is the 2024–2026 platform rules, which changed four or more times in 2026. The third is the general evidence on promotions, creators, live and measurement. Two outside facts matter most. Shopee carried **83% of September sales and 90.3% of 10.10 GMV**, yet TikTok Shop already outsold Shopee in Thai skincare in Q4 2025. And more than half of everything a seat might want to benchmark has no credible published Thai figure: double-day share of GMV, live share of GMV, return and cancellation rates, creator activation, host commissions, and multi-account rules. Expertise here therefore means knowing which numbers Moonshot must measure itself. It also means reconciling the vault's conflicting figures before any of them are used. The most urgent gaps are regulatory, not commercial. The Monchhichi licence's channel schedule may not cover Shopee, Lazada or Tops. And the vault's peroxide percentages for Linee and Monchhichi sit far above the ASEAN 0.1% consumer limit for oral products, a limit whose Thai in-force status is unconfirmed.

## Every seat starts from one shared, contested baseline

All six seats need the same few facts, and most of those facts arrive in more than one version. Expertise starts with knowing which version to use.

**Channel concentration.** Shopee is the company. It carried 83% of September sales, and the sales memory states plainly that "any Shopee issue is a company-level issue" (`[[moonshot-sales/MEMORY]]`, 2026-10-16). Shopee also carried 90.3% of combined 10.10 GMV (`[[02-Areas/Marketing-Brand/2026-10-16-10.10-MKP-Recap-Reconciliation]]`, 2026-10-16). The TikTok main account is unavailable. Replacement marketing accounts were still waiting on phone verification in early October, and a new account must wait **14 days before going live** (`[[10-Meetings/2026-10-08 Business Review (weekly)]]`). The May 2026 vault said "Primary channel TikTok Shop TH", and some brand notes still say so. That is stale.

**The market is moving the other way.** Thailand was Southeast Asia's fastest-growing platform e-commerce market in 2025, up **51.8%** ([Momentum Works](https://thelowdown.momentum.asia/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Embargoed-Press-release-Momentum-Works-Ecommerce-in-SEA-2026.pdf)). Cube data reported by the Bangkok Post put H1 2025 shares at Shopee 48%, TikTok Shop 33% and Lazada 18% ([Chiang Rai Times summary](https://www.chiangraitimes.com/business/thailand-e-commerce-sites/)). In skincare, TikTok Shop sold about **THB 4.7B in Q4 2025 against Shopee's THB 3.9B**, and nearly half of TikTok's skincare sales came through non-official stores ([Cube](https://cube.asia/why-the-same-skincare-brand-strategy-wont-work-across-shopee-lazada-and-tiktok-shop-in-thailand/)). Priceza expects overall Thai e-commerce growth to slow to about 7% in 2026 ([Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/2944837/priceza-highlights-latest-e-commerce-trends)). A slowing market means growth comes from taking share between platforms. A brand that is 83–90% Shopee is therefore under-exposed to the platform where its category is growing. This is the strategic context behind the "TikTok recovery" priority, and every seat should hold it.

**Two versions of the September result.** The September target was cut four times, 16M → 12M → 11M. One meeting says it landed at about 9.5M; another says "89% of target". Quick-Context lists this as unreconciled. October's target is 8.8M. Shopee also changed its shop-visitor metric on 1 October, so cross-month traffic trends must use product visitors (`[[...-Weekly-MKT-7-14Oct-Insight]]`).

## Sales performance must find the baseline before judging pace

The sales seat's job, as leadership defines it, is investor-grade. Targets are locked and not revised. Each gap gets one root cause, not a list of fixes. Break-even comes from COGS, trade spend and fees. CAC is reported every week (`[[_Hub/Quick-Context]]`, 2026-10-16). The rules in force are concrete. Targets must be accurate to **±20% in both directions**. The team must raise an alert after **two consecutive days below run-rate**. A forecast needs about 90% confidence. September is not to be used as a benchmark alone ("เซปเทมเบอร์เป็นหลุมดำของเรานิดนึง"). Instead, compare daily funnel metrics for October against September and August.

**What the vault's evidence diagnoses.** The data points to acquisition, not campaigns. Linee's cart bank of 8,605 units built over 4–9 October was harvested on D-day. Cart-to-buyer conversion rose to 49.2% on 10.10 while the add-to-cart rate stayed flat, so "the day only harvested intent banked beforehand" (`[[...-10.10-Shopee-Review-Reconciliation]]`). The vault therefore reads higher conversion on shrinking traffic as "a warning sign, not a win". Shopee-only confirmed 10.10 sales were down 17.2% against 9.9 and up only 2.2% against 8.8: two campaign cycles with no growth. Timing also matters. On 10.10, the 02:00–11:00 block lost ฿86.9k with no owner. Linee cancellations doubled from 7.6% to 15.6%, with 26 of 117 cancels in the midnight hour.

The Shopee daily baseline for the Linee shop is about ฿100k in August and ฿120k in September. 9.9 ran at about 4× baseline, and the real spikes fall on the 15th and 25th (`[[10-Meetings/2026-09-28 Campaign confirmation]]`). The monthly shape repeats: sales dip in week 3 after the double-digit day and recover in week 4.

**What external research adds.** The best method is to combine a D2C contribution stack (CM1 → CM2 → CM3) with the traffic × conversion × AOV identity, run per brand, then channel, then SKU. Common Thread Collective's practice turns the monthly contribution goal into daily expectations for revenue, spend and contribution ([CTC](https://commonthreadco.com/blogs/ecommerce-playbook/execute-every-single-day)). No citable Thai figure exists for the share of a seller's GMV that lands on double-days or payday. Platforms publish only uplift multiples, such as Lazada's "+469% vs normal days" for 11.11 2025 ([Lazada](https://lazada-com.oss-ap-southeast-1.aliyuncs.com/229-press-release.pdf)). The pacing curve must therefore be built from Moonshot's own last two or three equivalent months, not from a flat target ÷ 30.

Promotions shift demand in time. Pre- and post-periods "can actually swamp the total effect of the sale" ([Recast](https://getrecast.com/sales-promotions/)). The hangover test is to compare the 7–14 days after a double-day with the same days in a non-campaign month.

**Definitions differ across platforms.** TikTok defines GMV at payment time and includes cancelled and refunded orders ([TikTok Seller University](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=813364865828654)). Shopee Ads offers separate placed and confirmed bases ([Shopee Ads MY](https://ads.shopee.com.my/learn/faq/82/2127)). The seat should insist on a reconciliation ladder per channel: placed → confirmed → net of cancels, returns and seller vouchers → net sales → CM3.

**Running the weekly review.** The Amazon WBR model fits leadership's style: a fixed-format deck that spends meeting time on exceptions in controllable input metrics ([Holistics](https://www.holistics.io/blog/how-amazon-uses-input-metrics/)). The Control Tower already defines locked thresholds for this, such as RED at GMV ≤−30% against the same day last week, or a pace gap above 25% with fewer than 10 days left (`[[01-Projects/Control-Tower/README]]`). The missing pieces are an event log and a new-versus-returning buyer split, which together separate one-offs (floods, stock-outs, the lost TikTok account) from trend.

## Pricing and promotion is a margin-ceiling and routing problem

This seat needs the margin note memorised. Margin before advertising = 1 − platform take − COGS. A discount costs **(1 − take) of its depth**, so a 30% cut on Shopee costs about 20 margin points, not 30.

| Brand · platform | Margin | Ceiling | Break-even ROAS @30% off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Linee · Shopee | 57% | 71% | 1.90× |
| Moonshot Labs · Shopee | 59% | 77% | 1.80× |
| Monchhichi · Shopee | 55% | 65% | 2.01× |
| **Spiffy · Shopee** | 49% | **48%** | 2.43× |
| Spiffy · TikTok | 56% | 60% | 2.09× |

Source: `[[03-Resources/Marketplace-Intelligence/Margin-and-Discount-Ceilings]]` (2026-09-19).

A 50%-off Spiffy promotion needs a 3.23× marginal ROAS on Shopee, above the healthy 2.5–3.1× band. On TikTok it needs 2.66×, inside that band. The note calls this "a routing decision, not a veto". The seat should also know three limits of the note itself:

- None of the COGS ratios has been checked against a costed bill of materials.
- Moonshot Labs' 42.5% seller-promotion line is unverified.
- Moonshot Labs' plan EBIT is only **7.0% against Linee's 18.9%**: "Linee can afford an experiment that misses. Moonshot Labs cannot."

**Offer design drove the result more than media did.** On 7.7, buy-7-get-7 strips sold ฿450k. On 10.10, mostly 7-get-1 strips sold ฿179k: "offer, not media, set the ceiling" (`[[...-Weekly-Insight-10.10-and-Content]]`). The strongest 10.10 offers were live-only, so they barely reached shelf traffic. The Linee gift stack ran eight mechanics against a plan of three. Monchhichi daily sales fell from 30–40k to about 10k when the cap gift-with-purchase (GWP) ended: "GWP scarcity is the driver, not price". Buy-3-get-1 then failed to lift basket size. The Shopee 12.12 Platinum package (฿300k) was declined over a possible ฿100–200k loss, and that loss now serves as the reference cap for packages.

**External evidence explains why deep stacked deals rarely pay.** Nielsen's analysis of 39 million promotional events found almost three-quarters of promotions do not break even ([FoodNavigator-USA](https://www.foodnavigator-usa.com/Article/2014/10/09/Three-quarters-of-CPG-promotions-don-t-break-even-Nielsen)). The meta-analytic short-term promotion elasticity is about −3.6 ([Oxford Handbook of Pricing Management](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/28115/chapter/212276183)). By that elasticity, a 20% cut predicts about +72% units. If the stacked promotion halves contribution per order, it needs +100% just to stand still, before netting out pull-forward. Long-run promotion exposure trains households to "lie in wait" for deals ([Mela, Jedidi and Bowman](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/pubfiles/3048/jedidi.pdf)).

**Price-history rules bind the calendar.** TikTok TH seller flash sales must beat the lowest price of the last **14 days** ([TikTok Seller University TH](https://seller-th.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6837804139349762&lang=en)). The vault's doctrine, drawn from US documentation, uses 30 days and 60 for SS tiers. That conflict is unresolved, so the seat should plan to the stricter window until Seller Centre confirms. Either way, every mid-month flash price becomes the floor the next double-day must beat. Bundles, add-on deals and GWP are the levers that avoid resetting that floor. The vault adds a Thai consumer signal: buyers publicly accuse brands of inflating prices before mega days (*"11.11 … มันถูกจริงๆ หรือแค่วันลวงโลก"*).

**Licence and tax constraints.** Monchhichi royalty is charged on the actual selling price. Discounts therefore shrink the royalty, but platform fees do not reduce the royalty base (`[[01-Projects/Monchhichi-Linee-Collab/README]]`). The vault also records that the 7% VAT rate ran only to 30 September 2026 under Royal Decree No. 799, with 10% as the default, and it tells modellers to allow for about a 2.8% rise in recommended selling price. Nothing in the notes confirms whether that rate change took effect, so the seat must verify it before pricing November.

The seat should run every promotion with a pre-registration card. The card states the expected contribution per order, the break-even multiple, a THB loss cap, a read date and a concurrent control. Period-over-period comparisons "systematically overstate promotional value" ([Talon.One](https://www.talon.one/blog/measure-promotion-roi)).

## KOL and creator spend is under-priced on TikTok and unmeasured everywhere

**Measured economics.** The affiliate creator budget is ฿2M, and the KOL lead's own envelope is ฿300k committed against ฿2M in sales. A big KOL breaks even at ฿370k sales on a ฿250k fee (`[[moonshot-kol/MEMORY]]`).

**The results are poor.**

- Spiffy's 50-KOL programme produced about 300 TikTok videos. Only 3–4 passed 100 likes, and there were no affiliate sales.
- Spiffy TikTok follower growth collapsed from 1,243 in September to 6 in 1–13 October once KOL support stopped.
- Monchhichi celebrity seeding got 10–20% of creators to post, with zero conversion.
- A fan push with about 9.9M potential reach on X produced about 168 tracked TikTok clicks.
- Only 39 Monchhichi buyers had bought Linee before, and mixed orders fell from 120 to 15. Monchhichi sells to collectors; it is not acting as an acquisition engine for Linee.

Sources: `[[...-Weekly-Insight-10.10-and-Content]]`; `[[10-Meetings/2026-10-06 October Growth Plan…]]`.

**The commission structure is the most fixable error.** Monchhichi pays **3% affiliate commission on TikTok against 15% on Shopee**. The team itself calls equal rates across basket types "a structural error". A move to 7–10% was being negotiated, but no final rate is recorded. The vault's own doctrine says about **15% is the floor** for visibility in TikTok Open Collaboration, and 20–25% Targeted with samples is what gets a mid-tier creator to build a funnel (`[[TikTok-Shop-GMV-Max]]`). Moonshot's measured TikTok affiliate line is 4.6% of GMV. The vault never connects these two facts, but together they explain weak organic creator pull.

**Commission changes run on fixed timelines.** Increases apply instantly. Decreases leave existing creators on the old rate for 30 days ([TikTok Seller University](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6077860360177451&default_language=en)). The vault adds that Open Collaboration changes take about 30 days to go live, while Target Collaboration changes take 5 days. On 16 October, any Open-rate fix for 11.11 is already late; Target deals are the only lever still in time.

**GMV Max now controls creator-led sales.** Since July 2025, GMV Max has been the only Sales-objective format for TikTok Shop ads. It automatically pulls in affiliate videos that creators have mass-authorised ([TikTok Ads Help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-affiliate-creatives-for-tiktok-shop-ads?lang=en); [SEA Guidebook](https://seller-th.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6837806182074114&lang=en)). Mass authorisation therefore belongs in every Target deal. Hero assets should still carry per-video Spark codes, because a creator can revoke account-wide authorisation at any time.

**Fees and benchmarks.**

- Rate cards disagree by 2–3× for the same tier. Tellscore puts micro at ฿5,000–10,000 per post ([The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40041471)); ConvertCake puts mid-tier at ฿25,000–80,000 ([ConvertCake](https://convertcake.com/blog/updated-thai-influencer-rate-card-guide/)). Rate cards are negotiation anchors; proven GMV should set the price.
- On activation, a posting rate below 30% of sampled creators signals a vetting problem ([ShortFormNation](https://www.shortformnation.com/blog/product-seeding-for-tiktok-shop-the-roi-of-free-samples-in-2026)).
- About 5% of creators typically drive most affiliate revenue ([Hamster Garage](https://www.hamstergarage.com/article/tiktok-shop-affiliate-case-study-glossary-benchmarks)).
- All of these benchmarks come from US vendors. There is no Thai primary data.

**Compliance is the binding constraint.** The B.E. 2567 cosmetic advertising criteria ban therapeutic claims ("รักษาสิว", "ลดการอักเสบ"), permanent-whitening claims, and doctor or expert certification. A reviewer who presents as a real user but exaggerates is jointly liable ([Amway TH summary](https://amway.co.th/articles/business-ethics-2025-nov); [Thai FDA](https://www.fda.moph.go.th/news/110968)). OCPB is moving to make influencers liable like product owners, and it signed an influencer-ethics MOU on 10 September 2026 ([Spacebar](https://spacebar.th/business/skcb-mou-influencer-smes-consumer-protection-2569)). Withholding tax on creator fees is 3% for services, or 2% for pure advertising placement ([Revenue Department](https://rd.go.th/25182.html)).

## Live commerce works when slots, offers and ad support line up

**What the vault shows.** Live was the only channel growing in mid-September (+9% month on month), but the 25 September close says it fell, so treat the month as flat to down. The vault's live data has a consistent pattern:

| Brand | What happened | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Moonshot Labs | Live ads returned **11.26 ROAS on 8.8** and 8.45 for September. Live ads were then cut from ฿12.2k to ฿0.8k and live sales fell from ฿103k to ฿61k. | Ad support was working; cutting it cost sales. |
| Linee | Live fell 30% week on week (฿317k → ฿221k) with live ads down only 9%. | Content or slot quality, not budget. |
| Spiffy | Live rose from ฿9.1k to ฿19.4k on **zero live ads**. | Cheapest growth line in the portfolio. |
| Linee 11:01 session | ฿77k at **฿45.6 per viewer**, three times other sessions, with peak concurrency of 17. | Daytime slots are under-tested. |

Sources: `[[...-Weekly-MKT-7-14Oct-Insight]]`; `[[...-10.10-Shopee-Review-Reconciliation]]`.

Exclusive Streaming Price was ฿0 on 10.10, an unused lever. There is **no Moonshot GMV-per-live-hour baseline**, and the live memory says to build one before scaling hours.

**Platform rules.** TikTok treats the same host streaming on two channels at once as a "re-run". Only Tier 6 sellers (about ฿10M per month in shop sales) can request an exception, and the penalty is a ban plus −2,000 points against a 200-point start. TikTok TH is replacing violation points with a Creator Health Rating, rolled out from January 2026 ([TikTok Seller University TH](https://seller-th.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=5997110245115664&lang=en)), and a seller Account Health Rating, live by July 2026. Under that system one non-compliant live claim damages distribution for the whole shop.

**Benchmarks are thin.** The only Southeast Asian live-share figure is about 33% of TikTok Shop GMV, from a secondary table attributed to Momentum Works and Tabcut and not verified ([Branvas](https://branvas.com/blogs/news/tiktok-shop-statistics)). For comparison, Momentum Works puts the US live share at 10–14% ([Momentum Works](https://momentum.asia/insights/detail/tiktok-shop-in-the-us-2025)). TikTok officially confirms only that interactions and watch time drive live ranking ([Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/)). Thai engagement peaks around 21:00–23:00 for beauty and 12:00–13:00 at lunch, but that comes from one vendor's posting dataset, not live sales ([Krevio](https://krevio.co/en/blog/tiktok-posting-time-thailand)). The vault records that TikTok TH's 9.9 concurrent-viewer record came at 20:00, not midnight.

**In-house hosts are far cheaper per hour.** Job ads put Thai in-house hosts at about ฿18,000–25,000 per month base plus commission, working 4–6 hours a day in 2–3 hour rounds ([JobThai](https://www.jobthai.com/th/job/1507591)). That works out to roughly ฿150 per live hour before commission. Freelance tiers run ฿500–8,000 per hour ([HypeLive](https://www.hypelive.io/blog/live-stream-host-rates-2026-hiring-guide)). An agency benchmark recorded in the vault, about ฿50k fixed for about 300 hours plus 1–2% commission, is roughly the same per hour as in-house. Agency value must therefore come from conversion skill, not cheaper hours, and that matters for the November agency proposal that has no owner or pricing yet. Contribution per live hour, after ads, vouchers and host cost, should rank slots and hosts. GMV per hour should not.

## Marketplace operations decides who is eligible for the next campaign

**Shopee Prime/Xtra.** The programme costs about 15% of GMV on the programmes, about 11% of Linee's net sales in July. About 60% of GMV arrives via Extra codes. Exiting is estimated at −20–30% of sales. An early-2026 exit made sales collapse, and the shop re-joined around February. The decision in force is to keep Prime for Linee through Q4, test exit on the non-Mall shop, and decide in Q1 (`[[10-Meetings/2026-08-27 MKP Cost Structure Review]]`). External fee trackers add a reason to re-check sooner: the August 2026 round may have narrowed Prime's commission discount ([khummai](https://khummai.com/fee-updates)). The Xtra programme fee is reported at 5.35–8.56% by category ([Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/660467)). Prime's net benefit should be recomputed before 11.11.

**Mall status.** Mall now carries a roughly 3-point commission premium on Shopee (19.26% vs 16.05%) ([Brand Inside](https://brandinside.asia/shopee-thailand-adjusts-seller-fees-up-to-19-26/)). The vault notes that Shopee holds Mall funds for 15 days, which costs "roughly two weeks of working capital". TikTok settles at about D+7 business days.

**Campaign mechanics are where operations failures cost the next campaign.** The vault's checklist covers 152 tasks plus a platform-rules tab:

- The published registration deadline is the last date, not the target; submit 2–4 weeks out. 9.9 got zero flash slots because "we were not in the queue".
- Campaign stock is deducted at approval, and an external stock-system sync during a promotion can delete Shopee variants.
- Three penalty points means a 28-day campaign ban. Points reset on the first Monday of each quarter, so the October reset falls between 10.10 and 11.11.
- Shopee caps late shipments at 5% for Mall and Preferred sellers on a rolling 7-day window ([BigSeller](https://www.bigseller.pro/blog/articleDetails/3197/shopee-seller-updates-may-2025.htm)). In the vault's words: "Mega-day ops failure doesn't cost this campaign, it disqualifies us from the next one."

Shopee TH's 11.11 2025 window ran 6–15 November ([Duoke](https://www.duoke.com/en/blog/article/182-2025-Sales-Guide-Conquer-Shopee-Lazada-TikTok-Shop-11-11-Mega-Sales-with-Winning-Strategies)), so 11.11 nominations are due now.

**Listing and inventory problems the vault has already found:**

- No 10.10 SKU carried a Shopee Ads tag, so the post-mortem took 30 exports.
- The platform rebate of about 31.8% of gross (฿177k in one day) "sits in no channel P&L".
- Loom had 786 Shopee visitors and ฿0 sales in two consecutive weeks: "fix it or delist it".
- Linee KIT visitors rose 60% while conversion fell from 5.4% to 1.3%.
- One toothpaste line shows about 10,000 units in the system against about 3,600 physical, a gap of roughly 7,000 still under audit.
- The Monchhichi pre-order must ship about ฿188k by 31 October.

**Two compliance exposures outrank all of this.**

- **Peroxide.** The ASEAN Cosmetic Directive caps hydrogen peroxide in consumer oral products at **0.1%**. Products between 0.1% and 6% are restricted to supply through dental practitioners ([ChemLinked](https://cosmetic.chemlinked.com/news/cosmetic-news/asean-amends-cosmetic-ingredient-annexes-to-acd-17-added-11-revised-and-1-deleted)). Thailand has proposed the same 0.1% limit; its in-force status is unconfirmed ([ChemLinked TH](https://cosmetic.chemlinked.com/news/cosmetic-news/thailand-proposes-to-update-cosmetic-ingredient-regulations-57-new-additions-1-revision)). Quick-Context records the Thai FDA requiring the exact peroxide percentage on pack, citing **Linee HP 1% and Monchhichi 3%**. This needs regulatory confirmation before 11.11 scale-up. Loom's 12% PAP is a different chemistry, which the vault marks as verified retail-legal.
- **Licence channels.** Licence Schedule C allows "speciality store for personal care + online social platforms". The vault itself flags that Shopee, Lazada and Tops may not qualify without written approval, and that the licensor treats "silence = deemed refusal" (`[[01-Projects/Monchhichi-Linee-Collab/README]]`).

The research also found no published rules on multiple accounts for any of the three platforms. That is a gap for a satellite-account strategy while the main TikTok account remains unexplained.

## Marketing and growth must judge spend at the margin

**The KPI rule.** Since October, Net Marketing Contribution (net revenue after trade spend, minus ads) is the main KPI after net revenue. The old "ROAS 5× target / 3× floor" rule is retired.

**Where the vault already shows the right thinking.** Moonshot's ad cut lost ฿150k of GMV to save ฿64.7k, a marginal return of about 2.3. That is above the 1.7–1.9 break-even ROAS, so the cut cost roughly ฿20k or more in profit. The team's rule, "add ads back while ROAS stays above 4", uses average ROAS; the right test is **marginal ROAS above about 1.9** (`[[...-Weekly-MKT-7-14Oct-Insight]]`). A rival model put marginal ROAS at 1.12 (n=12). The vault calls that model "a decision rule, not a fact", and stock decides between the two views.

**The vault also catches attribution illusions:**

- Monchhichi's CPAS (Meta-to-Shopee cart retargeting) claimed about 70% of GMV at a ROAS of about 18.6, which is likely over-attribution.
- Linee's GMV Max ROI improved while impressions fell from 297k to 56k: "starved, not broken". The hero clips are 4–7 months old.
- On 9.9, GMV Max ROAS of 4.01× hid a fall in first-order contribution per new buyer from ฿194 to ฿137.
- Fresh TikTok clips sold ฿0.9k on 10.10 against ฿14.0k on 7.7: "the content engine and the commerce engine are disconnected".

**External measurement practice agrees.** Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ contribution margin before marketing ([eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/roas-vs-contribution-margin)). Platform ROAS is an upper bound. It should be calibrated with lift tests ([TikTok Conversion Lift](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-conversion-lift-study)) and, once about two years of weekly data exist, an open-source media-mix model such as Meridian ([Google](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/meridian-marketing-mix-model-open-to-everyone/)). Acquisition MER (new-customer revenue ÷ spend) should be tracked apart from blended MER, because repeat-heavy Linee can hide weak acquisition behind returning customers. Moonshot's own 10.10 data shows exactly that.

**The Thai media market is flat.** The DAAT/Kantar revised 2026 forecast is +0.3%, to about THB 32.3bn. TikTok holds 24% and is growing 16%; Meta holds 25% and is falling 4% ([Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/digital-marketing-en/daat-kantar-thailand-digital-ad-spend-2026-revised-forecast/)). Skincare was Thailand's largest digital ad category in 2025 ([Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/economy/40054053)), so Spiffy competes in the most cluttered auction. Brand-growth evidence says penetration through mental and physical availability drives growth ([Red C](https://redcresearch.com/?p=4477)), and the 60/40 brand-to-activation split is a context-dependent default ([WARC](https://www.warc.com/content/article/event-reports/beyond_6040_putting_effectiveness_in_context/124156)). Nearly all Moonshot spend is activation, which is consistent with a new-buyer pool that keeps shrinking.

**Two strategic bets need the evidence weighed against them.**

- **Chinese consumers.** Chinese arrivals to Thailand fell about **30% in 2025** ([Skift](https://dlr.skift.com/2026/01/20/thai-tourism-is-20-below-its-2019-peak/)). The CN phase-one target of Chinese tourists at Tops should be small, search-led on Xiaohongshu, and measured by store sell-out.
- **Monchhichi.** The licensor's sales doubled to about US$31M on the Labubu-led nostalgia wave ([Parapolitika](https://www.parapolitika.gr/stories/article/1607467/xehaste-ta-labubu-ta-monchhichi-erhodai-gia-revans-ta-iaponika-kouklakia-pou-klevoun-ti-skini/)), but 2026 commentary calls the art-toy hype cooling ([nss magazine](https://www.nssmag.com/de/fashion/43899/wer-wird-labubus-2026-ersetzen)). Monchhichi should be treated as a time-limited drop asset with shallow inventory. The ฿13.93M break-even under the minimum guarantee and the 15-month term make this pressing.

## Conclusion

The research changes what "expert" means for this council. Generic marketplace expertise largely fails here. Published fee stacks run seven points low. US creator and live benchmarks do not transfer. Platforms publish uplift multiples, not baselines. The seat that wins arguments will be the one that brings Moonshot's own confirmed-basis numbers and marginal economics. The vault already holds the sharpest insights, which are better than anything in the public literature: a 48% Spiffy ceiling on Shopee, marginal ROAS of about 2.3 against break-even of about 1.9, a 3% TikTok commission below a 15% visibility floor, and new buyers halving across three campaigns. The council's failure mode is that these insights sit in reconciliation notes while live decisions still run on average ROAS, planning-convention fees and campaign-day GMV.

Two implications follow. First, the six seats are not independent. Weak creator commission, starved GMV Max content, a lost TikTok account, a strip offer cut from 7+7 to 7+1 and activation-only media all point at one outcome, a shrinking new-buyer pool, while Shopee concentration hides it. The council should treat new-to-brand buyers and contribution per new buyer as the shared scoreboard above any single seat's KPI. Second, the largest downside risks are not commercial. Peroxide levels, licence channel scope and claims enforcement under the new health ratings could each remove a hero product or a shop from sale, and they take far less effort to verify than any growth lever.
