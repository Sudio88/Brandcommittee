## 1. Market: live share of GMV in Thailand/SEA; Shopee Live, Lazada Live

### Takeaway
Live is structurally bigger in SEA than the US. The only SEA live-share figure found is about 33% of TikTok Shop GMV (2025, Momentum Works/Tabcut, via a secondary table). Thailand is one of TikTok Shop's two biggest SEA markets. No platform publishes a Thailand-specific split of live, short video and Shop tab, and Shopee and Lazada publish no Thai live-only GMV.

### Inferences
- For a Thai beauty/personal-care brand, live is likely a larger share of TikTok GMV than the 33% SEA average, because beauty leads Thai TikTok Shop and depends heavily on hosts. This is unverified. Use Moonshot's own Seller Center channel split as the benchmark.
- Shopee is still the bigger Thai marketplace, so Shopee Live matters for conversion of existing searchers. TikTok LIVE is where demand gets created. Treat the two as different jobs: Shopee for conversion and retention, TikTok for reach.

## 2. Live operations playbooks: run-of-show, product sequencing, traffic sources, algorithm

### Takeaway
The Chinese playbook has three parts: a 引流款 traffic product, then a 爆款 hero product, then a 利润款 profit product, run on a tight scripted loop (one practitioner describes a 5-minute hook, pain, solution, price, urgency, CTA cycle). Paid traffic is used to "prime" the room so organic recommendation follows. TikTok officially says only that LIVE ranking uses interactions, with watch time weighted heavily. Claims that it ranks on GMV or conversion are third-party and unverified.

### Cited Findings
- The product matrix is a cheap "hook" item sold at or below cost to flood the room and raise retention, then the core bestseller, then premium high-margin items once trust is built. Hosts follow a strict "5-minute loop" (hook, pain point, solution, price reveal, urgency, CTA) over 4-6h sessions. This is practitioner blog material, not an official standard — [Bingo Digital, Ren-Huo-Chang model](https://www.bingo-digi.com/blog-list/digital-marketing/22)
- Same source: paid traffic via Ocean Engine/Dou+ brings viewers in, and if they convert the algorithm releases a larger free "traffic flood". Its numbers are illustrative, not measured — [Bingo Digital](https://www.bingo-digi.com/blog-list/digital-marketing/22)
- Academic fieldwork on Douyin (2025) reports that beginners are advised to open their first sessions with low-price products, coupons and free samples — [Wang 2025, Big Data & Society](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/20539517241309883)
- Brand case (Charlotte Tilbury, Douyin): 1-2 lucky draws per stream, plus a 0.1 RMB fan-club join to comment and enter, used to drive engagement actions that the author believes lift traffic — [WalktheChat](https://walkthechat.com/douyin-live-streaming-how-brands-are-using-live-shopping-to-drive-direct-sales/)
- TikTok's official position (via Hootsuite summary): LIVE recommendations use user interactions (watched, liked, commented, gifted broadcasts, followed creators, time spent watching), content information and user information. Interaction signals, including watch time, are weighted more heavily. There is no public formula — [Hootsuite, TikTok algorithm 2026](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/)
- Third-party claim: LIVE distribution responds to product clicks, add-to-cart and purchases more than to comments, with a ">3% session conversion" target. The source cites no TikTok evidence — [LiveBuzz Studio](https://livebuzzstudio.com/tiktok-live-algorithm-commercial-velocity-framework/); contrasted with [tiktokstats.com](https://tiktokstats.com/articles/how-tiktok-live-algorithm-works), which emphasises session length, gifts, viewer turnover and FYP vs follower-notification entry
- LIVE GMV Max (agency summary of TikTok guidance): set the target ROI from historical live performance, and let campaigns run 5-7 days with more than 10 conversions before making major changes. Verify in Seller Center — [Ecomdy Media](https://ecomdymedia.com/blog/tiktok-shop-live-gmv-max-guide)
- McKinsey (2021, global/China): companies report live conversion "approaching 30%", up to 10x conventional e-commerce. China's live commerce market was about US$171B in 2020. Taobao Live did about US$7.5B in transactions in the first 30 minutes of 2020 Singles' Day presales. These are dated, not Thai, and the claims are unattributed — [McKinsey, "It's showtime!"](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/mckinsey-digital/our-insights/its-showtime-how-live-commerce-is-transforming-the-shopping-experience)

### Inferences
- Run-of-show framework for Moonshot, synthesised from the sources above:
  - (a) Open with a traffic SKU, such as a Spiffy single-sheet or mini pack or a live-only bundle, priced to make the room "stick".
  - (b) Move to the hero (core Spiffy or Linee).
  - (c) Pitch the profit items, such as Loom premium or Monchhichi collectibles, once concurrency and comments peak.
  - (d) Re-hook every few minutes for new arrivals, since live audiences turn over constantly.
- Watch time and interaction are the only officially confirmed ranking inputs, so retention mechanics carry the most evidence. These include comment prompts, timed flash drops and countdown vouchers. GMV-based ranking is plausible but not officially confirmed.
- Practitioners treat paid LIVE traffic (GMV Max) as a primer for organic recommendation. Judge contribution per hour after ad spend, not GMV per hour.
- Monchhichi's fandom suits event-style lives such as drops and limited editions, as a different format from always-on acne or oral-care selling.

### Gaps
- GAP: no TikTok Shop Academy TH or Shopee Seller Education Hub run-of-show template was retrieved verbatim.
- GAP: no verified benchmarks for Thai live conversion, average watch time or click-through rate in beauty or oral care. Aggregator figures conflict and lack methodology, so I have not used them.
- GAP: no official data on how much LIVE GMV Max traffic counts toward organic recommendation.

## 3. Slotting: hours, days, payday, double-days, session length

### Takeaway
Thai evening is the strongest window: 18:00-23:00 overall, with beauty skewing to 21:00-23:00, plus a lunch peak at 12:00-14:00. The evidence comes from vendor datasets, not platform data. I found no published evidence on payday (25th-5th) or double-day live uplift for Thailand.

### Cited Findings
- Analysis of 50 Thai TikTok channels found three windows: lunch (12:00-13:00), after work (18:00-20:00, the largest audience, "+35% engagement") and late evening (21:00-23:00). Beauty peaks at 21:00-23:00, fashion and tech at 18:00-21:00, and 14:00-17:00 is weak. This is one vendor's dataset of posting engagement, not live sales — [Krevio](https://krevio.co/en/blog/tiktok-posting-time-thailand)
- A Thailand TikTok Shop setup guide gives peaks of 12:00-14:00 and 19:00-23:00 and recommends sessions of at least 2 hours (agency guide) — [Sphere Agency](https://sphereagency.com/articles/tiktok-shop-setup-guide-thailand)
- Douyin practitioners run 4-6h continuous anchor sessions on scripted loops — [Bingo Digital](https://www.bingo-digi.com/blog-list/digital-marketing/22)
- Global non-Thai studies conflict. Sprout Social favours Tue-Thu 14:00-18:00, while Buffer favours 18:00-23:00 with Saturday strongest. Neither measures live sales — via [Gyre](https://gyre.pro/blog/the-best-time-to-going-live-on-tiktok-optimizing-tiktok-stream-schedule)
- Thai MC job ads describe 2-3h shift blocks within a 4-6h day — [JobThai](https://www.jobthai.com/th/job/1507591)

### Inferences
- With about 9h/day of scope and 6 MCs, a reasonable allocation is: put the strongest MC in 19:00-23:00, use lunch 12:00-14:00 as a secondary slot, and use daytime as a cheaper always-on and training slot. Test this against Moonshot's own GMV/hour by hour-of-day rather than assuming it.
- Session length: the evidence only supports a minimum of about 2h for the algorithm to ramp up, and rotating MCs in 2-3h blocks. I found no evidence on diminishing returns per hour.

### Gaps
- GAP: no published Thai data on payday-window (25th-5th) or double-day (9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12) live GMV uplift. Use Moonshot's historical data.
- GAP: no evidence comparing always-on and event live efficiency in Thailand.
- GAP: no session-length vs GMV/hour curve.

## 4. Host economics: in-house MC vs affiliate vs agency

### Takeaway
Thai in-house live MCs are typically advertised at about 12,000-30,000 THB/month base plus sales commission, with 18-25k being common. Part-time rates are around 1,000 THB per 3h live plus commission. Freelance and agency hosts are quoted from 500 to 8,000+ THB/hour by tier, from a vendor source. Commission rates and burnout data are not published.

### Cited Findings
- Live Streaming Host (MC), Ari area: base about 18,000-25,000 THB/month plus sales commission, 4-6h/day in 2-3h rounds (job ad) — [JobThai](https://www.jobthai.com/th/job/1507591)
- Cosmetics company MC, 3-month contract: 18,000-20,000 THB/month, 6 days/week (job ad) — [JobBKK](https://www.jobbkk.com/jobs/detail/35047/1300083)
- In-house studio MC: 12,000-30,000 THB/month, with order or sales wrap-up duties after each live (job ad) — [JobMyWay](https://www.jobmyway.com/jobdescription/12926)
- Part-time (Lat Phrao): about 1,000 THB per 3h live plus commission (job ad) — [JobTH](https://www.jobth.com/%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%99/0000994712UOXLK.html)
- Freelance host tiers (2026, from a vendor selling host services, so possibly biased upward): beginner 500-1,500 THB/h, mid 1,500-3,500 THB/h, pro 3,500-8,000 THB/h, celebrity up to 30,000+ THB/h — [HypeLive](https://www.hypelive.io/blog/live-stream-host-rates-2026-hiring-guide)
- Job listings for live roles in Bangkok, about 25+ open roles (June 2025 snapshot) — [Indeed TH](https://th.indeed.com/q-%E0%B9%84%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%9F%E0%B9%8C%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%94-l-%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B8%E0%B8%87%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%9E%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%A3-%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%99.html)

### Inferences
- Implied in-house cost per live hour, base pay only: 20k THB/month ÷ (about 5h/day × 26 days ≈ 130h) ≈ 150 THB/h, before commission, social security, studio and production staff. This is far below freelance tiers (500-8,000 THB/h). In-house wins on cost per hour. Freelance or celebrity hosts make sense only for event lives where incremental GMV covers the premium. This is an arithmetic illustration, not a benchmark.
- Contribution per hour = (live GMV/h × gross margin after platform fees and vouchers) − (MC cost/h + commission + live ad spend/h + promo cost/h + studio and ops cost/h). Use this, not GMV/h, to rank MCs and slots.
- KPI design: pay commission on contribution, or on GMV net of returns, rather than raw GMV. This keeps MCs from giving away margin through flash drops. Pair it with process KPIs that are confirmed algorithm inputs: average watch time, comment rate and product-click rate.

- GAP: no data on affiliate live-host commission structures specific to Thai beauty or oral care.

## 5. Platform rules 2025-2026: TikTok Shop TH and Shopee Live

### Takeaway
TikTok Shop Thailand is replacing violation points with health ratings: Creator Health Rating (CHR) from Jan 2026, and seller Account Health Rating (AHR) rolling out from mid-June and complete by July 2026. LIVE-specific enforcement includes action against LIVEs where products are not correctly categorised. Detailed Thai LIVE prohibited-content tables, and multi-account or dual-channel host rules, were not retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok Shop TH creators: Creator Health Rating replaces violation points, rolling out 12-19 Jan 2026. Accounts start at 200 points. Under the old system points reset after 90 days — [TikTok Shop TH Seller University, Violation Points & Milestones for Creators](https://seller-th.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=5997110245115664&lang=en)
- TikTok Shop TH sellers: Account Health Rating gradually replaces violation points from mid-June, fully by July 2026. LIVEs "without correctly categorising your product" may draw enforcement (page marked deprecated, last updated 25 Jun 2026) — [TikTok Shop TH Latest Policy Updates](https://seller-th.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=4532824293361409&lang=en)
- Legacy point scale (third-party, likely outdated): 1-48 points, with milestones at 12, 24, 36 and 48, and 48 meaning permanent suspension. LIVE penalties escalate from reduced visibility or disabled comments to suspensions of 24-48h and then 7-30 days. Unconfirmed by TikTok TH — [Shipfusion](https://www.shipfusion.com/blog/tiktok-shop-violation-points); [e-Cabilly](https://e-cabilly.com/blog/tiktok-shop-live-suspension-guide/)
- TikTok (US policy) prohibits gambling and game-like activities in LIVEs and videos. This matters for lucky-draw mechanics, which TH may treat similarly, though this is unverified — [TikTok Shop US Content Policy](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6837891779151617&lang=en)
- Thai press lists words to avoid saying in TikTok clips and lives (Khaosod) — [Khaosod](https://www.khaosod.co.th/sentangsedtee/howto-biz/learning/article_285829)

### Inferences
- Under health-rating systems, one bad LIVE (for example a medical claim on acne) can damage the whole shop's distribution. Live claim compliance is therefore a channel-level risk, not a per-video risk.
- Lucky draws and giveaways copied from Douyin need a Thai rules check before use. Treat them as restricted until TikTok TH policy confirms they are allowed.

- GAP: TikTok Shop TH rules on multiple accounts, or the same host streaming on two channels at once, were not found.
- GAP: TikTok Shop TH seller tier criteria were not retrieved.
- GAP: Shopee Live Thailand policies and penalties were not retrieved.

## 6. Compliance for spoken claims: cosmetics and oral care (Thai FDA)

### Takeaway
Thai FDA rules apply to anything said on a live stream. Cosmetic advertising needs no pre-approval licence, but it must not be false, exaggerated, misleading or therapeutic, under the 2024 (B.E. 2567) cosmetic advertising guidelines. "Treats acne" and "reduces inflammation" are therapeutic claims and off-limits for a cosmetic. Thai FDA gave guidance on live selling in Oct 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Thai FDA (Sept 2025): cosmetic ads need no advertising licence but must be fair to consumers. They must not be false or exaggerated, must not mislead on material facts, and must not claim to treat disease. Refer to the revised Cosmetic Advertising Manual B.E. 2567 (2024) — [Thai FDA news 110968](https://www.fda.moph.go.th/news/110968); [Matichon](https://www.matichon.co.th/local/quality-life/news_5364294)
- Examples under the 2024 criteria (secondary summary, check against the FDA manual):
  - No exaggerated results such as treating acne, melasma, freckles, dark spots or wrinkles, or permanent whitening.
  - No before/after images unless lab-verified.
  - No therapeutic terms such as "reduces inflammation" or "stimulates collagen production".
  - No doctor or expert endorsement.
  - No guaranteed results such as "100% results".
  - Source: [Amway TH business ethics, Nov 2025](https://amway.co.th/articles/business-ethics-2025-nov)
- Influencer and beauty-seller guidance on what can and cannot be said — [Prachachat](https://www.prachachat.net/hilight-prachachat/news-1672151); prohibited-word list (2026 update) — [TGC Thailand](https://tgcthailand.com/blog/prohibited-words-and-phrases-not-allowed-in-cosmetic-advertising-updated-2026/)
- Thai FDA issued guidance on precautions for live-selling products in Oct 2025, asking sellers to stay lawful and not deceive consumers (via search summary) — [Thai FDA](https://www.fda.moph.go.th/news/110968)

### Inferences
- The Spiffy Patch is an acne-patch cosmetic, so MC scripts must avoid "รักษาสิว" (treats acne), "ลดการอักเสบ" (reduces inflammation) and guaranteed-result language. Use cosmetic-permitted phrasing such as covering, protecting or absorbing, depending on registration class. If any SKU is registered as a medical device rather than a cosmetic, different rules apply. Confirm each SKU's registration class before writing scripts.
- For Linee oral care, toothpaste with fluoride and similar claims sits under cosmetic rules in Thailand. Avoid therapeutic gum-disease claims. Verify against the FDA manual.
- Operational control: keep a pre-approved claim sheet per SKU, a banned-phrase list in Thai, and spot-check live recordings. Under TikTok's health rating, each infringement also hurts distribution.

### Gaps
- GAP: penalty amounts under the Cosmetic Act B.E. 2558 and Consumer Protection Act for live-stream claims were not retrieved. Check the Royal Gazette or the FDA directly.
- GAP: no Thai enforcement cases specific to live streams for oral care or acne products were found.
