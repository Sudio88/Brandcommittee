**Ceilings**

Ceiling = past this discount depth, ads must beat the platform's healthy ROAS band. BEROAS is break-even ROAS at a 30% discount.

| Brand · platform | Margin | Ceiling | BEROAS@30% | Contribution zero |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Linee · Shopee | 57% | 71% | 1.90× | 85% |
| Linee · TikTok | 64% | 78% | 1.68× | 86% |
| MSL · Shopee | 59% | 77% | 1.80× | 88% |
| MSL · TikTok | 66% | 82% | 1.61× | 89% |
| Monchhichi · Shopee | 55% | 65% | 2.01× | 82% |
| Monchhichi · TikTok | 62% | 73% | 1.77× | 84% |
| Spiffy · Shopee | 49% | **48%** | 2.43× | 73% |
| Spiffy · TikTok | 56% | 60% | 2.09× | 76% |

- Healthy ROAS bands: Shopee 2.5-3.1×, TikTok 2.8-3.5×, Lazada 2.3-2.8×.
- A discount costs (1 − take) of its depth.
- A 50%-off Spiffy needs 3.23× on Shopee (above the ceiling) but 2.66× on TikTok, so it is a routing decision, not a veto.
- The 30% participate/skip floor never binds for us.
