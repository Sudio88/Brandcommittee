# KOL / Creator / Affiliate Strategy for Thai Consumer Brands (TikTok Shop + Shopee Affiliate, 2024-2026)

Research date: 2026-10-16. Method note: WebFetch could not resolve most target domains from this environment (DNS failures on seller-th.tiktok.com, momentum.asia, cube.asia, twfdigital.com, amway.co.th, digitalinasia.com). Every finding below comes from search-engine snippets of the cited URL, not from a full-page read. Treat figures as "reported by source X" and re-verify the official pages (TikTok Seller University TH, Shopee Seller Center TH, rd.go.th, ratchakitcha) before using them in decisions. Third-party blog figures are labelled as such.

## 1. Platform mechanics: TikTok Shop TH affiliate and Shopee Affiliate (AMS) TH

### Takeaway
TikTok Shop gives sellers two levers. Open Collaboration is broad discovery at a lower rate. Target Collaboration is invite-only, with a higher rate and sample settings, and a Target rate overrides the Open rate. Lowering a commission triggers a 30-day protection for existing creators, and since July 2025 GMV Max is the only Shop Ads campaign type for sales, pulling in affiliate videos that creators have authorised. Shopee AMS runs on a commission the seller sets per sale. The seller can add an "Extra Comm" on top, and orders count only when the link is clicked within 7 days and the order completes with no return.

### Cited Findings
**TikTok Shop: collaboration types**
- Thai Seller Center guidance: first-time sellers often use Open Collaboration to see which creators pick their products and how they perform, then move to Target Collaboration with creators they have built partnerships with. — [TikTok Shop Seller University TH, Affiliate Collaborations](https://seller-th.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6837804139136770&lang=en)
- Commission range (global TikTok Shop guidance): sellers can set 1%-80%. A Target rate overrides the Open rate on the same product, and a creator earns only one commission rate per product even when the product sits in several collaborations. Open Collaboration has a "Require Approval" mode. — [TikTok Seller University US, Setting Up Affiliate Collaborations](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6837873164896001&lang=en); [Makeinfluence](https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/tiktok-shop-open-collaboration-vs-target-collaboration-how-creators-get-matched)
- A Target invitation has a name, validity period, contact info, optional message, up to 100 products with per-product rates, and free-sample settings. One agency guide adds caps of about 50 creators per invitation, 1,000 invitations a day and 1,000 saved creators. These caps come from a single third-party guide and are not verified for TH. — [StarterX](https://starterx.co/blog/tiktok-shop-open-vs-target-collaboration/); [Huskee Digital](https://www.huskeedigital.com/guides/hire-tiktok-shop-affiliates-open-vs-target-collaboration)
- Typical rates (third-party benchmarks, mostly US, 2026): Open 10-15%, Target 18-25%+, VIP up to 50%. One blog reports a June (year implied 2026, US context) change that capped Open Collaboration at 10-15%. Neither figure is TikTok policy and neither is TH-specific. — [Hamster Garage](https://www.hamstergarage.com/article/tiktok-shop-creator-partnerships-guide); [Hyper-Star](https://www.hyper-star.org/blog/tiktok-shop-commission-rate-cuts/)
- Thai-language blogs put the affiliate fee sellers usually set at "ปกติ 5–15%". A fee calculator example uses "Affiliate 5%" for health and beauty. Both are low-authority sources. — [Sphere Agency TH](https://sphereagency.com/th/articles/tiktok-shop-setup-guide-thailand); [khummai.com](https://khummai.com/tiktok-shop-fees)
- Creators can see commission as a flat %, tiered by sales, or split between LIVE and video. — [Lemon8 creator guide](https://www.lemon8-app.com/@ideawin47/7571791279991603732?region=th)

**TikTok Shop: commission-change lock (confirms the existing doctrine)**
- Third-party sources agree: an increase applies immediately, while a decrease leaves existing creators on the old rate for 30 days. Creators who remove their content or delist the product lose that protection. Some sources say creators are notified 2 days before the decrease takes effect. — [Zorilla Marketing](https://www.zorillamarketing.com/articles/tiktok-shop-30-day-commission-lock-explained); [DAMI](https://www.tkdami.com/blog/7896/)
- Official TikTok page (US Seller University): decreases in Shop Ads commission now follow the same 30-day protection as standard affiliate commission. A clock icon marks the grace period in Open and Target Collab. — [TikTok Seller University US, How Standard Affiliate Commission Works](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6077860360177451&default_language=en)
- Practical implication flagged by a blog: a cut made just before a mega-sale does not take effect until 30 days later. — [Hyper-Star](https://www.hyper-star.org/blog/tiktok-shop-commission-rate-cuts/)

**TikTok Shop: ads x creators (Spark codes, mass authorisation, GMV Max)**
- Affiliate creatives are creator posts authorised for use in TikTok Shop Ads, and the creator still earns commission on the resulting sales. In GMV Max the advertiser can choose "Affiliate post" as a video source. — [TikTok Ads Help, About affiliate creatives for TikTok Shop Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-affiliate-creatives-for-tiktok-shop-ads?lang=en)
- Authorisation works two ways. A Spark Ads code covers one post, with a term the creator chooses. Affiliate Mass Authorization covers the creator's whole account, so all eligible affiliate videos become available to the seller. Revoking a code stops one ad, while revoking mass authorisation can pull a creator's entire supply at once. Authorisation grants ad use only: it does not transfer copyright, and a collaboration does not automatically grant ad rights. — [AdRate](https://www.adrate.io/en/blog/tiktok-shop-affiliate-seller-ads); [DAMI, Mass Authorization](https://www.tkdami.com/blog/8325/)
- Since July 2025, GMV Max has been the default and only supported campaign type for TikTok Shop Ads with a Sales objective (third-party report). The SEA TikTok Shop Ads Guidebook says GMV Max automatically fetches creatives that affiliates have mass-authorised. — [AdRate](https://www.adrate.io/en/blog/tiktok-gmv-max-vs-spark-ads); [SEA TikTok Shop Ads Guidebook (seller-th)](https://seller-th.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6837806182074114&lang=en)

**TikTok Shop TH: seller fee stack (affects CM2 and the affordable commission)**
- From 1 Apr 2024 (1 เม.ย. 2567), TikTok Shop TH moved from a flat 4% commission to rates that vary by product subcategory. — [Amarin TV](https://www.amarintv.com/spotlight/economy/63152); [Khaosod](https://www.khaosod.co.th/sentangsedtee/featured/article_274272)
- Reported 2026 fee stack (blog and calculator sources, effective 6 May 2026 / 6 พ.ค. 2569): non-mall commission about 6.42-9.63% incl. VAT, Mall 5.35-11.77%; Commerce Growth Fee about 8.03% for non-electronics, capped at ฿199 per item; Order/Transaction fee 3.21% incl. VAT; Infrastructure fee ฿1.07 per order. Total platform cost is reported at roughly 10-20% of sales. One calculator lists beauty and personal care at 6.42% commission, but that figure is about 283 days old. — [khummai.com](https://khummai.com/tiktok-shop-fees); [hypelive.io calculator](https://www.hypelive.io/tools/tiktok-fee-calculator); [FlowAccount](https://flowaccount.com/blog/commission-fees-ecommerce-shopee-lazada-tiktok/)

**Shopee Affiliate (AMS) TH**
- Shopee runs two programmes: "Affiliate Marketing Solution" and "Shopee Social Partners (Seller Affiliate)". AMS commission counts only orders that come through Seller Affiliate links and are delivered with no cancellation or return. — [Shopee Seller Education TH](https://seller.shopee.co.th/edu/article/14970)
- "สินค้าให้ค่าคอมพิเศษ (Extra Comm)" is extra commission that a shop pays on top, to affiliates who promote that shop. Shops offering it carry an Extra Comm logo on affiliate.shopee.co.th. — [Shopee Help TH](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/123900); [Shopee Affiliate Program](https://affiliate.shopee.co.th/)
- Extra Commission launched on 8 May 2023 (8 พ.ค. 2566). From then, a promoted shop pays the Shopee commission plus the Extra Comm, while a non-promoted shop pays only the base commission. — [ACCESSTRADE TH](https://accesstrade.in.th/shopee-new-commission/)
- Recommended seller rates by category (TWF Digital, Dec 2024): fashion 7-12%, FMCG/consumables 9-12%, electronics 4-8%, lifestyle/home 5-10%. Attribution goes to the last link click, with purchase required within 7 days. — [TWF Digital](https://www.twfdigital.com/blog/2024/12/shopee-kol-marketplace-ams/)
- A 2026 comparison blog puts the general Shopee affiliate range at about 1-20% by category and says Mega Sale campaigns can multiply commission 2-3x. This is not official. — [Southern Whale](https://southernwhale.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-thailand-2026/)
- Shopee's official rate table: "อัตราค่าตอบแทนของโปรแกรม Shopee Affiliate" (figures not retrieved). — [Shopee Help TH](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/123898)

### Inferences
- The 30-day lock means the opening commission is effectively a 30-day floor. Launch Target deals at the rate the brand can sustain, then use time-boxed Target boosts and raise rates (raises apply instantly) rather than launching high and cutting.
- With GMV Max now the only sales campaign type and pulling affiliate videos automatically, mass authorisation in practice belongs in every Target deal. It is the bridge between "creator GMV" and "ads GMV". Contracts should still require per-video Spark codes for hero assets, because a creator can switch mass authorisation off at any time.
- Fee stack plus affiliate commission: for a Linee single tube, TH platform fees of about 18-20% combined with 10-15% affiliate commission take roughly 30-35% of the price before COGS and shipping. This supports the doctrine that single-unit toothpaste cannot fund commission and should be sold in bundles.
- Shopee attributes on a last click within 7 days, while TikTok attributes on in-app content, so the same creator should get a separate Shopee AMS link and Extra Comm offer for cross-posting to Shopee Video and Facebook.

### Gaps
- Official TH-specific commission caps, invitation limits and sample-request rules (cap on free samples, sample-to-content deadline): the TikTok Seller University TH pages were not fetchable.
- Official TikTok TH affiliate tier names and thresholds: no source found.
- Shopee Video affiliate mechanics in TH (whether video GMV counts toward AMS, any separate video commission): no reliable source found.
- Official 2026 TikTok TH beauty-category commission: only blog and calculator figures were found, and they conflict.

## 2. Thai creator market: size, tiers, fees, benchmarks, affiliate share of GMV

### Takeaway
The Thai influencer market is usually put at about ฿45bn (2024), but definitions vary widely. Thailand is one of the fastest-growing ecommerce markets in SEA, and TikTok Shop SEA reached about US$45.6bn GMV in 2025. Per-post fee tiers from Tellscore and agencies give a THB baseline. I found no reliable Thailand-specific figure for the affiliate share of TikTok Shop GMV.

### Cited Findings
**Market size (definitions differ; always state which one)**
- Thai influencer market ฿45bn (4.5 หมื่นล้านบาท) in 2024, forecast to grow 15-20% a year over 2025-2029, per a Ministry of Commerce trade policy office (สนค.) related report. — [Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/economics/1234173); [Dailynews](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5866708/)
- The narrower KOL/influencer-marketing spend is above ฿10bn, up 25% in 2025 (IdeasLabs). — via [Dailynews](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5866708/) / [Positioning](https://positioningmag.com/insight/108534)
- Statista puts Thai influencer advertising spend at US$77.91m (about ฿2.67bn) in 2025. — [Marketeer Online](https://marketeeronline.co/archives/404758)
- The Thai Media Fund reportedly proposed a national platform covering about 9 million creators (Sept 2026). — [Chiang Rai Times](https://www.chiangraitimes.com/finance/thailand-tax-content-creators/)

**Ecommerce and TikTok Shop scale**
- Momentum Works: TikTok Shop SEA GMV was US$45.6bn in 2025, 71% of its global GMV of US$64.3bn. Thailand and Malaysia were the fastest-growing SEA ecommerce markets at +51.8% and +47.6% YoY (total ecommerce, 2025). Momentum's GMV counts paid orders, including ones later cancelled or returned. — [Momentum Works press release, Ecommerce in SEA 2026 (Apr 2026)](https://thelowdown.momentum.asia/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Embargoed-Press-release-Momentum-Works-Ecommerce-in-SEA-2026.pdf); [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/tiktok-shop-data-analysis/)
- TikTok Shop Thailand GMV estimates conflict: one analysis gives US$5.9bn for H1 2025, others give US$10-12bn for full-year 2025. — [DAMI](https://www.tkdami.com/blog/8892/); [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/tiktok-shop-strategy-southeast-asia/)
- TikTok Shop TH beauty (Marketing Oops, 18 Nov 2025): the #รีวิวบิวตี้ hashtag passed 8 million videos, more than 5 million users added products to cart, and purchase decision was 1.5x higher. The #TikTokShopThaiRisers campaign featured Korich, Dr. Pong, Fresh Me Thailand, Madame Fin and Kathy Cosmetic. — [Marketing Oops](https://www.marketingoops.com/reports/tiktok-shop-t-beauty/)

**Creator fee tiers (THB per post)**
- Tellscore (CEO Suvita Charanwong, about 2024): nano (1k-10k) ฿3,000-5,000; micro (10k-50k) ฿5,000-10,000; mid (50k-500k) ฿10,000-30,000; macro (500k-1M) ฿30,000-100,000. Note that these tier boundaries are non-standard. — [The Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40041471)
- Sphere Agency (2026): nano ฿1,000-5,000; micro ฿5,000-30,000; macro ฿30,000-150,000. — [Sphere Agency](https://sphereagency.com/articles/influencer-marketing-pricing-thailand)
- ConvertCake (2026 rate card): mid-tier ฿25,000-80,000; macro (500k-1M) ฿80,000-200,000. — [ConvertCake](https://convertcake.com/blog/updated-thai-influencer-rate-card-guide/)
- Bangkok lifestyle/tourism deals (2025): ฿7k-15k per IG Reel for 10k-30k followers; ฿45k+ for 200k-400k. — [Mayphoopan](https://mayphoopan.com/blog/thailand-tourism-lifestyle-influencer-marketing-rates-insights-2025)
- Thai KOL/influencer income can reach about ฿300,000 per campaign (headline figure). — [Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/business/business/1169120)

**Affiliate share of GMV / concentration**
- The only percentage found is for the US: affiliate creator content drives about 42% of US TikTok Shop GMV (Hamster Garage benchmark, 2026, not Momentum Works). Creators with 10k-100k followers reportedly drive 29% of platform affiliate GMV. — [Hamster Garage](https://www.hamstergarage.com/article/tiktok-shop-affiliate-statistics-benchmarks-roi)
- "Over 80% of SEA consumers bought via affiliate creator content" appears in secondary sources with no primary citation. Do not use it.

### Inferences
- The THB fee tiers set the fixed fee for hybrid deals. Under the doctrine, orders to break even = fee / contribution per order. Example: a ฿8,000 micro fee and ฿60 contribution per bundle order means 134 orders are needed before commission-only economics take over. Use this to cap fees by tier.
- Follower-based rate cards are only loosely tied to sales, and sources disagree by 2-3x for the same tier. Rate cards are therefore a negotiation anchor only. GPM and proven GMV (from Kalodata/FastMoss/TikTok Affiliate Center data) should set the price, as the doctrine says.

### Gaps
- Thailand-specific share of TikTok Shop GMV from affiliates vs the shop's own accounts/LIVE: not found in public Momentum Works or Cube Asia snippets (the full reports are paywalled or were not fetchable).
- CPM/CPV benchmarks for Thai TikTok and creator content: no reliable 2024-2026 TH source found.
- Hypeauditor Thailand and Ann Creator reports: not surfaced by searches.

## 3. Seeding / affiliate "army" programmes and the creator power law

### Takeaway
Public data on sample-to-post activation and GMV concentration is mostly from US vendor blogs. Typical benchmarks: 20-40% of sampled creators post, 10-20% of onboarded affiliates sell monthly, and about 5% of creators drive most affiliate revenue. I found no SEA/TH primary data.

### Cited Findings
- No-strings seeding posting rate is about 30-40%, and a sample post rate below 30% signals a vetting problem. — [ShortFormNation](https://www.shortformnation.com/blog/product-seeding-for-tiktok-shop-the-roi-of-free-samples-in-2026)
- Post rate rises with programme maturity: about 20% on first-round cold outreach, 40%+ with refined targeting, 62% with "white-glove" vetting (SeedingOps data via a GRIN-based piece). — [Archive.com](https://archive.com/blog/track-tiktok-shop-seeding-affiliate?p=1600)
- A healthy share of onboarded affiliates making at least 1 sale a month is 10-20%. — [Hamster Garage](https://www.hamstergarage.com/article/tiktok-shop-affiliate-statistics-benchmarks-roi)
- "5-10-85" heuristic: about 5% of creators generate the majority of affiliate revenue. If top-10 creator concentration exceeds 50%, the programme depends on too few creators. — [Hamster Garage case study](https://www.hamstergarage.com/article/tiktok-shop-affiliate-case-study-glossary-benchmarks); [SocialTale](https://socialtale.co/blog/tiktok-shop-affiliate-strategy)
- Seeding case: 700 samples produced 1,000 videos, about 1.4 videos per sample. — [ShortFormNation](https://www.shortformnation.com/blog/product-seeding-for-tiktok-shop-the-roi-of-free-samples-in-2026)

### Inferences
- Sample economics framework: cost per posting creator = (sample COGS + shipping) / post rate. At a 30% post rate, a ฿40 landed patch sample costs about ฿133 per posting creator. Then require GMV from the expected top 5% of creators to cover the whole seeding batch.
- Run the "army" as a funnel: open-collab, low-cost samples for discovery, promote the top decile by GPM to Target (higher rate, mass authorisation, hybrid fee), and cap concentration so no single creator exceeds roughly 25-30% of affiliate GMV. This last threshold is my judgment, not a sourced figure.

### Gaps
- No SEA/Thai primary data on activation rates or GMV concentration, and no fitted power-law exponent.
- No public case studies of how top Thai beauty brands (e.g. Srichand, Mizumi, Dr.Pong) run affiliate armies with numbers.

## 4. Thai legal and compliance

### Takeaway
The main risks are claims, not disclosure. Under Thai FDA (อย.) cosmetic advertising rules, creators and brands must not claim "รักษา", must not have doctors or experts endorse, and must not use unverified before/after imagery, and the reviewer can share liability. OCPB (สคบ.) is moving to treat influencers as advertisers, but I found no enacted rule that mandates "#โฆษณา". Withholding tax on creator fees is generally 3% (services, Art. 40(8) via ท.ป.4/2528 ข้อ 8), or 2% for pure advertising placement (ข้อ 10).

### Cited Findings
**Thai FDA (อย.): cosmetics claims and endorsements**
- The Cosmetic Act (พ.ร.บ.เครื่องสำอาง) sections 41 and 42 bar advertising that is unfair to consumers or harmful to society. — [iLaw](https://www.ilaw.or.th/articles/5298)
- A summary of "หลักเกณฑ์การโฆษณาเครื่องสำอาง พ.ศ. 2567" says the following are not allowed: therapeutic claims such as "รักษาสิว ฝ้า กระ", permanent whitening, and "ลดการอักเสบ"; citing a doctor, expert, agency or celebrity to certify the product (example of a banned phrase: "ยืนยันโดยแพทย์และผู้เชี่ยวชาญ"); and before/after images unless lab-verified. A reviewer who presents as a real user but gives exaggerated information is jointly liable with the advertiser. A personal experience with no guarantee of results is allowed. — [Amway TH summary, Nov 2025](https://amway.co.th/articles/business-ethics-2025-nov) (secondary; verify against the official announcement)
- Prohibited wording lists include "ฆ่าเชื้อแบคทีเรีย", "ป้องกันการเกิดสิว", "รักษาโรคผิวหนัง". — [Touch-One](https://www.touch-one.com/8911-2/); [Zortout](https://zortout.com/blog/ads-forbidden-keywords-cosmetics)
- Thai FDA (oryor.com) media: claims implying อย. certification and "ไร้สารเคมี ไร้สารพิษ" are illegal, as are claims of results better than laser, Botox or surgery. — [oryor.com](https://oryor.com/%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%A2/detail/media_printing/1742)
- A creator-facing summary lists 4 prohibitions for cosmetic advertising content. — [Marketthink](https://www.marketthink.co/60426)

**OCPB (สคบ.): influencer advertising**
- OCPB warned that boasting about sales volume (e.g. "sold out") can count as exaggerated or misleading advertising, and that online sellers or influencers with sales of ฿1.8m+ a year must obtain a direct-marketing licence (ตลาดแบบตรง). Reported Oct 2025. — [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/news/local/2889465); [Matichon](https://www.matichon.co.th/local/news_5414807); [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/641622)
- OCPB plans to amend the Consumer Protection Act (พ.ร.บ.คุ้มครองผู้บริโภค) to cover reviews by influencers and to make influencers liable like product owners. — [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/economy/669577)
- On 10 Sept 2026 (10 ก.ย. 2569), OCPB signed 2 MOUs, one focused on ethical influencer advertising. — [Spacebar](https://spacebar.th/business/skcb-mou-influencer-smes-consumer-protection-2569)
- The Consumer Council (สภาองค์กรของผู้บริโภค) asked OCPB to stop clips showing influencers eating household or cosmetic products. — [TCC](https://www.tcc.or.th/misleading-advertisement/)
- Analogy: Thailand's insurance regulator (คปภ.) requires paid insurance influencer content to be disclosed as advertising, pre-approved and paid a fixed fee. — [Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/finance/investment/1249840)
- Industry transparency guide on paid reviews. — [The Standard](https://thestandard.co/marketing-decoder-influencer/)

**Withholding tax (ภาษีหัก ณ ที่จ่าย)**
- Advertising fees: 2%, under ข้อ 10 of "คำสั่งกรมสรรพากร ที่ ท.ป.4/2528 เรื่องสั่งให้ผู้จ่ายเงินได้พึงประเมินตามมาตรา 40 แห่งประมวลรัษฎากร มีหน้าที่หักภาษีเงินได้ ณ ที่จ่าย ลงวันที่ 26 กันยายน พ.ศ. 2528". Advertising-specific guidance is in ป.6/2528, amended by ป.19/2530. — [rd.go.th](https://rd.go.th/3479.html); [rd.go.th ruling](https://rd.go.th/28765.html)
- Service fees: 3% under ข้อ 8 of ท.ป.4/2528. — [rd.go.th ruling](https://rd.go.th/25182.html)
- Practitioner guides say a company paying for reviews, clips or promotional posts withholds 3% (Art. 40(8)) whether the payee is an individual or a company, while pure ad placement is 2%. — [tax-ez.info](https://tax-ez.info/QA/View/Uf53TQZg); [Quasa](https://quasa.io/th/media/cchaanginfluuenechr-eriiykkhaaokhsnaahruuekhaabrikaar-phaasiitaangkan); [Thairath Money](https://www.thairath.co.th/money/personal_finance/financial_planning/2920789)
- A creator earning ฿1.8m+ a year must register for VAT. — [Thairath Money](https://www.thairath.co.th/money/personal_finance/financial_planning/2920789)
- The Revenue Department is stepping up enforcement on influencers and live sellers using platform data and its RD10X unit (Oct 2025). A separately reported proposal for 5% withholding on e-commerce payments is not confirmed as enacted. — [Expat Tax Thailand](https://www.expattaxthailand.com/influencers-online-sellers-digital-nomads-thailand-tightens-tax-net/); [Orbitax](https://wpqa.orbitax.com/taxhub/news/country/article/Thailand-Planning-Withholding--37791)

### Inferences
- For The Spiffy Patch: any "รักษาสิว / ลดการอักเสบ / ฆ่าเชื้อ" claim in creator scripts is a direct risk under อย. rules. If microneedle patches are notified as cosmetics, briefs must use cosmetic-safe language (e.g. ช่วยปกปิด/ดูแลผิวบริเวณสิว) and keep before/after footage out unless it is lab-backed. Dermatologist creators must not "certify" the product. The brand's physical-demo rule is compatible with this ("personal experience without guaranteeing results" is allowed).
- For Linee whitening toothpaste (toothpaste is regulated as a cosmetic in Thailand), avoid treatment claims for gums or teeth and avoid "ขาวถาวร" (permanent whitening).
- Disclosure: even without a confirmed OCPB "#โฆษณา" mandate, putting "#โฆษณา" / "#ได้รับการสนับสนุน" in contracts is a low-cost hedge given OCPB's direction (liability for influencers, Sept 2026 MOU).
- Tax operations: withhold 3% on creator fixed fees, both individuals and companies. TikTok and Shopee pay affiliate commission directly, so the brand does not withhold on platform commission. Off-platform cash bonuses paid by the brand do require withholding.

### Gaps
- Official text of the 2567 cosmetic advertising criteria (ratchakitcha / fda.moph.go.th): not fetched, and the verbatim clauses are unverified.
- Whether an OCPB regulation mandating "#โฆษณา" exists: not found. Status unconfirmed.
- The 5% withholding rate (e.g. for นักแสดงสาธารณะ, public performers): not confirmed for creators in the sources found.
- Microneedle patch regulatory classification (cosmetic vs medical device) in Thailand: not researched here and needs confirmation.
- PDPA (พ.ร.บ.คุ้มครองข้อมูลส่วนบุคคล พ.ศ. 2562) clauses for creator contracts: no specific source found.

## 5. Contract best practice

### Takeaway
Standard terms: paid usage and whitelisting windows of 30/60/90 days. A 6-month usage licence typically adds 20-30% to the fee, and perpetual rights add 50-100%. Category exclusivity runs 30-60 days around the campaign and costs 20-50% more. Include a kill fee and a takedown remedy. I found little public material on Thai-specific clauses or KPI clawbacks.

Use a kill fee and takedown remedy for breach, because otherwise the only recourse is a damages suit. — [JoinStatus](https://brands.joinstatus.com/influencer-marketing-agreements); [Pactlio](https://www.pactlio.com/blog/influencer-agreement-guide)
