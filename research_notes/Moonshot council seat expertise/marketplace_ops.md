Research date: 2026-10-16. 60-day freshness cutoff: 2026-08-17. Anything dated before that is marked **[RE-VERIFY IN SELLER CENTRE]**. In practice almost every fee and rule below needs that check.

**Method caveat for the report writer:** the egress proxy blocked direct page fetches (khummai.com, brandinside.asia, seller-th.tiktok.com, flashfulfillment.co.th all refused). ## 1. Fee stacks (Shopee TH, TikTok Shop TH, Lazada TH), with chronology 2022-2026

### Takeaway
All three platforms raised fees repeatedly in 2025-2026. After Shopee's 4 Aug 2026 round, the top Shopee commission is reported at 19.26% (Mall) and 16.05% (non-Mall), VAT-inclusive. Add a 3.21% transaction fee, a ฿1.07 per-order platform fee, and 5.35-8.56% for the PRIME/Xtra "ร้านโค้ดคุ้ม" service fee, and the stack is about 25-31% before ads and affiliate commission. That is consistent with Moonshot's measured ~33% total take. TikTok and Lazada also raised fees in mid-2026: TikTok adjusted commissions on 5 Jul 2026 and the growth fee on 6 May 2026; Lazada added about +1pp on 25 Jun 2026, with another round reported for 1 Aug 2026.

## 2. Mall vs non-Mall (Shopee Mall / LazMall / TikTok Shop Mall)

### Takeaway
Mall status now carries a clear commission premium on Shopee (about 3pp: 19.26% vs 16.05% top rates) and on Lazada (about 2.7pp: 18.73% vs 16.05% in general goods). It buys trust signals, exclusive campaign slots and stricter-but-protected service. No independent conversion-uplift data was found. TikTok Mall historically charged the same commission as normal shops.

### Cited Findings
- Shopee Mall vs non-Mall top commission after 4 Aug 2026: 19.26% vs 16.05% (incl. VAT). — [Brand Inside](https://brandinside.asia/shopee-thailand-adjusts-seller-fees-up-to-19-26/) **[RE-VERIFY]**
- Shopee Mall sellers face a stricter late-shipment cap: 5% vs 10% for regular sellers (May 2025, not confirmed TH-specific). — [BigSeller](https://www.bigseller.pro/blog/articleDetails/3197/shopee-seller-updates-may-2025.htm) **[RE-VERIFY]**
- Shopee Search Ads: manual keyword selection is reportedly limited to Mall sellers (consultant claim). — [Shopee ads guides via search summary](https://www.relevantaudience.com/ecommerce-marketing/shopee-ads-guide-2026/) **[unverified]**
- LazMall: limited to brand owners and authorised distributors. Requires a business licence and brand authorisation. — [atsell.io](https://atsell.io/blog/thailand-ecommerce-guide)
- LazMall commission premium: general goods 18.73% vs 16.05% Marketplace (incl. VAT). — [khummai](https://khummai.com/lazada-fees/general) **[RE-VERIFY]**
- TikTok Shop Mall TH: requires 100% authentic, branded (no OEM) goods, accurate brand tagging, shop-performance criteria, and rules on name, logo and images. Benefits include Shoptab, banner and Mall-entrance visibility, Brand Carnival / Brand Zone campaigns, and extra free-shipping coupons. Buyers get free shipping and 15-day returns. — [The Story Thailand](https://www.thestorythailand.com/en/tiktok-shop-mall/); [TikTok Shop Mall Program T&C TH](https://seller-th.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=1208437905065730&lang=en)
- In 2024 TikTok Shop Mall commission was 4-6%, the same as non-Mall. — [Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/2765068/tiktok-looks-to-shop-mall-for-growth). Since 5 Jul 2026 Mall and non-Mall have separate tables, which suggests the parity may have ended. — [TikTok Mall Commission page](https://seller-th.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7485510424282896) **[RE-VERIFY]**

### Inferences
- For The Spiffy Patch and Linee, the Shopee Mall premium of about 3pp of GMV has to be paid for by mega-campaign slots, the Mall-only voucher stack, trust (important for a regulated product like peroxide whitening) and manual keyword control. If those drive less than about 3pp of extra margin through conversion or traffic, a non-Mall shop on PRIME/Xtra may be cheaper.
- Monchhichi licensed goods arguably need Mall/LazMall/TikTok Mall for authenticity signalling. Brand authorisation from the licensor will be required.
- A hybrid is possible: keep the Mall flagship for the hero brand and run a non-Mall store for clearance and bundles. Multi-account rules apply (Section 4).

### Gaps
- Official Shopee Mall TH entry criteria, the Mall-exclusive campaign list and any Mall vs non-Mall conversion or CTR study were not found. Momentum Works and Cube Asia data were not reached.

## 3. Search/SEO and listing best practice

### Takeaway
Shopee search works as a two-pass system. Title keyword match is a hard filter, then results are ranked by recent sales velocity, conversion, CTR, listing completeness, shop quality and price. Thai-language keywords dominate Thai search. Ads feed organic rank only indirectly, through the sales and clicks they generate. Most sources here are agency blogs rather than official documentation.

### Cited Findings
- Two-pass model: listings must first contain the searched words, then are ordered by recent sales, conversion, completeness, shop quality and price. — [selleraihub](https://selleraihub.com/shopee-search-algorithm-ranking-factors-2026/)
- Seven factors in a 2026 guide: keyword relevance, sales velocity, conversion, CTR, shop performance, stock/fulfilment, freshness. The weights it gives are "rough" and unverified. — [selleraihub](https://selleraihub.com/shopee-search-algorithm-ranking-factors-2026/)
- Title front-loading advice conflicts across sources: first 30, 50 or 60 characters. — [zenweb](https://zenweb.my/services/seo/shopee-lazada-seo/); [hashmeta](https://hashmeta.com/blog/shopee-seo-ranking-higher-in-marketplace-search-results/); [izidigital](https://izidigitalmarketing.com/blog/shopee-seo-ranking-guide/)
- Thailand: most Shopee TH searches are in Thai. Use Thai keywords plus English brand terms. — [Sphere Agency](https://sphereagency.com/articles/shopee-seller-guide-thailand)
- Paid placements do not directly raise organic rank, but they generate the signals that do. — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/ecommerce-marketing/shopee-ads-guide-2026/)

### Inferences
- Thai title structure that follows from the above: [Brand] + [core Thai product noun people search, e.g. แผ่นแปะสิว / ยาสีฟัน ฟันขาว] + [key benefit/ingredient] + [size/pack] + [English brand/product term]. Avoid keyword stuffing and repeated words.
- For TikTok Shop, discovery is content-driven: product search plus video/LIVE attribution. The loss of the main account resets content history, so new and satellite accounts start with no sales-velocity signal.

### Gaps
- No official Shopee/Lazada/TikTok TH documentation on ranking factors was reachable. Lazada and TikTok Shop search ranking factors were not found.

## 4. Shop health, penalties, bans and multi-account rules

### Takeaway
Shopee TH uses a penalty-point system based on NFR, LSR (rolling 7 days) and seller-requested cancellations. Points accumulate per quarter, and benefits are suspended in 28-day blocks. TikTok TH is moving from violation points (12/24/36/48 milestones, 48 = permanent deactivation) to a 0-1,000 Account Health Rating in 2026. Nothing was found on multi-account rules on any platform, which matters for Moonshot's satellite-account plan.

### Cited Findings
**Shopee TH**
- Penalty metrics: non-fulfilment rate (NFR), late shipment rate (LSR, on the last 7 days of orders), seller asking buyer to cancel, and others. — [Shopee Seller Education TH, Penalty Point Course PDF](https://cdngarenanow-a.akamaihd.net/shopee/seller/seller_cms/9d23969bfb7bc3a93ebd853b76d4c3e0/EDH%20Seller%20Penalty%20Point%20Course.pdf); [Shopee TH policy page นโยบายคะแนนความประพฤติ](https://seller.shopee.co.th/edu/article/7052) **[RE-VERIFY]**

Privileges are suspended for 28 days and restored after 28 days if performance improves. — [MyCloud Fulfillment](https://www.mycloudfulfillment.com/blog/education/shopee-penalty); [Shopee TH 7052](https://seller.shopee.co.th/edu/article/7052) **[RE-VERIFY]**
- Appeals must be filed within the window shown in the notification, otherwise the points stand permanently. LSR and penalty points can be appealed in Seller Centre. ## 5. In-app ads (Shopee Ads, TikTok GMV Max, Lazada Sponsored Solutions)

### Takeaway
On TikTok Shop, GMV Max became the only Sales-objective format for new Shop ads from July 2025. LIVE GMV Max is available in Thailand. Shopee Ads is run on ROAS. The common practice is to set a break-even ROAS after all fees, start with auto keywords, then promote winning keywords to manual campaigns. Discovery ads are bid by placement. Nothing reliable was found on Lazada Sponsored Solutions.

## 7. Regulatory items on listings (Thai FDA, peroxide limits, marketplace enforcement)

### Takeaway
Under the ASEAN Cosmetic Directive Annex III, hydrogen peroxide in oral-care products is capped at **0.1%** (present or released) for products sold to the public. Concentrations above 0.1% and up to 6% are restricted to supply via dental practitioners. Thailand has proposed aligning with this. Thai cosmetics need a TFDA notification before production or import, valid 3 years. No source was found on how marketplaces enforce display of the notification number (เลขที่ใบรับจดแจ้ง).

### Cited Findings
- ACD Annex III, hydrogen peroxide: 0.1% for oral products. For >0.1% to ≤6%, supply only through a qualified dental practitioner, not directly to consumers. — [ChemLinked ACD update](https://cosmetic.chemlinked.com/news/cosmetic-news/asean-amends-cosmetic-ingredient-annexes-to-acd-17-added-11-revised-and-1-deleted); [ASEAN Annex III, release 5 Jun 2023 (PDF)](https://asean.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Annex-III_Release_5-Jun-2023.pdf)
- Thailand proposal lists oral products at 0.1% H2O2 (present or released). Status (proposed vs in force) was not confirmed. — [ChemLinked Thailand](https://cosmetic.chemlinked.com/news/cosmetic-news/thailand-proposes-to-update-cosmetic-ingredient-regulations-57-new-additions-1-revision)
- Consolidated ACD amendments from the 40th-42nd ACC meetings (Philippines FDA Circular 2026-0001), a sign of ongoing annex changes. — [FDA Philippines](https://www.fda.gov.ph/fda-circular-no-2026-0001-consolidated-amendments-to-the-asean-cosmetic-directive-acd-as-adopted-during-the-40th-to-42nd-asean-cosmetic-committee-acc-meeting-and-their-related-meetings/)
- Thai cosmetic notification is required before manufacture or import. Foreign firms need a Thai entity or agent. Validity is 3 years. — [CIRS](https://www.cirs-group.com/en/cosmetics/asean-cosmetics-registration-thailand-vietnam-philippines-malaysia-singapore-indonesia)
- The Thai e-notification system auto-screens restricted substances (2016 document). — [ITH Thailand](https://www.iththailand.net/public/uploads/conf2016/8-3_sumalee_tpp_sumalee_080859-2_yee.pdf) **[old]**

### Inferences
- Linee whitening with peroxide must be at or below 0.1% H2O2 (or equivalent released, e.g. from carbamide peroxide) for consumer sale in TH, SG and MY. Any listing claims or "whitening strength" messaging implying more is a takedown and regulatory risk. The same limit applies in the SG and MY routes because both follow the ACD.
- Putting the notification number in listing images or description is standard defensive practice. The marketplace-enforcement specifics are unverified (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No source was found on Shopee/Lazada/TikTok TH rules requiring the FDA notification number on cosmetic listings, or on their enforcement actions. The Thai 0.1% H2O2 oral-care limit's in-force status and date under the Thai Ministry of Public Health notification were not confirmed. The `th-fda-compliance` skill in the user's workspace may hold this.
